## Summary:

This paper introduces LPFQA, a novel benchmark designed to evaluate the capabilities of Large Language Models (LLMs) in handling long-tail, professional knowledge. The authors address a critical gap in existing LLM evaluation datasets, which often focus on general knowledge or simplified tasks, by constructing LPFQA from real-world professional forums across 20 diverse academic and industrial fields. The benchmark comprises 502 tasks that assess LLMs' expertise in specialized areas, incorporating fine-grained evaluation dimensions, a hierarchical difficulty structure, authentic scenario modeling, and interdisciplinary knowledge integration. The authors evaluate 12 mainstream LLMs on LPFQA, revealing significant performance disparities, particularly in specialized reasoning tasks, and highlighting the challenges LLMs face with long-tail knowledge. The construction process of LPFQA involves a fully automated pipeline, including data collection from professional forums, automated question generation and quality control using an LLM, and expert verification to ensure factual accuracy and relevance. The paper emphasizes the importance of evaluating LLMs in complex, real-world scenarios and provides a valuable resource for assessing and advancing LLM performance in professional domains. The authors also conduct ablation studies to analyze the impact of different evaluation dimensions on model performance. The results demonstrate that while some models show strong performance in certain domains, there are still significant challenges in achieving uniform cross-disciplinary generalization, particularly in handling long-tail knowledge. The paper concludes by emphasizing the need for more robust evaluation methods that can accurately assess LLMs' abilities in complex reasoning and specialized knowledge, and the potential of LPFQA to guide future research in this area. Overall, the paper makes a significant contribution by introducing a new benchmark that addresses a critical need in the field of LLM evaluation, and provides valuable insights into the capabilities and limitations of current LLMs in handling professional knowledge.
