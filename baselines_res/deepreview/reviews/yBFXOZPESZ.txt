## Summary:

This paper introduces ANO, a novel optimization algorithm designed to address the challenges posed by noisy and non-stationary environments, which are common in areas like reinforcement learning. The core innovation of ANO lies in its decoupling of the direction and magnitude of parameter updates. Unlike traditional optimizers like Adam, which intertwine these two aspects, ANO uses the sign of the momentum for directional guidance while employing the instantaneous gradient magnitude to determine the step size. This approach aims to enhance robustness against gradient noise, a significant issue in many real-world applications. The authors further propose ANOlog, a variant of ANO that expands the momentum window over time using a logarithmic schedule, effectively removing sensitivity to the momentum coefficient. Theoretically, the paper provides convergence guarantees for ANO, demonstrating a convergence rate comparable to other sign-based methods in non-convex settings. Empirically, the authors conduct extensive experiments across diverse domains, including computer vision, natural language processing, and deep reinforcement learning. The results indicate that ANO achieves substantial performance gains in noisy and non-stationary regimes, particularly in reinforcement learning tasks, while remaining competitive with existing optimizers on low-noise benchmarks. The paper's main contribution is the novel approach to optimization by decoupling direction and magnitude, which is well-motivated and addresses a key limitation in noisy environments. The introduction of ANOlog further enhances the practical applicability of the method by reducing sensitivity to the momentum hyperparameter. The theoretical analysis provides a solid foundation for the proposed approach, and the empirical results demonstrate its effectiveness in various domains. Overall, the paper presents a significant contribution to the field of optimization, offering a new perspective on handling noisy gradients and non-stationary landscapes. The method's ability to achieve state-of-the-art performance in several challenging tasks highlights its potential impact on the field. However, as I will discuss in the weaknesses section, there are areas where the paper could be strengthened to provide a more complete picture of ANO's capabilities and limitations.
