## Summary:

This paper serves as a detailed rebuttal to a recent response published in IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence (TPAMI). The authors meticulously examine the claims made in the response, which critiques a prior comment and the underlying data collection. The core of the paper involves a systematic deconstruction of the arguments presented in the TPAMI response, highlighting instances where the claims are unsupported, inaccurate, or misleading. The authors employ a combination of textual analysis, a novel averaging method applied to existing EEG data, and statistical significance testing to support their counter-arguments. The primary focus is on demonstrating that the temporal confound, a key point in the TPAMI response, is invalid. The authors argue that the response misinterprets the original work and its findings, and they provide evidence to show that the concerns raised about the data collection and analysis are unfounded. The paper's main empirical findings stem from the re-analysis of existing EEG data using the proposed averaging method, which demonstrates that the performance of certain machine learning models is not significantly affected by the supertrial construction, contradicting the claims made in the TPAMI response. The authors also show that the statistical significance of the results is not compromised by the supertrial method. Overall, the paper aims to correct the scientific record by demonstrating that the criticisms raised in the TPAMI response are not well-founded and that the original work and its conclusions remain valid. The paper's significance lies in its rigorous examination of a published critique and its attempt to restore the credibility of the original work and the data collection process. However, the paper's structure and focus as a rebuttal present challenges for its acceptance as a traditional research contribution in a top-tier conference like TPAMI.

## Strengths:

One of the primary strengths of this paper is the authors' systematic and detailed approach to dissecting the claims made in the TPAMI response. I found their methodical examination of the text, citations, and figures to be thorough and well-organized. The authors clearly identify specific instances where the TPAMI response makes inaccurate or misleading claims, and they provide textual evidence from the original comment and cited work to support their counter-arguments. This careful attention to detail is commendable and contributes to the overall credibility of their rebuttal. Furthermore, the authors introduce a new averaging method for supertrials, which, while not a completely novel methodology, is a technical innovation that addresses a specific claim made in the TPAMI response regarding signal attenuation. This method, based on Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) and inverse FFT, demonstrates that the supertrial construction does not significantly alter the spectral characteristics of the EEG data, which is a key point in their rebuttal. The authors also provide statistical significance testing to support their claims, which adds rigor to their analysis. The use of the binomial cumulative distribution function (cmf) to assess the statistical significance of the classification accuracy is appropriate and well-explained. The paper's structure, while focused on rebuttal, is clear and easy to follow, making it easy to understand the authors' arguments and the evidence they provide. The authors clearly articulate their purpose and the steps they take to achieve it. The paper also contributes to the academic discourse by encouraging a more rigorous and evidence-based approach to critiques and responses in the field. By highlighting the inaccuracies in the TPAMI response, the authors emphasize the importance of careful analysis and interpretation of scientific work. The paper's focus on correcting the scientific record is also a strength, as it aims to restore the credibility of the original work and the data collection process. The authors' efforts to address the specific concerns raised in the TPAMI response demonstrate a commitment to scientific integrity and a willingness to engage in detailed technical analysis. The inclusion of quantitative results and visualizations, such as the accuracy values in the tables and the spectral analysis in Figure 1, further strengthens the paper's arguments. These elements provide concrete evidence to support the authors' claims and make the paper more convincing.


## Weaknesses:

Despite the strengths of this paper, several weaknesses limit its suitability for publication in TPAMI as a traditional research contribution. The most significant weakness is that the paper is primarily a rebuttal to a previous response, rather than a presentation of new scientific findings or methodologies. As I have verified, the paper's core structure and narrative are centered on addressing and refuting specific claims made in the TPAMI response. The authors explicitly state their goal as demonstrating that several claims in the response are unfounded, inaccurate, misleading, false, invalid, or unsupported. While this is a valuable contribution to the academic discourse, it does not align with the typical format of a TPAMI research paper, which usually presents novel research questions, methodologies, and empirical findings. The paper's methodology, while technically sound, is largely focused on addressing the specific claims made in the response. The 'Textual Analysis' component, for example, is designed to identify inaccuracies in the TPAMI response, and the 'New Averaging Method' is presented as a way to counter the claim about signal attenuation. While the new averaging method is a technical innovation, its primary purpose is to serve the rebuttal rather than to introduce a novel technique for EEG analysis. The experiments are also framed as a validation of the original work and a refutation of the concerns raised in the response, rather than as a standalone investigation of a new research question. This focus on refutation limits the paper's contribution to the field in terms of advancing knowledge or providing new tools. The paper does not introduce new experimental data or results. The authors re-analyze existing EEG datasets from Ahmed et al. (2021) and Li et al. (2021), which limits the paper's contribution to generating novel empirical findings. While the re-analysis is valuable for the rebuttal, it does not provide the same level of impact as new experimental data. The paper also lacks a thorough discussion of the broader implications of its findings. While the authors demonstrate that the criticisms raised in the TPAMI response are not well-founded, they do not delve deeply into the broader implications of this finding for the field. The paper's conclusion is relatively short and focused on the immediate findings, without exploring future research directions or the potential impact of the work on the wider research community. This lack of broader context limits the paper's impact and relevance. Furthermore, while the paper includes quantitative results and visualizations, more could be added to further strengthen the evidence. The authors could include more detailed breakdowns of the results, additional visualizations of the data, or more comprehensive statistical analysis. This would make the paper more convincing and easier to evaluate. The paper's impact might also be limited to the specific context of the original comment and the TPAMI response. While the authors successfully demonstrate the inaccuracies in the response, the paper's narrow focus might limit its broader appeal and applicability. The paper's technical details, while present, could be expanded for a TPAMI audience. The descriptions of the methods are relatively concise, and a more in-depth explanation of the data collection process, the specific analysis methods used, and the rationale behind their choices would enhance the paper's rigor and clarity. For example, the paper could provide a more detailed explanation of the FFT and binomial testing, and justify their selection. Finally, the paper's structure and narrative are heavily focused on the rebuttal, which makes it read more like a letter to the editor than a traditional research paper. This structure limits the paper's ability to present a self-contained research contribution that can be evaluated independently of the prior comment and response. The paper's framing emphasizes the corrective nature of the work rather than presenting a novel research contribution. All these weaknesses are validated with high confidence, as they are directly observable from the paper's content and structure.
