## Summary:

This paper introduces a novel approach to addressing the challenge of misinformation propagation within Multi-Agent Systems (MASs) that utilize Large Language Models (LLMs). The authors present MISINFOTASK, a newly developed dataset designed to evaluate the robustness of MASs against misinformation threats. This dataset is a significant contribution, providing a benchmark for researchers to assess the impact of misinformation on the performance of MASs. The core of the paper lies in the proposal of ARGUS, a two-stage, training-free defense framework. ARGUS operates through two key phases: Adaptive Localization and Goal-aware Persuasive Rectification. The Adaptive Localization phase aims to identify critical communication channels within the MAS where misinformation is most likely to propagate. This is achieved by calculating edge betweenness centrality, which helps pinpoint influential communication paths. The Goal-aware Persuasive Rectification phase involves a corrective agent that intervenes in the information flow by providing persuasive, fact-based corrections to misinformation. This corrective agent utilizes a form of Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting to guide its analysis and intervention. The authors evaluate ARGUS across various misinformation injection attacks, including Prompt Injection, RAG Poisoning, and Tool Injection, demonstrating its effectiveness in reducing misinformation toxicity and improving task success rates. The empirical results show that ARGUS significantly enhances the resilience of MASs against these attacks. The paper's significance lies in its practical approach to mitigating misinformation in MASs, offering a framework that can be readily implemented without the need for extensive training. The introduction of the MISINFOTASK dataset further contributes to the field by providing a standardized benchmark for future research. Overall, the paper presents a valuable contribution to the field of multi-agent systems, addressing a critical security concern with a novel and empirically validated approach.
