## Summary:

This paper introduces PELICAN, an innovative adaptive tutoring framework designed to personalize education by integrating collaborative cognitive diagnosis with dynamic instructional adaptation. The framework operates in two distinct stages: first, it models a student's cognitive state through a collaborative diagnosis process, and second, it leverages this model to provide tailored tutoring support. The cognitive diagnosis phase employs a successor-first strategy, where the system generates diagnostic questions based on a hierarchical knowledge structure, ensuring accuracy through an expert-assistant-verifier pipeline. This stage aims to identify the student's current knowledge state by assessing their mastery of specific concepts. The adaptive tutoring stage then utilizes a slow-thinking algorithm, which simulates future dialogue paths to select the most effective teaching strategies based on the student's diagnosed cognitive state. This algorithm allows the system to dynamically adjust its instructional approach, providing feedback and guidance that is tailored to the individual student's needs. The authors validate their framework using the Gaokao dataset, a collection of high school exam questions, demonstrating improvements in critical thinking stimulation and task completion rates compared to several baseline models. The results indicate that PELICAN is effective in assessing knowledge levels, promoting active problem-solving, and enhancing learning outcomes. The framework's ability to adapt to individual student needs and provide personalized instruction represents a significant advancement in the field of AI-driven education. The paper's core contribution lies in its novel integration of cognitive diagnosis and adaptive tutoring, supported by a well-defined methodology and empirical validation. The use of a slow-thinking algorithm to select optimal teaching strategies based on simulated dialogue paths is a particularly innovative aspect of this work. The authors' approach to modeling student knowledge and dynamically adapting instruction based on this model offers a promising direction for future research in personalized learning.

This is a crucial step in creating a personalized learning experience, as it allows the system to tailor its instruction to the specific needs of each student.
