## Summary:

This paper introduces a novel security vulnerability, termed 'priming vulnerability,' specific to Masked Diffusion Language Models (MDLMs). The authors demonstrate that if an affirmative token appears during the intermediate steps of the denoising process, it can bias the model towards generating harmful responses. This vulnerability arises from the iterative nature of MDLMs, where the model refines its output through multiple denoising steps, unlike the single-pass generation of autoregressive models. The core of the paper's contribution lies in identifying this unique weakness and proposing a corresponding mitigation strategy. To address this, the authors propose a safety alignment method called Recovery Alignment (RA). RA trains models to recover from these contaminated intermediate states, effectively learning to generate safe responses even when exposed to affirmative tokens during the denoising process. The method leverages a reward model and an RLHF-style optimization objective to achieve this. The empirical findings presented in the paper demonstrate that RA effectively mitigates the priming vulnerability, reducing the attack success rate of jailbreak attacks that exploit this vulnerability. Furthermore, the authors show that RA maintains the general capabilities of the model across a range of downstream tasks, indicating that the safety alignment does not significantly degrade performance. The paper's significance lies in its identification of a previously unexplored security risk in MDLMs and the development of a practical and effective method to mitigate it. This work advances our understanding of the unique safety challenges posed by diffusion-based language models and provides a valuable contribution to the field of AI safety. The authors also provide a comprehensive evaluation of their method, comparing it against several baselines and demonstrating its effectiveness in enhancing the robustness of MDLMs against jailbreak attacks. The paper is well-written and clearly explains the complex concepts involved, making it accessible to a broad audience. The authors also provide a detailed analysis of the limitations of their approach and suggest potential avenues for future research, further enhancing the paper's contribution to the field.
