# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes to use generative models to do instance segmentation. The authors propose to finetune Stable Diffusion and MAE for category-agnostic instance segmentation using their instance coloring loss exclusively on a narrow set of object types. The authors show that the proposed method has strong zero-shot generalization ability.

## Strengths
- The paper is well-written and easy to follow.
- The authors show that generative models have strong zero-shot generalization ability for instance segmentation.

## Weaknesses
- The proposed method has limited application. The proposed instance coloring loss can only be applied to generative models.
- The proposed method underperforms SAM on COCO dataset.

## Questions
- What is the performance of the proposed method on COCO test set?
- What is the inference speed of the proposed method compared to SAM?
