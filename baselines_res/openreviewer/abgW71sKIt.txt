# Review

## Summary
The paper presents an innovative 1-bit post-training quantization (PTQ) method for large language models (LLMs) that enhances efficiency by minimizing activation error accumulation and preserving attention mechanisms. This approach consistently outperforms existing methods in empirical tests.

## Strengths
- The paper is well-written and easy to follow.
- The authors conduct a thorough analysis of the impact of layer output matching on block-level performance, the accumulation of quantization errors, and the effect of output matching on attention mechanisms.
- Based on their analysis, they propose a selective layer-wise output alignment strategy and an attention-aware masking mechanism called AMP to preserve attention behavior.
- The experiments demonstrate that the proposed method consistently improves performance over existing 1-bit PTQ techniques for LLMs.

## Weaknesses
- The paper lacks an analysis of the overhead, including the training time and memory consumption required for the proposed method.
- The authors only use the C4 dataset for calibration. It would be beneficial to verify the results using other datasets.

## Questions
- In the proposed method, do you still use the weight alignment methods mentioned in the paper, such as ARB-RC, to quantize the layers of the model? If so, what is the proportion of layers that are output-aligned to?
- What is the training overhead of the proposed method compared to other methods?
