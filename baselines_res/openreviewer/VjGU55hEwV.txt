# Review

## Summary
This paper introduces RLIE, a framework that combines LLMs with probabilistic rule learning to generate robust, interpretable rule sets for classification tasks. RLIE’s four stages—rule generation, logistic regression, iterative refinement, and evaluation—ensure effective rule generation and integration. Through experiments on real-world datasets, RLIE demonstrates superior performance compared to other LLM-based methods.

## Strengths
1. The paper is well-written and easy to follow.
2. The proposed method is technically sound.
3. The experimental results are comprehensive.

## Weaknesses
1. The novelty of this paper is limited. The proposed method appears to be a straightforward combination of traditional rule learning with LLMs, lacking significant innovative elements.
2. The experiments are insufficient. The authors compare only a few baseline methods, neglecting many state-of-the-art approaches in this field.
3. The motivation for the paper is unclear. The authors do not adequately explain the challenges of previous methods or the specific problems they aim to address.
