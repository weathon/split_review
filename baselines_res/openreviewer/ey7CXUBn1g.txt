# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes AdaSVD, an adaptive SVD-based LLM compression approach. Specifically, AdaSVD introduces adaComp, which adaptively compensates for SVD truncation errors by alternately updating the singular matrices U and V^T. Additionally, AdaSVD introduces adaCR, which adaptively assigns layer-specific compression ratios based on the relative importance of each layer. Extensive experiments across multiple LLM/VLM families demonstrate that AdaSVD consistently outperforms state-of-the-art (SOTA) SVD-based methods, achieving superior performance with significantly reduced memory requirements.

## Strengths
1. This paper proposes adaComp, an adaptive compensation technique designed to adjust the weights of U and V^T after SVD truncation.
2. This paper proposes adaCR, a method that assigns adaptive compression ratios to different layers based on their importance.
3. Extensive experiments on LLMs/VLMs demonstrate that the method, AdaSVD, significantly outperforms the previous SVD-based LLM compression method, SVD-LLM.

## Weaknesses
1. The paper lacks a comparison with other SOTA LLM compression methods, such as pruning and quantization.
2. The paper lacks experiments on larger models, such as 13B and 70B.
3. The paper lacks experiments on other downstream tasks, such as reasoning and coding.

## Questions
1. How does AdaSVD compare to other SOTA LLM compression methods, such as pruning and quantization?
2. How does AdaSVD perform on larger models, such as 13B and 70B?
3. How does AdaSVD perform on other downstream tasks, such as reasoning and coding?
