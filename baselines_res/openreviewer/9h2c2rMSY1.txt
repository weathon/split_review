# Review

## Summary
The authors study the application of conformal prediction to time-dependent PDEs. They prove that in a function space setting, distributions at arbitrarily close times can be mutually singular, making exact coverage guarantees impossible. As a solution, they facilitate weighted conformal prediction for a broad class of PDE problems arising from discretized models and validate these results in experiments.

## Strengths
1. The paper is well-written and easy to follow.

2. The authors provide a theoretical analysis of the problem.

3. The proposed method is validated via numerical experiments.

## Weaknesses
1. The contribution of this paper is limited. The authors simply apply the existing weighted conformal prediction method to time-dependent PDEs.

2. The authors only provide a theoretical analysis for linear PDEs. 

3. The authors do not compare the proposed method with the existing time-dependent conformal prediction methods, such as [1].

[1] Angelopoulos, Anastasios N., et al. "Cdf-based conformal prediction for time series." Advances in neural information processing systems 36 (2024).

## Questions
1. The authors only provide a theoretical analysis for linear PDEs. Is it possible to extend the analysis to nonlinear PDEs?

2. The authors should compare the proposed method with the existing time-dependent conformal prediction methods, such as [1].

3. The authors should add more detailed descriptions of the experiments, such as the choice of the neural operator model.

[1] Angelopoulos, Anastasios N., et al. "Cdf-based conformal prediction for time series." Advances in neural information processing systems 36 (2024).
