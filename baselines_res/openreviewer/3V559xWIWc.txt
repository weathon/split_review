# Review

## Summary
This paper introduces two techniques for speculative decoding. The first one is TALF, a tree-aware loss function that aligns the draft model’s output distribution over the entire expansion tree with that of the target LLM. The second one is SALF, a systematic approach for building the tree dynamically with a provable monotonicity guarantee, reducing unnecessary drafting computations during inference.

## Strengths
1. The proposed methods are novel. The observation of training-inference mismatch for tree-based SpD is interesting.
2. The paper is well-written and easy to follow.
3. The proposed methods bring consistent improvement over EAGLE and HASS.

## Weaknesses
1. The authors only evaluate the proposed methods on small LLMs (up to 8B). It is unclear whether the methods are still effective on larger models (e.g., 70B).
2. The authors only evaluate the proposed methods on Llama models. It is unclear whether the methods are still effective on other model families (e.g., Mistral, Phi, Gemma, etc.).
3. The authors only evaluate the proposed methods on greedy decoding. It is unclear whether the methods are still effective on sampling-based decoding (e.g., temperature > 0).
