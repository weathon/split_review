# Review

## Summary
The paper proposes CALM, a framework that combines prompt evolution with on-the-fly LLM adaptation for Automatic Heuristic Design. Unlike existing methods that rely solely on prompt engineering, CALM fine-tunes the LLM using reinforcement learning based on heuristic performance, enabling it to co-evolve with the search process. This approach outperforms state-of-the-art baselines across various optimization tasks, providing a more adaptive and effective solution discovery mechanism.

## Strengths
1. The idea of adapting the LLM itself is novel and provides a more adaptive solution discovery mechanism.
2. The paper is well-written and easy to follow.
3. The authors provide comprehensive experiment results to demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method.

## Weaknesses
1. The method is computationally intensive. The training cost for the LLM is not fully disclosed.
2. The method's generalizability to other LLMs is not explored.

## Questions
1. How does the computational cost of training the LLM compare to the inference cost of using API-based LLMs in other methods?
2. How well does CALM perform with other LLMs? Does the performance of the LLM affect the overall performance of CALM?
