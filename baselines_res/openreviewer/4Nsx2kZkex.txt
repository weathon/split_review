# Review

## Summary
The paper presents a framework for safe reinforcement learning in verifiable code synthesis. The framework integrates formal verification constraints into the policy optimization loop, aiming to improve both code generation and safety guarantees. The authors propose a differentiable verification layer that uses smoothing surrogate functions to allow gradient-based improvement. The framework is evaluated on benchmark tasks, showing improvements in verification success rate, functional correctness, and verification efficiency.

## Strengths
+ The paper proposes a novel approach by integrating differentiable verification directly into the RL policy optimization loop.
+ The use of smoothing surrogate functions allows for gradient-based improvement of both code generation and safety specifications, which is a unique contribution.
+ The empirical results demonstrate significant improvements over existing approaches, especially in complex scenarios requiring multiple verification objectives.

## Weaknesses
- The paper could provide more details on the feature functions used for differentiable verification, as their choice and effectiveness are crucial to the framework's success.
- While the empirical results are promising, the paper could benefit from a more thorough discussion of the theoretical foundations and potential limitations of the proposed approach.
- The paper could include a more comprehensive comparison with a broader range of baselines to further validate the effectiveness of the proposed framework.
- The writing is not clear, and the paper is not self-contained. For example, it does not explain the metrics used in the experiments, such as VSR, FC, and SQ.

## Questions
- Can you provide more details on the feature functions used for differentiable verification? How were they designed, and how do they impact the performance of the framework?
- What are the theoretical foundations of your approach? Are there any assumptions or limitations that should be aware of?
- Can you provide a more comprehensive comparison with other state-of-the-art methods? This would help to better position your work in the context of existing research.
- Can you provide more details on the metrics used in the experiments? How are they defined, and how do they relate to the performance of your framework?
