# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes a novel framework, named STBP, for continual spatio-temporal forecasting. STBP integrates a general spatio-temporal backbone with a scalable contextual pattern bank to handle dynamic spatio-temporal correlations and mitigate catastrophic forgetting. Extensive experiments demonstrate that STBP outperforms state-of-the-art baselines in both forecasting accuracy and scalability.

## Strengths
1. This paper studies an interesting research problem, i.e., continual spatio-temporal forecasting. This problem is highly practical in real-world applications.
2. The authors propose an effective framework to handle the research problem. The proposed framework can model dynamic spatial correlations and mitigate distribution shift. The proposed prompt-based guidance mechanism can support dynamic model adaptation and alleviate catastrophic forgetting.
3. The authors conduct extensive experiments to evaluate the effectiveness of the proposed method. The results on three datasets show that the proposed method can outperform state-of-the-art baselines in terms of forecasting accuracy, adaptability, and scalability.

## Weaknesses
1. The authors should give more details about the implementation of baselines, including model architecture and training strategy. It is not clear why the performance of iTransformer is much lower than other baselines on PEMS-Stream and CA-Stream.
2. The authors should give more details about the parameter expansion operation in the contextual pattern bank. For example, how to generate the new parameters for the current incremental period? 
3. The authors should give more analysis about the experimental results to help readers better understand the effectiveness of the proposed method. For example, why the performance of STRAP is notably poor on CA-Stream?
