# Review

## Summary
This paper presents a benchmark for evaluating text-to-image models on taxonomy image generation. The benchmark includes 3 datasets and 9 evaluation metrics. The authors evaluate 12 T2I models on this benchmark and provide some findings.

## Soundness
2

## Presentation
1

## Contribution
2

## Strengths
1. The proposed benchmark is open-sourced and includes 3 datasets with 9 evaluation metrics.
2. The authors evaluate 12 T2I models on this benchmark and provide some findings.

## Weaknesses
1. The presentation of this paper is poor. For example, Table 2 is not referenced in the main text. Figures 4 and 5 are not discussed in the main text. The main text should be self-contained.
2. The technical contribution is limited. The authors do not propose any new methods.
3. The evaluation of T2I models is not comprehensive. The authors do not evaluate the T2I models' capability of generating images for concepts at different levels of abstraction. Besides, the authors do not evaluate the T2I models' capability of generating images for a given synset and its hyponyms.
4. The authors do not provide any insights or guidance on how to improve T2I models based on the proposed benchmark and the evaluation results.
