# Review

## Summary
The paper introduces DIST, a novel plug-in method for diffusion models to improve the generation of 3D molecular structures. The authors identify a unique challenge in molecular data: their distributions are highly concentrated, with sharp, densely packed peaks representing valid molecular configurations. This dense-concentrated structure (DC-structure) makes diffusion modeling fragile in molecular contexts, as minor errors during the reverse process can lead to invalid or unstable molecular structures. The authors formalize the DC-structure concept and demonstrate how it affects the intermediate distributions during the diffusion process and how errors propagate through reverse inference. To address these challenges, DIST diffuses and steers the intermediate distribution back toward valid molecular peaks. The method is model-agnostic and can be integrated into various diffusion-based molecular generation models. Experimental results show that DIST improves the stability and overall performance of molecular generation while reducing computational costs by nearly half the standard number of timesteps.
