# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes a new dataset, MisInfoTask, to evaluate the robustness of MAS against misinformation injection. It also proposes a new framework, Argus, which is a two-stage training-free method that leverages goal-aware reasoning for precise misinformation rectification within information flows.

## Strengths
1. The paper is well-structured and easy to follow.
2. The proposed dataset is a valuable contribution to the community.
3. The experiments are comprehensive and well-executed.

## Weaknesses
1. The authors should provide more details about the dataset, such as the number of agents involved in each task and the number of turns in each task.
2. The paper lacks a comparison with existing misinformation datasets, such as Trustworthy Multi-Agent Systems (T-MAS) [1]. The authors should clearly explain the differences between their dataset and T-MAS.
3. The paper lacks an explanation of the performance metrics used to evaluate the MAS system. For example, the authors should provide more details about how the conclusion agent synthesizes the final result and how the explanation is generated.
4. The paper lacks a comparison with existing misinformation baselines, such as the methods mentioned in the related work section. The authors should clearly explain the differences between their method and these baselines.
5. The authors should provide more details about the threat model, such as the capabilities of the attacker and the defender.

[1] Trustworthy Multi-Agent Systems (T-MAS): A Benchmark for Evaluating Trustworthiness in Multi-Agent Systems

## Questions
1. What is the difference between misinformation and malicious information?
2. How does the proposed method handle the situation when there is no corrective agent available?
3. How does the proposed method handle the situation when there are multiple misinformation injections?
4. How does the proposed method handle the situation when the attacker is more powerful (e.g., controlling multiple agents)?
5. How does the proposed method handle the situation when the defender has limited resources (e.g., number of corrective agents)?
