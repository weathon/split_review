# Review

## Summary
This paper presents a method for fusing visual and tactile information for dexterous manipulation. It proposes a framework called Tactile Affordance in Robot Synesthesia (TARS) that uses robotic synesthesia through a unified point cloud representation. The framework integrates visual affordance techniques with tactile perception to handle tasks involving both contact and non-contact states. The approach is evaluated on four manipulation tasks and compared to existing methods.

## Strengths
The paper addresses an important problem in robotics, namely the integration of multiple modalities for manipulation.

## Weaknesses
The paper is not well-written and is difficult to follow. The main ideas are not clearly explained, and the technical details are hard to understand. The figures are also confusing and do not help in understanding the proposed method. The experimental evaluation is limited and does not provide a comprehensive comparison with existing methods. The results are not convincing and do not demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed approach. The paper lacks a clear motivation and contribution. The problem statement is not well-defined, and the novelty of the proposed method is not clearly explained. The related work section is also insufficient and does not provide a comprehensive overview of existing methods in the field.

## Questions
1. How does the proposed method compare to existing approaches in terms of performance and computational complexity?
2. What are the limitations of the proposed method, and how can they be addressed in future work?
