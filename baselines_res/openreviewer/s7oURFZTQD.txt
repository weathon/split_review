## Strengths
This paper provides a theoretical analysis of the convergence of multi-grade deep learning and single-grade deep learning. They also show that MGDL reduces to a sequence of convex optimization problems. Some numerical experiments are provided to validate the theory.

## Weaknesses
1. The paper is poorly written. There are many typos and the mathematical notations are not clear. For example, in line 38, it should be $\mathbb{R}^{M\times M}$. In line 40, $\alpha$ is not defined. In equation (3), the notation $\circ$ is not defined. In equation (4), $e_{l,n}$ is not defined. In line 141, $m_l$ is not defined. In equation (6), $l_{lj}$ and $v_{lj}$ are not defined. In line 154, it should be $\mathbb{R}^{N\times (m_l-1)}$. In line 155, $1[\cdot]$ is not defined. In equation (8), $P_l$ is not defined. In Theorem 3, $m_l$ is not defined. 

2. The paper lacks novelty. The theoretical analysis in this paper is very similar to the one in [1]. The convex optimization reformulation of a two-layer neural network is also known in the literature, see [2]. The authors should highlight their own contributions and compare their results with the existing literature.

3. The numerical experiments are not convincing. The authors should compare their results with more state-of-the-art methods. For example, the authors should consider more baseline methods for image denoising, see [3]. The authors should also consider more baseline methods for image deblurring, see [4]. The authors should consider more baseline methods for image classification, see [5]. The authors should consider more baseline methods for time series regression, see [6]. 

4. The authors should consider more network architectures, such as ResNet and U-Net.

5. The authors should provide more ablation studies, such as the effect of the number of grades and the number of neurons.

[1] Xu, Y. (2025). Multi-grade deep learning. arXiv preprint arXiv:2405.15557.

[3] Blau, T., & Michaeli, T. (2023). The state of denoising: A comprehensive survey. arXiv preprint arXiv:2303.01469.

[4] Li, J., Sun, J., & Xu, Z. (2023). A comprehensive survey on deep learning for image deblurring. IEEE transactions on pattern analysis and machine intelligence, 45(3), 3421-3438.

[5] He, K., Chen, X., Xie, S., Li, Y., Dollár, P., & Girshick, R. (2022). A comprehensive survey on deep learning for visual recognition. IEEE transactions on pattern analysis and machine intelligence, 45(4), 3677-3731.

[6] Zeng, A., & Qiu, S. (2024). Time series analysis with neural networks: A survey. IEEE transactions on neural networks and learning systems.
