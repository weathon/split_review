# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes a multi-head SimCLR-based framework for contrastive learning of EEG representations, leveraging independent CNN encoders and adaptive temperature parameters for each of the 5 frequency bands.

2

## Presentation

## Strengths
1. The paper is well-written and easy to follow.
2. The source code is provided.
3. The proposed method is simple and effective.

## Weaknesses
1. The technical novelty is limited. The proposed method is a simple application of SimCLR on EEG classification tasks.
2. The experiments are insufficient. The compared methods are too old, and more SOTA methods in 2024 should be compared. 
3. The proposed method is only validated on a single dataset. More datasets, such as ADNI+ (https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.18765), should be included.
4. The proposed method is only validated on a single task. More EEG tasks, such as EEG2IQ (https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-023-02151-9), should be included.
