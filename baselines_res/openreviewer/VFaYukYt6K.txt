# Review

## Summary
This paper presents an autoencoder-based approach to motion planning for autonomous driving. The method first encodes the driving trajectory into a latent space and then decodes it back into the trajectory domain. By adjusting the number of tokens used during decoding, the method can generate trajectories of varying lengths. The authors conducted experiments on the Waymo Open Motion Dataset to evaluate the performance of the proposed method.

## Strengths
1. The authors conducted extensive experiments to validate the performance of the proposed method.

2. The idea of performing motion planning in a latent space is interesting.

## Weaknesses
1. The presentation of this paper is poor. For example, the caption of Figure 1 does not provide any information about the figure. I suggest the authors revise the caption to better explain the content of the figure. Additionally, the authors should add references to the figures and tables in the main text, as I found some figures and tables that were not mentioned in the main text. Furthermore, the authors should add descriptions to the x-axis and y-axis of each figure to facilitate understanding.

2. The motivation for using an autoencoder to learn the latent space for motion planning is unclear. I suggest the authors provide more explanation on this point.

3. The authors should compare their method with more state-of-the-art baselines, such as [1] and [2].

4. The authors should provide a comprehensive literature review on motion planning for autonomous driving.

[1] Li, Sheng, et al. "Curb your enthusiasm: Safe and efficient planning for on-road self-driving." IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters 7.4 (2022): 10913-10920.

[2] Yang, Ziqi, et al. "Learning to plan for autonomous driving: A graph-based deep reinforcement learning approach." IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters 7.4 (2022): 10941-10948.

## Questions
1. Can the proposed method be applied to other robotics tasks, such as manipulation?

2. Can the proposed method be applied to multi-agent scenarios?
