# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes a novel method for combining intrinsic and extrinsic rewards in reinforcement learning (RL). The intrinsic reward is designed to align the predicted outcome distribution with a given preferred outcome distribution. The authors conduct experiments on a toy problem, MountainCar, and MiniGrid Doorkey 8x8 to demonstrate the effectiveness of their method.

## Strengths
The paper is well-written and easy to follow. The proposed method is straightforward and can be easily integrated into existing RL algorithms.

## Weaknesses
The experiments conducted are too simplistic. The toy problem and MountainCar are both discrete action problems, and it is well-known that RL algorithms perform poorly in such environments. Therefore, it is not convincing to demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method only in these environments. Furthermore, the performance in the MiniGrid Doorkey 8x8 environment is not compared with any baseline algorithms.

## Questions
- Why is the performance of the proposed method not compared with any baseline algorithms in the MiniGrid Doorkey 8x8 environment?
- The authors claim that the proposed method is cognitively inspired. Could they please elaborate on the cognitive aspects of the proposed method?
- How does the proposed method perform in more complex environments, such as continuous control tasks?
