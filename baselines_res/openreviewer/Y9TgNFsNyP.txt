# Review

## Summary
This paper introduces a new unlearning framework for FF models, which utilizes a goodness-guided approach. This method involves a stable guidance model that generates target goodness distributions, adjusting the original model to forget specific data by modifying its layer-wise goodness scores. Additionally, the authors propose a goodness-based membership inference attack (G-MIA) to evaluate the unlearning performance.

## Strengths
1. The paper is well-organized and easy to follow.
2. The authors provide a thorough analysis of the challenges associated with applying conventional unlearning methods to FF models.
3. The authors introduce a novel unlearning framework specifically for FF models, along with a new evaluation method.

## Weaknesses
1. The paper lacks a theoretical analysis of the effectiveness of the proposed unlearning method.
2. The paper does not provide a comprehensive comparison with other unlearning methods.
3. The authors do not thoroughly discuss the limitations of the proposed method.

## Questions
1. What are the theoretical advantages of the proposed method over existing unlearning methods designed for BP-based models?
2. How does the proposed method compare to other unlearning methods in terms of effectiveness and efficiency?
3. What are the potential limitations of the proposed method, and how might these impact its applicability in real-world scenarios?
