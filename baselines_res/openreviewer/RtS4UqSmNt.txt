# Review

## Summary
This paper studies the problem of controlled social learning with an information-mediating planner. The planner can choose the precision of the information received by the agents. The planner's goal could be to maximize the social welfare or to induce a specific action. The authors characterize the optimal policies for both altruistic and biased planners. They also run simulations with LLMs to verify their theoretical results.

## Strengths
1. The paper is very well-written and easy to follow.
2. The problem of controlled social learning is interesting and well-motivated.
3. The characterization of optimal policies for both altruistic and biased planners is novel and useful.
4. The LLM simulations are interesting and verify the theoretical results.

## Weaknesses
1. The framework only considers binary state and actions, which is limited.
2. The planner's action space is also limited, as it can only choose the precision of the information. It would be more interesting if the planner could also choose the message sent to the agents.

## Questions
1. How do you generalize the convexity of $V_A^*$ to more general settings? What are the main challenges?
2. Can the framework be extended to multiple states and actions?
