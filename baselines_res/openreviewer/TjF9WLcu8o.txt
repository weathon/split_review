# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes a framework, COM, for continual learning of CodeLLMs. COM combines contrastive learning and meta-learning to adapt to new tasks while preventing catastrophic forgetting. COM achieves state-of-the-art performance on three benchmarks.

## Strengths
- The paper is well-written and easy to follow.
- The proposed method is simple and clear.
- The experimental results are promising.

## Weaknesses
- The proposed method is a simple combination of existing techniques. The authors should provide more insights and analysis to show why this combination works.
- The authors should provide a more detailed analysis of the experimental results. For example, why does MIT perform better than ER? Why does COM perform better than other baselines on unseen tasks but not on seen tasks?
- The authors should conduct more comprehensive experiments. For example, the authors should compare the proposed method with more recent baselines, such as CodeAct [1] and Data-Efficient LLM Fine-Tuning for Code Generation [2]. The authors should also conduct experiments on more benchmarks, such as BigCodeBench [3] and MBPP [4].

[1] Lv, W., Xia, X., & Huang, S. J. (2024). CodeAct: Code Adaptive Compute-Efficient Tuning Framework for Code LLMs. arXiv preprint arXiv:2408.02193.

[2] Lv, W., Xia, X., & Huang, S. J. (2025). Data-Efficient LLM Fine-Tuning for Code Generation. arXiv preprint arXiv:2504.12687.

[3] https://github.com/bigcode-project/bigcode-evaluation-harness

[4] Austin, J., Odena, A., Nye, M., Bosma, M., Michalewski, H., Dohan, D.,... & Sutton, C. (2021). Program synthesis with large language models. arXiv preprint arXiv:2108.07732.
