# Review

## Summary
This paper introduces QUOKA, a training-free and hardware-agnostic sparse attention algorithm designed to accelerate transformer inference during the prefill stage. QUOKA improves efficiency by retaining a small subset of representative queries and subselecting the keys that align most closely with these queries. The authors demonstrate QUOKA's effectiveness through extensive experiments on various long-context benchmarks, showing significant reductions in latency and memory usage while maintaining near-baseline accuracy.

## Strengths
1. QUOKA is a training-free and hardware-agnostic sparse attention algorithm that accelerates transformer inference during the prefill stage. 

2. QUOKA improves efficiency by retaining a small subset of representative queries and subselecting the keys that align most closely with these queries. 

3. The authors demonstrate QUOKA's effectiveness through extensive experiments on various long-context benchmarks, showing significant reductions in latency and memory usage while maintaining near-baseline accuracy.

## Weaknesses
1. The performance of QUOKA depends on the chunk size for chunked prefill. The authors should provide a more detailed analysis of how different chunk sizes impact the effectiveness of QUOKA.

2. The authors should provide a more detailed analysis of how different sparse attention baselines impact the performance of QUOKA.

## Questions
1. How does QUOKA perform with different chunk sizes? Can the authors provide a more detailed analysis of how chunk size affects the effectiveness of QUOKA?

2. How does QUOKA compare to other sparse attention baselines? Can the authors provide a more detailed analysis of how QUOKA performs relative to other sparse attention methods?
