# Review

## Summary
The paper proposes a novel type of hyperbolic neural network based on the proper velocity model of hyperbolic geometry. This model is interesting as it is an unbounded version of hyperbolic geometry, similar to the Poincaré ball model but without the boundary. The paper derives a number of results related to the geometry of the model, and then adapts a number of standard neural network layers to this model. Finally, the paper presents a number of experiments showing the effectiveness of the proposed architecture.

## Strengths
* The paper is well-written and well-structured, with a clear introduction and motivation for the problem, a clear statement of the contributions, a clear description of the method, and a clear presentation of the experimental results.
* The paper presents a novel approach to hyperbolic neural networks, which is interesting and potentially useful.
* The paper provides a thorough and well-presented set of theoretical results, including the derivation of the fundamental operators of the PV model, as well as the adaptation of standard neural network layers to this model.
* The paper presents a thorough and well-presented set of experimental results, including a comparison of the PV model with other hyperbolic models on a number of tasks, as well as a comparison of the proposed PVNN with other hyperbolic neural networks on a number of tasks.

## Weaknesses
* The paper does not provide a clear explanation of why the PV model is better than the Poincaré ball model, which is also unbounded. The paper mentions that the PV model is "more general" and "offers more flexibility", but does not provide a clear explanation of what this means and why it is important.
* The paper does not provide a clear explanation of why the proposed PVNN is better than other hyperbolic neural networks, such as HNN and LNN. The paper mentions that PVNN "achieves superior performance on a number of tasks", but does not provide a clear explanation of why this is the case.
* The paper does not provide a clear explanation of the computational complexity of the proposed PVNN, or how it compares to other hyperbolic neural networks. The paper mentions that PVNN "can be implemented efficiently using standard neural network libraries", but does not provide a clear explanation of the implementation details or the computational complexity.
* The paper does not provide a clear explanation of how to choose the curvature parameter K for the PV model, or how to choose the architecture of the PVNN. The paper mentions that K "can be chosen based on the specific problem at hand", but does not provide a clear explanation of how to make these choices.
