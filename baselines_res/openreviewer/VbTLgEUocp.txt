# Review

## Summary
This paper introduces a method to hide meaningful text within seemingly coherent text of the same length using LLMs. The authors demonstrate the effectiveness of their approach, named Calgacus, using open-source LLMs and show that the hidden messages can be accurately recovered. They also discuss the implications of this technique on AI safety and the nature of knowledge in LLMs.

## Strengths
1. The paper is well-written and easy to follow.
2. The paper introduces a novel method for hiding text within seemingly coherent text of the same length using LLMs.
3. The authors provide a detailed explanation of the Calgacus method and its technical details.
4. The authors demonstrate the effectiveness of their approach using open-source LLMs and show that the hidden messages can be accurately recovered.

## Weaknesses
1. The paper lacks a thorough discussion of the potential applications and implications of the Calgacus method. While the authors mention some possible uses, they do not explore the broader impact of this technique in detail.
2. The paper does not provide a comprehensive comparison with existing methods for hiding information within text. It would be beneficial to discuss how Calgacus improves upon or differs from other techniques.
3. The authors could provide more insights into the limitations of the Calgacus method, such as the potential for the hidden message to be detected or the impact of the choice of LLM on the effectiveness of the approach.

## Questions
1. Can you provide more details on the potential applications of the Calgacus method?
2. How does the Calgacus method compare to other techniques for hiding information within text?
3. What are the limitations of the Calgacus method, and how might they impact its effectiveness in practice?
