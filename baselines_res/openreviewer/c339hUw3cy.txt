# Review

## Summary
The paper proposes a data curation pipeline for collecting high-quality CUDA kernel data and introduces the Concise CUDA Reasoning Dataset (ConCuR). Using this dataset, the authors fine-tune a model called KernelCoder, which achieves state-of-the-art performance on the KernelBench benchmark. The paper also introduces a method for dividing tasks by difficulty level based on reasoning length.

## Strengths
1. The paper is well-written and easy to follow.
2. The paper makes a valuable contribution to the field by addressing the challenge of data scarcity in kernel generation and introducing a high-quality dataset.
3. The paper demonstrates that SFT remains an effective method for enhancing the model’s ability to generate high-performance kernels.

## Weaknesses
1. The paper does not provide a detailed analysis of the reasons behind the performance improvements achieved by KernelCoder, which would strengthen the understanding of the model's effectiveness.
2. The paper does not include a comparison of the generated kernels' performance on real-world applications or benchmarks, which would provide a more comprehensive evaluation of the model's practical impact.
3. The paper does not provide a detailed analysis of the limitations of the proposed approach or potential areas for improvement.
