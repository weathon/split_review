# Review

## Summary
The paper presents a deep reinforcement learning architecture for DAG scheduling in heterogeneous environments. The proposed architecture is based on cross-attention layers and graph neural networks. The authors also propose a training methodology based on REINFORCE. The experimental evaluation is based on a set of synthetic and real benchmarks. The results show that the proposed approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods.

## Strengths
The paper addresses an important problem in the area of task scheduling. The proposed architecture is sound and the results show that it outperforms state-of-the-art methods.

## Weaknesses
- The proposed architecture is based on well-known techniques, hence the technical contribution is limited.

- The authors claim that the proposed approach is an end-to-end reinforcement learning framework. However, the training methodology is based on REINFORCE, which is a popular, but not end-to-end RL method.

- The authors claim that the proposed approach can rapidly generate schedules. However, the experimental results do not show the benefit of the proposed approach in this regard. It would be useful to compare the proposed approach with the state-of-the-art in terms of training time and inference time.
