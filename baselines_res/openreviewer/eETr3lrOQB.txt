# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes a novel VQ module, termed VQ-Transplant, which can be integrated into existing pre-trained VQ models without the need for retraining the entire model. The authors also introduce a new VQ algorithm, MMD VQ, within this framework. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness and versatility of the proposed approach.

## Strengths
1. The paper is well-written and easy to follow.
2. The proposed VQ-Transplant is a plug-and-play method that can be integrated into existing pre-trained VQ models, eliminating the need for retraining the entire model. This is particularly advantageous in scenarios where computational resources are limited.
3. The authors also introduce a new VQ algorithm, MMD VQ, within this framework, which has been proven to be effective.
4. The experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness and versatility of the proposed approach.

## Weaknesses
1. The authors only validated the proposed VQ-Transplant on VAR and LDM. It would be beneficial to demonstrate its effectiveness on a broader range of models to further establish its versatility.
2. The authors only conducted experiments on datasets such as ImageNet, FFHQ, and CelebA-HQ. It would be advantageous to include more diverse datasets in the experiments to further validate the generalizability of the proposed method.

## Questions
