# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes a general framework for the predictor-corrector method. The proposed framework, called Neural Predictor-Corrector (NPC), treats the choice of predictor and corrector strategies as a sequential decision-making process and employs reinforcement learning (RL) to adaptively learn effective policies. The authors evaluate NPC on four representative homotopy tasks: robust optimization via GNC, global optimization via GH, polynomial root-finding via HC, and sampling via ALD.

## Strengths
1. The proposed framework is general and can be applied to different homotopy tasks.
2. The proposed method is novel and interesting.
3. The experiments are thorough and demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method.

## Weaknesses
1. The authors only consider the simple RL baseline PPO. It would be interesting to see if more advanced RL methods can further improve the performance of NPC.
2. The authors only consider the simple neural network architecture MLP. It would be interesting to see if more advanced neural network architectures can further improve the performance of NPC.
3. The authors do not provide a theoretical analysis of the convergence properties of NPC.
4. The authors do not provide a sensitivity analysis of NPC to different hyperparameters.

## Questions
1. How does the choice of RL method affect the performance of NPC?
2. How does the choice of neural network architecture affect the performance of NPC?
3. What are the convergence properties of NPC?
4. How sensitive is NPC to different hyperparameters?
