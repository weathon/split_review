# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes ViF, a new vision backbone model. ViF is based on Fourier Neural Operator, and extends it to better capture high-frequency patterns. ViF is evaluated on image classification, object detection and semantic segmentation tasks.

## Soundness
3

## Presentation
3

## Contribution
2

## Strengths
1. The paper is well written and easy to follow. 
2. The proposed method is well motivated. 
3. The proposed method achieves good performance on image classification, object detection and semantic segmentation tasks.

## Weaknesses
1. The performance of ViF is not significantly better than previous methods. On ImageNet, ViF is only slightly better than VMamba and LocalVMamba. On COCO, ViF is only slightly better than VMamba under the 1x schedule. On ADE20K, the improvements over VMamba are also marginal. 

2. The efficiency comparison on ImageNet in Figure 1 is a bit misleading. The authors only compare throughput with VMamba, which is not very efficient. There are other more efficient Mamba-based methods, such as LocalVMamba and EfficientVMamba. 

3. The ablation study is not very comprehensive. The authors only conduct ablation study on the image classification task. It is unclear how the different components affect the performance on the other two tasks. 

4. It is unclear how much ViF can benefit from large-scale training. The authors only train ViF up to 96M parameters. It is unclear how the performance of ViF scales up with the model size, and whether it can achieve better performance than ViT with large-scale training.

## Questions
1. How does ViF compare with more efficient Mamba-based methods, such as LocalVMamba and EfficientVMamba?

2. How does ViF perform on the two dense prediction tasks under the multi-scale training? 

3. How does the proposed method perform with a larger model size?
