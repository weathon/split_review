## Summary

This paper proposes DIPOLE, a reinforcement learning algorithm for diffusion policies that replaces the unstable exponential weighting of standard KL-regularized RL with a dichotomous decomposition into positive (reward-maximizing) and negative (reward-minimizing) policies trained by bounded sigmoid weights. At inference, the optimal action is generated by a linear combination of the two score functions, providing principled and controllable greediness analogous to classifier-free guidance. The paper evaluates DIPOLE on offline and offline-to-online RL benchmarks (ExORL, OGBench) spanning 39 tasks, and scales it to fine-tune a 1-billion-parameter vision-language-action model for autonomous driving on NAVSIM.

## Strengths

- **Elegant theoretical derivation with a principled connection to CFG.** The greedified KL-regularized objective (Eq. 5) is cleverly constructed to yield a closed-form optimal policy (Theorem 1) that decomposes naturally into a ratio of positive and negative policies (Eq. 7–8), each trained with stable bounded sigmoid weights. The resulting score combination (Eq. 10) exactly mirrors classifier-free guidance, providing a theoretically grounded alternative to heuristic guidance methods like CFGRL — a genuine insight that bridges RL and diffusion guidance.

- **Strong empirical results on locomotion benchmarks.** On ExORL (Table 1), DIPOLE achieves the best performance on 8 of 9 tasks, often by a substantial margin (e.g., Walker-stand: 953 vs next-best 873 IFQL; Quadruped-run: 657 vs next-best 595 IFQL). On OGBench (Table 2), DIPOLE leads in 4 of 6 task categories and is competitive in the remaining 2. The offline-to-online results (Table 3) show consistent and often large gains (e.g., humanoidmaze-medium: 61→97 vs IFQL 56→82), validating the stability of the method during online fine-tuning.

- **Scalability to a billion-parameter real-world system.** Fine-tuning a 1B-parameter DP-VLA model on NAVSIM with DIPOLE improves PDMS from 88.3 (pretrained) to 94.8 on the navtest split, substantially outperforming the DPPO baseline (89.0). This demonstrates that the method scales stably to a large, real-world autonomous driving system, moving beyond typical RL benchmarks.

- **Clear presentation of the core idea.** The theoretical derivation (Section 3) is well-structured and easy to follow. The connection to CFG is clearly explained, and the limitations of the standard exp-weighted regression are concretely identified before the proposed solution is introduced.
