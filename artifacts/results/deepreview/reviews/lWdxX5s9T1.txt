# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes a framework, namely RADAR, to incorporate asymmetric distance information into neural VRP solvers. Specifically, the authors propose to use SVD for static asymmetry and Sinkhorn normalization for dynamic asymmetry. The authors evaluate RADAR on 17 synthetic and 3 real-world VRP variants and show that RADAR outperforms strong baselines on both in-distribution and out-of-distribution instances.
