# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes an influence-aware time series forecasting framework. It first points out the self-stimulation assumption in traditional time series forecasting and the error bound induced by this. Then, the paper proposes an influence-aware framework and designs a benchmark containing different types of influences. Finally, the paper proposes FIATS, a model for influence-aware time series forecasting, and conducts extensive experiments on the benchmark to demonstrate its effectiveness.

## Strengths
1. The paper is well-written and easy to follow.
2. The paper proposes a new benchmark for influence-aware time series forecasting, which can be useful for future research in this direction.
3. The paper proposes a model for influence-aware time series forecasting and conducts extensive experiments to demonstrate its effectiveness.

## Weaknesses
1. The paper lacks a discussion on the quality of the influence input. As the influence input is usually provided by human or LLM, it may contain error. How does the quality of the influence input affect the performance of the proposed framework?
2. The paper lacks a discussion on how to obtain the channel descriptions. Channel descriptions are an important input of the proposed framework. How to obtain them in different scenarios?
