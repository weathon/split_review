# Review

## Summary
This paper introduces MOCHA, a resource designed for developing and evaluating multi-sample spatial transcriptomics methods. MOCHA integrates molecular profiles, spatial coordinates, and high-resolution H&E images from multiple subjects, with each sample annotated by expert pathologists.

## Strengths
1. This paper integrates multiple spatial transcriptomics datasets, offering a comprehensive resource for research.
2. The datasets are annotated by expert pathologists, enhancing the quality of the annotations.

## Weaknesses
1. This paper primarily integrates existing datasets and does not present novel findings or methods, lacking significant academic contributions.
2. The paper's format is incorrect; it should adhere to the standard submission guidelines requiring a main text and supplementary materials in separate files.
3. The paper lacks a dedicated section for acknowledging and listing datasets used, instead mixing them within the references. It also fails to provide a data access statement, which is essential for research reproducibility.
4. The paper does not include a discussion or conclusions section, which are crucial for summarizing key findings and implications.
5. The paper contains several formatting errors, such as the misuse of the \paragraph{} command, which affects the overall readability and professionalism of the document.
