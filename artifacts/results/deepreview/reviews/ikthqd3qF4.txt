# Review

## Summary
This paper presents a clustering-based algorithm to detect cross-applicants in large anonymized datasets, such as loan-level mortgage data. The approach introduces a new evaluation method that enables a researcher to optimize the trade-off between precision and sample size (or recall) without the need for labeled training data.
