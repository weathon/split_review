# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes a method for learning POMDPs. The method is based on estimating the PSR of the POMDP and then recovering the observation and transition matrices from the PSR. The paper proposes a method to recover these matrices up to a partition of the state space.

## Strengths
The paper is well-written and easy to follow. The method is novel and the theoretical results are interesting.

## Weaknesses
The main weakness of the paper is the lack of experiments. The method is only evaluated on a few small POMDPs and it is hard to get a sense of how well the method works in general. It would be useful to see how the method performs on larger POMDPs and how it compares to other methods for learning POMDPs.

## Questions
- How does the method perform on larger POMDPs?
- How does the method compare to other methods for learning POMDPs?
- How sensitive is the method to the assumptions made in Section 3.3?
