# Review

## Summary
This paper presents a shape-adaptive guidance signal for interactive cortical sulcal labeling using spherical convolutional neural networks, which preserves structural information without sacrificing anatomical details. The approach solves the eikonal equation with a speed function that incorporates the mean curvature of the cortical surface, allowing for fine-grained anatomical detail capture. Experimental results demonstrate that a single click using this encoding scheme outperforms fully automatic methods and equidistance schemes, achieving efficiency and improved labeling accuracy.

## Strengths
1. The proposed shape-adaptive guidance signal for interactive cortical sulcal labeling is novel.
2. The paper is well-written and easy to follow.
3. The experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method.

## Weaknesses
1. The proposed method was validated on a limited dataset, and its effectiveness has not been verified on a larger dataset.
2. The proposed method was only compared to fully automatic methods and equidistance schemes, without being compared to other interactive methods.
3. The proposed method requires manual adjustment of hyperparameters, which reduces its usability.

## Questions
1. How does the proposed method perform on a larger dataset?
2. How does the proposed method compare to other interactive methods?
3. Can the hyperparameters be automatically adjusted?
