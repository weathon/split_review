# Review

## Summary
The paper proposes MoEP (Modular Expert Paths), a method to introduce sparsity in language models without increasing the total parameter count. MoEP combines model parallelism with Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) techniques to enable selective token activation, which accelerates learning and improves performance compared to the GPT-2 baseline.

## Strengths
- The paper presents a new approach to adding sparsity in language models without increasing the total parameter count.

## Weaknesses
- The paper is poorly written. It is hard to follow the methodology section and there are many typos in the manuscript.
- The paper lacks a proper baseline comparison. The authors only compare their method to GPT-2, which is a very outdated model. The authors should compare their method to more recent and stronger baselines.
- The paper lacks a proper ablation study. The authors should investigate the impact of different components of their method on the model's performance.

## Questions
- Can the authors provide a more detailed explanation of the MoEP method, including how it combines model parallelism with MoE-style projections?
- Can the authors provide a comparison of MoEP with more recent and stronger baselines than GPT-2?
- Can the authors provide an ablation study to investigate the impact of different components of MoEP on the model's performance?
