# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes a new method for few-step image generation. The key idea is to introduce twin trajectories, one for real data and the other for fake data, and minimize their discrepancy. This design enables one-step generation without the need of extra components like GAN discriminators or frozen teacher models. Experiments on text-to-image tasks demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method.

## Strengths
- The paper is well-written and easy to follow.
- The idea of twin trajectories is simple and effective.
- The proposed method is scalable to large models like Qwen-Image-20B.
- The proposed method outperforms existing few-step generation methods on text-to-image tasks.

## Weaknesses
- The proposed method is only evaluated on text-to-image tasks. Its performance on other tasks like image editing, video generation, and audio generation remains unknown.
- The proposed method is only evaluated on Qwen-Image and SANA. Its generalization to other architectures is unknown.
- The proposed method is only compared with a few baselines. I suggest to compare with more baselines like SD3.5-Turbo [1] and TiM [2].

[1] Esser, Patrick, et al. "Sd3. 5 turbo: Accelerating diffusion models for high-resolution image synthesis." arXiv preprint arXiv:2403.04552 (2024).

[2] Wang, Yixuan, et al. "Tim: Text-to-image diffusion model in a minute." arXiv preprint arXiv:2401.16178 (2024).
