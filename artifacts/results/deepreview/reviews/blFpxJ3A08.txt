# Review

## Summary
The paper introduces LPFQA, a benchmark designed to evaluate LLMs using real-world professional forum data across 20 fields. LPFQA aims to address limitations in existing benchmarks by focusing on long-tail knowledge and real-world complexity. It features fine-grained evaluation dimensions, a hierarchical difficulty structure, and authentic, interdisciplinary scenarios. The authors evaluate 12 LLMs on LPFQA, revealing significant performance disparities, especially in specialized reasoning tasks.

## Strengths
- The paper is well-organized and easy to follow.
- The proposed benchmark covers a wide range of domains and includes questions spanning different difficulty levels.
- The paper evaluates 12 mainstream LLMs on the proposed benchmark, providing a comprehensive comparison of their performance.

## Weaknesses
- The proposed benchmark is relatively small, with only 505 questions.
- The paper lacks details on the number of expert verifiers and the time spent on expert verification. It also does not specify the number of LLMs used for difficulty adjustment or the time required for this process.
- The paper does not provide detailed information about the LLM used for automated question generation, such as its model name or size.
- The paper does not discuss the costs associated with building the benchmark, such as server costs, the number of LLM API calls required, or the time needed for LLM API calls.

## Questions
- How many expert verifiers were involved in the process, and what is the time spent on expert verification?
- How many LLMs were used for difficulty adjustment, and what is the time required for this process?
- What is the LLM used for automated question generation?
- What are the costs associated with building the benchmark?
