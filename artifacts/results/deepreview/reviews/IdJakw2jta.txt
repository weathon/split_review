# Review

## Summary
The paper introduces a novel framework for Long-Form Spatio-Temporal Video Grounding (LF-STVG), which aims to locate target events in long videos using textual queries. The authors propose the AutoRegressive Transformer framework for LF-STVG (ART-STVG), which processes video frames sequentially rather than all at once. This approach addresses the challenges of handling long videos and irrelevant information by focusing on relevant spatio-temporal contexts. The framework includes spatial and temporal memory banks with selective memory strategies to improve performance. Additionally, a cascaded spatio-temporal design connects spatial and temporal decoders, enhancing the localization process. The ART-STVG outperforms existing methods on newly extended datasets for LF-STVG and shows competitive results on conventional Short-Form STVG.

## Strengths
1. The paper introduces a novel approach to handling long videos by processing them sequentially, which is a significant departure from existing methods that process all frames simultaneously.
2. The proposed ART-STVG framework shows superior performance on newly extended datasets for LF-STVG, demonstrating its effectiveness in handling long videos.
3. The paper provides a thorough explanation of the proposed framework, including the spatial and temporal memory banks and the selective memory strategies, which enhances the understanding of the approach.

## Weaknesses
1. The paper does not provide a clear explanation of the memory selection strategies, making it difficult to understand how relevant information is selected for the decoders.
2. The paper lacks a detailed analysis of the computational efficiency of the proposed approach, which is an important consideration for practical applications.
3. The paper does not provide a comprehensive comparison of the proposed approach with existing methods in terms of model complexity and inference time.

## Questions
1. Can the authors provide a more detailed explanation of the memory selection strategies used in the proposed framework? Specifically, how are the most relevant spatio-temporal contexts selected for the decoders?
2. How does the proposed ART-STVG framework compare to existing methods in terms of computational efficiency? Can the authors provide details on the training and inference times, as well as the memory requirements of their approach?
3. The paper mentions that the spatial and temporal decoders are connected in a cascaded design. Can the authors elaborate on how this design enhances the performance of the framework? Specifically, how does the fine-grained target information from the spatial decoder assist with temporal localization?
