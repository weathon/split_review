# Review

## Summary
The paper introduces a lightweight denoising autoencoder (DAE) plug-in designed to enhance the generalization of single-image super-resolution (SISR) models. It uses low-resolution (LR) images prediction-based cyclic regularization and leverages the properties of diffusion models to align high-resolution (HR) and LR features after noise is added. The LDP can be applied during training as a loss function or during inference as a post-processing step to correct artifacts.
