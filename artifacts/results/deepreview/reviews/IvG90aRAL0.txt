# Review

## Summary
This paper studies projection-free sparse convex optimization problems. For the vector domain, the proposed quantum Frank-Wolfe algorithm improves the query complexity by a factor of $\sqrt{d}$ for sparse constraints compared to the best classical algorithm. For the matrix domain, the proposed quantum Frank-Wolfe algorithm improves the time complexity by a factor of $\sqrt{d}$ for nuclear norm constraints compared to the best classical algorithm.

## Strengths
The paper is well-structured and presents its results in a clear and organized manner. The authors effectively communicate complex concepts, making the paper accessible to readers with varying levels of expertise in the field.

## Weaknesses
The novelty of the paper appears to be limited, as it primarily combines existing quantum algorithms with the Frank-Wolfe method. While this integration is executed well, the paper does not substantially advance the state of the art in terms of algorithmic innovation. The results, although interesting, seem to be a straightforward application of known techniques rather than a groundbreaking new approach. To strengthen the paper, the authors could focus on developing novel algorithmic components or providing fresh insights that specifically address the challenges of quantum optimization, going beyond the combination of existing methods.

## Questions
How does the proposed quantum Frank-Wolfe algorithm perform for non-smooth objective functions? Can the authors provide insights or extensions of their algorithm for non-smooth settings?
