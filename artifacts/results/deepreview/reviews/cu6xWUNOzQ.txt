# Review

## Summary
The authors propose a nonlinear, multimodal prediction model that combines audio and linguistic features from pre-trained models (e.g., Llama, Whisper) to predict brain responses. The proposed method improves prediction performance over traditional unimodal linear models and prior state-of-the-art models relying on weighted averaging of linear unimodal predictions.

## Strengths
1. The paper is well-written and easy to follow.
2. The authors conducted extensive experiments to demonstrate the effectiveness of nonlinear multimodal encoding.

## Weaknesses
1. The novelty is limited. The proposed method simply combines the linguistic and audio features to predict brain responses, which is somewhat incremental compared to the baseline method (Antonello et al., 2024).
2. The performance improvement is marginal. Compared to the baseline method (Antonello et al., 2024), the proposed method only slightly improves the prediction performance.
3. The proposed method is not well-motivated. The authors should clearly state the motivation for proposing the method.

## Questions
1. What is the motivation for proposing the method?
2. How does the proposed method contribute to advancing the field?
3. What are the unique challenges in applying nonlinear models to speech encoding compared to vision?
