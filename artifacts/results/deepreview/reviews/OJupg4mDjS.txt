# Review

## Summary
The paper proposes a method for Geodesic Principal Component Analysis (GPCA) on a collection of probability distributions using the Otto-Wasserstein geometry. The goal is to identify geodesic curves in the space of probability measures that best capture the modes of variation of the underlying dataset. The paper proposes a method for the case of a collection of Gaussian distributions, and a neural network based method for the more general setting of absolutely continuous probability measures. The paper compares the proposed method with classical tangent PCA through various examples and provides illustrations on real-world datasets.
