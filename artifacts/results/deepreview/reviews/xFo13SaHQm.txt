# Review

## Summary
The paper addresses the limitations of existing methods in generating identity-consistent images, particularly the copy-paste failure mode, where models replicate reference faces rather than generating varied images that maintain identity similarity across different poses, expressions, and lighting conditions. The authors introduce WithAnyone, a diffusion-based model that leverages a new large-scale paired dataset, MultiID-2M, and a novel training paradigm to mitigate copy-paste artifacts while preserving high identity fidelity. The paper demonstrates the effectiveness of WithAnyone through extensive qualitative and quantitative evaluations, showing improved controllability and reduced copy-paste artifacts compared to existing methods.

## Strengths
- The introduction of the MultiID-2M dataset, paired with a benchmark (MultiID-Bench) for evaluating multi-identity image generation, provides a foundation for future research in this area.
- The paper provides a thorough evaluation of the proposed method, using both qualitative and quantitative metrics, as well as user studies, to validate the effectiveness of WithAnyone in reducing copy-paste artifacts and improving identity fidelity.
- The model architecture and training objectives are well-explained, and the use of a contrastive identity loss function to balance identity fidelity with diversity is innovative and effective.

## Weaknesses
- The paper mentions the use of a negative pool for the contrastive loss but does not provide a detailed analysis of how the size of this pool affects performance.
- While the paper demonstrates the effectiveness of WithAnyone in reducing copy-paste artifacts, it would benefit from a more in-depth discussion on how the model handles other challenging scenarios, such as significant pose variations or expressions not included in the reference images.

## Questions
- How does the size of the negative pool used in the contrastive loss affect the model's performance?
- How does the model handle significant pose variations or expressions not included in the reference images?
- Are there any specific types of prompts or scenarios where WithAnyone struggles to maintain identity consistency?
