# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes a new turbulence modeling framework that enhances the frequency-aware learning by both implicitly and explicitly mitigating spectral bias and common-mode noise. The proposed method is validated on three canonical turbulent flow scenarios, demonstrating superior performance compared to state-of-the-art methods. Furthermore, the model exhibits strong generalization capabilities in challenging settings such as out-of-distribution domains, long-term temporal extrapolation, and robustness to noisy inputs.

## Strengths
1. The paper is well-written and easy to follow.
2. The authors provide a thorough theoretical analysis of the limitations of existing diffusion-based models in learning turbulent dynamics.
3. The proposed method is validated on three canonical turbulent flow scenarios, demonstrating superior performance compared to state-of-the-art methods.

## Weaknesses
1. The paper lacks a detailed analysis of the computational efficiency and scalability of the proposed method, which are important factors for practical applications.
2. The paper does not provide a detailed comparison of the proposed method with other state-of-the-art methods in terms of computational efficiency and scalability.

## Questions
1. How does the proposed method perform on larger and more complex turbulent datasets?
2. Can the proposed method be extended to other types of fluid dynamics simulations, such as those involving non-Newtonian fluids or multiphase flows?
3. How does the proposed method compare to other state-of-the-art methods in terms of computational efficiency and scalability?
