# Review

## Summary
The paper proposes a novel attention mechanism for DiT-based multi-condition image generation. The method is built upon an analysis of the attention patterns between different conditions and the image tokens. The paper proposes to replace the original full attention with a combination of Position-Aligned Attention (PAA) for spatial conditions and Keyword-Scoped Attention (KSA) for subject-driven conditions. The method is evaluated on three different multi-condition generation tasks and compared with two baseline methods. The results show that the proposed method achieves better generation quality, subject consistency, and controllability, with significantly improved efficiency.

## Strengths
1. The proposed method is well-motivated by the attention pattern analysis. 
2. The proposed method achieves significant efficiency improvement over the baseline methods while maintaining or improving the generation quality and controllability.
3. The paper is well-written and easy to follow.

## Weaknesses
1. The proposed method is only evaluated on three specific multi-condition generation tasks. It would be better to show more diverse applications of the method.
2. The proposed early-timestep sampling strategy is not well evaluated. It would be better to have a more thorough analysis of the strategy, such as how the choice of the distribution parameters affects the results.

## Questions
1. How does the proposed method perform on other multi-condition generation tasks?
2. How does the choice of the distribution parameters affect the results of the early-timestep sampling strategy?
