# Review

## Summary
The authors introduce a framework to enhance de novo peptide sequencing from tandem mass spectra by prioritizing causal fragment ions over noise peaks. This approach improves the robustness and accuracy of peptide predictions.

## Strengths
1. The paper is well-organized and easy to follow.
2. The authors conducted comprehensive experiments and provided detailed analyses.
3. The motivation is clearly explained, and the proposed method is both simple and effective.

## Weaknesses
1. The proposed method relies on the identification of non-causal ions, which is based on a mass tolerance threshold. However, it is unclear how to determine the optimal tolerance threshold. The authors should provide a more detailed discussion on this aspect.
2. The proposed method is model-agnostic; however, the authors only integrated it with CasaNovo, AdaNovo, and π-HelixNovo for evaluation. The authors should explain their criteria for selecting these specific models and discuss why they did not integrate the method with other models.
3. The proposed method is designed to enhance the performance of existing models; however, the performance of the enhanced models still falls short compared to state-of-the-art methods, such as SearchNovo and ContraNovo. The authors should discuss the reasons for this performance gap and propose potential strategies to address it.
