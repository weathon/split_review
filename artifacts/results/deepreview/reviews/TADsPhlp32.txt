# Review

## Summary
This paper presents a novel approach to AI-generated fake image detection by incorporating structural semantic features through a method called cuboidal partitioning. The authors argue that existing methods often miss subtle inconsistencies and artifacts left by generative models because they do not consider the underlying structure of the image. The proposed method recursively divides an image into meaningful sub-regions and extracts statistical differences between parent and child segments, integrating these features with existing detection frameworks. The paper reports superior performance on the GenImage benchmark and strong generalization on the AIGCDetect benchmark and the Chameleon dataset.

## Strengths

## Weaknesses

2. The paper may lack sufficient significance as its contributions, while valuable, may not be groundbreaking or transformative in the field of AI-generated fake image detection.
