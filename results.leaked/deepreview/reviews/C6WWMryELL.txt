# Review

## Summary
The paper investigates the issue of length volatility in long-form generation by Large Language Models (LLMs). To address this, the authors introduce VOLTBench, a benchmark designed to quantify length volatility across various tasks. They also propose SELB (Structural Enforcement via Logits Boosting), a lightweight, training-free decoding strategy to mitigate this issue.

## Strengths
1. The paper addresses a critical issue in long-form LLM generation, namely length volatility, which has significant implications for reliable application.
2. The introduction of VOLTBench provides a systematic and comprehensive framework for evaluating length volatility across diverse tasks.
3. The proposed SELB strategy is innovative and offers a practical solution to mitigate the issue of length volatility without requiring additional training.

## Weaknesses
1. The paper does not provide a clear and detailed explanation of how the modified logit vector $s'_t$ is computed in Equation 1, leaving gaps in understanding the methodology.
2. The paper does not clearly explain how the proposed SELB method integrates with existing decoding strategies, making it difficult to understand the practical implementation.
3. The evaluation of the SELB method is limited to a 100-section task under simple settings, which may not be sufficient to demonstrate its effectiveness across different scenarios.
4. The paper does not provide a detailed analysis of the computational cost and efficiency of the proposed SELB method, which is important for practical applications.
