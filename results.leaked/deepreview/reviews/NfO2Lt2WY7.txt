# Review

## Summary
The paper studies the components of Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) for reasoning in LLMs. It finds that negative feedback is important, PPO-style constraints are not necessary, and group relative advantage estimation can be used with simpler methods like REINFORCE. Experiments on math and science reasoning benchmarks show that the proposed RGR method performs well.

## Strengths
1. The paper is well-written and easy to follow.
2. The paper conducts a detailed ablation study on the components of GRPO.
3. The paper proposes a simpler method, RGR, that performs well on reasoning benchmarks.

## Weaknesses
1. The novelty of the paper is limited. The paper conducts an ablation study of GRPO and proposes a simpler method based on REINFORCE. The REINFORCE algorithm is a well-known algorithm in RL.
2. The paper does not conduct experiments on more complex reasoning benchmarks, such as MATH and MMLU-Pro. Therefore, it is unclear whether the findings of the paper still hold in more complex reasoning tasks.
3. The paper does not compare with other baselines, such as rejection sampling fine-tuning (RAFT) and self-consistency. Therefore, it is unclear whether the proposed method is better than existing methods.
4. The paper does not conduct experiments on larger models, such as 7B and 13B. Therefore, it is unclear whether the findings of the paper still hold in larger models.
