# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes a new LoRA-MoE structure, where LoRA modules are inserted into the linear projection layers in the attention module. To train the router, this paper designs a new loss function, which combines the effects of load balance and expert specialization. The experimental results on several datasets show the effectiveness of the proposed method.

## Strengths
1. The paper is well-organized and easy to follow. The authors provide a clear motivation for their work and a thorough explanation of their method.
2. The authors provide detailed theoretical analysis and proofs for the proposed loss function.
3. The experiments are conducted on several datasets, and the results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method.

## Weaknesses
1. The novelty of the proposed method is limited. The authors propose a new loss function to train the router, but the structure of the router and the way of inserting LoRA modules are not new.
2. The authors claim that the proposed method can use pre-trained LoRA modules, but the experiments are conducted on the datasets of the same domain. It is unclear how the proposed method works on the datasets of different domains. 
3. The authors do not provide the experimental results of the ablation study on the hyper-parameters α, β, and λ in the proposed loss function.

## Questions
1. How does the proposed method work on the datasets of different domains when using the LoRA modules of different domains?
2. How do the hyper-parameters α, β, and λ in the proposed loss function affect the performance of the model?
