# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes a new method for in-context image editing, which can be trained solely on video data. To achieve this, the authors propose a new data construction pipeline to annotate videos as interleaved multimodal sequences. Then, three proxy tasks are designed to train the model. Additionally, a new benchmark is proposed for multi-turn image editing.

## Strengths
- The paper is well-written and easy to follow.
- The idea of learning in-context image editing from videos is novel and interesting.
- The data construction pipeline and the new benchmark are useful to the community.

## Weaknesses
- The paper lacks important details about the model and training, making it difficult to fully understand and reproduce the method. For example, 
    - How are the visual transition annotations used? Are they used to generate the mask of changing areas in the source/target image? What if the annotations do not describe the changes in the video?
    - How is the model trained? Are the losses from all three proxy tasks used at the same time? 
    - What is the loss function? 
    - What is the number of frames and the length of videos in the training data?
- The authors do not provide an analysis of the model's capabilities and limitations. For example, 
    - What is the maximum length of video that the model can handle?
    - How well can the model handle videos with fast motion?
    - What is the model's performance on out-of-distribution videos? For example, can the model handle videos with multiple objects or scenes?
- The authors do not provide an analysis of the impact of different components of the model. For example, 
    - What is the impact of the number of frames in the context?
    - What is the impact of the length of the video in the context?
    - How important is the initialization from a video foundation model?

## Questions
- What is the impact of the quality of the video foundation model on the final performance?
- How well can the model handle videos with different resolutions and frame rates?
