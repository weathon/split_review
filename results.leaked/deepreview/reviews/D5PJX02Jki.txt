# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes RoPE++, which leverages the full complex-valued representation to create a dual-component attention score. The method enhances the modeling of long-context dependencies by preserving more positional information. Evaluations on a suite of long-context language modeling benchmarks show that the method consistently improves performance over the standard RoPE.

## Strengths
1. The paper is well-written and easy to follow.
2. The method is simple and clear.
3. The experimental results are good.

## Weaknesses
1. The method seems to be a simple variant of RoPE, which is not very innovative.
2. The experiments are not solid enough. The authors only conduct experiments on two small models, which are not enough to verify the effectiveness of the method. Besides, the authors do not conduct experiments on larger models (such as 7B and 13B), which are more meaningful in practical applications.
3. The authors do not provide the code, which harms the reproducibility of the paper.

## Questions
1. Can the method be applied to larger models?
2. Can the method be applied to other fields, such as CV?
