# Review

## Summary
This paper studies the problem of privacy-preserving sharing of text data, where the goal is to share noisy versions of transformer embeddings of texts. The authors propose Nonparametric Variational Differential Privacy (NVDP), a method that ensures both useful data sharing and strong privacy protection. They integrate a Nonparametric Variational Information Bottleneck (NVIB) layer into the transformer architecture to inject noise into the embeddings and measure privacy protection using Rényi divergence and Bayesian Differential Privacy (BDP). The authors test NVDP on the GLUE benchmark and demonstrate a tradeoff between privacy and accuracy by varying the noise level.

## Strengths
The paper is well-written and the problem is well-motivated. The method is described clearly and the paper is easy to follow. The experiments are conducted on the GLUE benchmark and the results are presented in a clear and concise manner.

## Weaknesses
1. The paper's technical contribution is limited. The proposed method combines existing techniques such as transformer embeddings, nonparametric variational information bottleneck, and Bayesian differential privacy. The authors should clarify what are the new challenges and what are the new technical contributions of this paper.

2. The paper lacks a theoretical analysis of the proposed method. The authors should provide a theoretical analysis of the privacy guarantees and utility bounds of their method.

3. The paper lacks a discussion of the computational complexity of the proposed method. The authors should provide an analysis of the computational complexity of their method and compare it with existing methods.
