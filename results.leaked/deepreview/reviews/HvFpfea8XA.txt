# Review

## Summary
This paper introduces AMADEUS, a training-free framework to improve persona consistency in RAG-based role-playing agents (RPAs). It addresses the challenge of handling questions beyond a character’s knowledge by using Adaptive Context-aware Text Splitter (ACTS), Guided Selection (GS), and Attribute Extractor (AE). The framework effectively maintains consistency even when answering out-of-knowledge questions by partitioning personas into optimally sized chunks and identifying general attributes to context.

## Strengths
1. The proposed framework effectively maintains persona consistency even when answering questions that a character’s knowledge.
2. The paper introduces the CharacterRAG dataset, a valuable resource for implementing and evaluating RAG-based RPAs.
3. The paper is well-organized and clearly written.

## Weaknesses
1. The paper lacks a comparison with other role-playing methods, such as [1] and [2]. Additionally, it does not include an analysis of the computational efficiency of the proposed framework.
2. The paper does not explain why the CharacterRAG dataset was created using Namuwiki, a Korean wiki, instead of using existing English role-playing datasets, such as Role-Playing Dataset (RoPD) [1] and Character-Action-Relation Dataset (CHARA) [2].
3. The paper does not provide a detailed analysis of the quality of the CharacterRAG dataset. For example, it does not include a comparison between the ground truth and the answers generated by the proposed framework.

[1] Role-Playing Evaluation for Large Language Models. EMNLP 2025
[2] TimeChara: Evaluating Point-in-Time Character Hallucination of Role-Playing Large Language Models. ACL 2024

## Questions
1. Why not compare with other role-playing methods?
2. Why not include an analysis of the computational efficiency of the proposed framework?
3. Why was the CharacterRAG dataset created using Namuwiki, a Korean wiki, instead of using existing English role-playing datasets, such as Role-Playing Dataset (RoPD) [1] and Character-Action-Relation Dataset (CHARA) [2]?
4. What is the quality of the CharacterRAG dataset? For example, what is the comparison between the ground truth and the answers generated by the proposed framework?
