# Review

## Summary
This paper introduces a content-aware Mamba-based model for learned image compression. The model includes two main components: (1) content-adaptive token permutation, which clusters tokens and reorders the scan sequence to bring similar tokens into proximity, and (2) global-prior prompting, which leverages clustering results to inject sample-specific global priors into the state-space model. Experimental results show that the proposed method achieves state-of-the-art rate-distortion performance on the Kodak, Tecnick, and CLIC datasets.

## Strengths
1. The proposed method is novel. The idea of clustering tokens to form content-aware Mamba is interesting and promising.
2. The paper is well-organized and easy to follow.
3. The experimental results are comprehensive and demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method.

## Weaknesses
1. The authors should provide a comparison of the computational complexity (e.g., FLOPs, throughput) between the proposed method and previous approaches.
2. The authors should provide a comparison of the model size and computational complexity between the proposed method and other Mamba-based methods.
3. The authors should discuss the complexity-performance trade-off of the proposed method.
4. The authors should discuss the impact of different clustering methods on performance.
5. The authors should discuss the impact of different numbers of clusters on performance.
