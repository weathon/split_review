# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes Dynamic Nested Depth (DND), a method to enhance the performance of LLMs by dynamically selecting critical tokens for reprocessing. The approach involves identifying difficult tokens and giving them an extra computational pass, while skipping less challenging ones to avoid redundancy. This selective reprocessing helps the model refine its understanding of complex tokens without wasting resources on simpler ones.

## Strengths
1. The proposed method is novel and intuitive.
2. The experiments are extensive and demonstrate the effectiveness of the method.
3. The paper is well-written and easy to follow.

## Weaknesses
1. The performance improvement is not very significant. 
2. The method requires additional training.
3. The proposed method is only validated on the Qwen-3 model. I would expect to see the effectiveness of the method on more model families.

## Questions
1. The performance improvement is not very significant. For example, in Table 1, the best performance gain is only 3.7%. 
2. The method requires additional training. How much extra training cost will this method bring? Will the training difficulty increase with the scale of the model? Is it possible to train a model directly using this method?
3. The proposed method is only validated on the Qwen-3 model. I would expect to see the effectiveness of the method on more model families, such as LLaMA, Mistral, and Phi.
