# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes Stylos, a feed-forward method for 3D stylization. It keeps geometry predictions on the self-attentive backbone path and conditioning appearance via global CrossBlocks on the style path, together with a voxel-space 3D style loss that aggregates multi-view features. Extensive experiments across category-level and large-scale scene datasets demonstrate the generalization ability of the proposed approach.

## Weaknesses

2. The paper does not discuss the limitations of the proposed method. What are the potential limitations or failure cases of the proposed method?
3. The paper does not provide a user study. Is it possible to conduct a user study to evaluate the aesthetic scores of the stylized results?
