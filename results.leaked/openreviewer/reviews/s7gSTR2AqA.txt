# Review

## Summary
The paper explores the capability of large language models (LLMs) to develop human-aligned semantic systems for categorizing colors. The authors investigate whether LLMs can evolve efficient, Information Bottleneck (IB)-efficient semantic systems similar to those used by humans. They conduct experiments using the Information Bottleneck framework and a cultural transmission simulation to test LLMs' inductive biases toward efficient categorization systems.

## Strengths
- The paper is well-written and easy to follow. The authors present their ideas clearly.
- The authors provide a thorough analysis of the results, discussing the implications and significance of the findings.
- The study addresses a unique and interesting research question about the emergence of human-aligned categorization in LLMs.

## Weaknesses
- The paper does not adequately address the potential impact of training data on the models' performance. The authors should consider the influence of the training dataset on the models' ability to learn an efficient semantic system. It would be helpful to explore how different training datasets might affect the results and whether the observed behavior is merely a result of the models learning patterns present in the training data.
- The paper could benefit from a more detailed discussion of the limitations of the study. The authors should consider the generalizability of their findings to other domains beyond color categorization and the implications of their results for the broader field of AI and cognitive science. What are the implications of your findings for the broader field of AI and cognitive science? How might this research inform the development of more human-aligned AI systems in the future?
- The paper lacks a thorough comparison with existing literature. The authors should consider how their findings relate to other studies that have explored the capabilities of LLMs in learning and transmitting linguistic and categorical systems. A more comprehensive comparison would help to contextualize the contributions of this work and highlight its novelty.

## Questions
- How might the training data of the LLMs influence their ability to learn an efficient semantic system? Could the observed behavior be simply a result of the models learning patterns present in the training data?
- What are the implications of your findings for the broader field of AI and cognitive science? How might this research inform the development of more human-aligned AI systems in the future?
- How do your findings relate to other studies that have explored the capabilities of LLMs in learning and transmitting linguistic and categorical systems? A more comprehensive comparison would help to contextualize the contributions of this work and highlight its novelty.
