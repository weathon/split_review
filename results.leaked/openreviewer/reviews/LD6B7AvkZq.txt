# Review

## Summary
This paper presents a formalization of the substructure of context-free grammars (CFGs), which are the languages with the most interesting learning dynamics. It then proves several theorems about the loss of language modeling in terms of the subgrammar structure. It shows that small transformers learn subgrammars in parallel, unlike humans. It also shows that pretraining on a subgrammar helps small transformers but not large ones, and that all language models struggle with deeper recursive structures.
