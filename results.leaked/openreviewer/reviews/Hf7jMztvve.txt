# Review

## Summary
The paper investigates strategic deception in large language models (LLMs) using two testbeds: Secret Agenda and Insider Trading compliance. The Secret Agenda game elicits deceptive behavior across 38 models, while SAE analysis reveals limitations in detecting this deception using auto-labeled features. In contrast, unlabeled aggregate activations provide population-level structure for risk assessment.

## Weaknesses
The paper's structure and presentation require significant improvement. The introduction lacks a clear problem statement and research questions, making it difficult to understand the motivation and objectives. 

The methodology is poorly described, with insufficient details about the experimental setup and procedures. 

The results section is confusing, with unclear organization and presentation of findings. 

There is no clear discussion of limitations or potential negative societal impacts. 

The paper lacks a comprehensive literature review and comparison with existing work. 

The contributions are not clearly articulated and lack significance. 

The paper does not provide any concrete conclusions or recommendations for future research. 

The overall writing quality is poor, with numerous grammatical errors and unclear expressions.
