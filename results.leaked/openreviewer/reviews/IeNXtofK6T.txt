# Review

## Summary
This paper presents a baseline defense against prompt injection attacks on LLMs. The defense uses a carefully designed system prompt to ask a modern LLM to detect and remove injected prompts. Experiments on three benchmarks show that the proposed defense can achieve low false positive and false negative rates in detecting contaminated data samples.

## Strengths
1. The paper is well-written and easy to follow.
2. The proposed defense is easy to deploy.

## Weaknesses
1. The proposed defense is too simple and lacks novelty. It is not surprising that using a system prompt to ask LLMs to detect and remove injected prompts works. The paper does not provide new insights into the vulnerability of LLMs to prompt injection attacks.
2. The paper does not evaluate the proposed defense against adaptive attacks. Prompt injection attacks are relatively easy to defend against. If a simple defense like the one proposed in the paper can be broken, it would be better to know how to break it and how to improve the defense.
