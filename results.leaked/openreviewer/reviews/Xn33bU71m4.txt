# Review

## Summary
The paper evaluates the performance of several popular LLMs in reverse engineering tasks such as function name recovery and type inference. The authors conclude that none of the models achieves a high F1 score in either task, and that fine-tuning improves the performance only marginally.

## Strengths
The authors evaluate a wide range of LLMs, and the paper is easy to follow.

## Weaknesses
The paper does not provide any new insights, and the authors do not offer a solution to the problem. The paper also does not compare the LLMs with other non-LLM approaches, such as neural-symbolic approaches, which have been shown to be quite effective in reverse engineering tasks.

## Questions
What are the insights provided by this paper that are not already known to the community?
How do LLMs compare to non-LLM approaches for reverse engineering?
