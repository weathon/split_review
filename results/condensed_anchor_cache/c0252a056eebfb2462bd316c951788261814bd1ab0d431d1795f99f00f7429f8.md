## Merged Review

### Summary
The paper proposes FourierMamba, an image deraining framework that integrates Mamba (state space model) into Fourier space to correlate different frequencies. It introduces two components: a spatial interaction SSM using zigzag scanning to rearrange frequency orders from low to high in the spatial-dimensional Fourier space, and a channel evolution SSM to directly model frequency correlations along the arranged channel axis. Experiments are conducted on synthetic and real-world deraining datasets.

### Strengths
- Novel application of Mamba to the Fourier domain for image deraining. (Reviewer 3, score 8, particularly highlights this as new and worthy to explore.)
- The proposed zigzag scanning strategy is novel and intuitive for rearranging low-to-high frequency orders in the spatial Fourier space, enabling systematic frequency correlation while utilizing Mamba's efficient sequence modeling. (Reviewers 1, 2, 4)
- The paper provides a thorough and intuitive discussion of the advantages of zigzag scanning in the deraining context. (Reviewer 4)
- The model achieves state-of-the-art or competitive quantitative results on several deraining datasets (e.g., Rain13k, SPA-Data, others in Table 1) while maintaining reasonable computational cost and model size. (Reviewers 1, 3, 4)
- Evaluation is performed on both synthetic and real-world datasets. (Reviewer 1)

### Weaknesses
- **Insufficient experimental comparisons.** Missing comparisons with recent methods: DRSformer, FADformer (Reviewer 2), Wavelet Approximation-Aware Residual Network (2023), Hybrid Transformer-Mamba Network (2024), Frequency-Enhanced State Space Model (2024) (Reviewer 4). Competitors in Fig. 6 are outdated (Reviewers 1, 4). Test100 dataset is not included in Table 1 (Reviewer 1). SPA-Data results for the closely related FreqMamba are missing (Reviewers 2, 3). Compared to other deraining or Mamba-based methods, the evaluation data/tasks are few (Reviewer 1).
- **No clear justification for why Mamba is superior to alternative architectures for frequency interaction.** The paper lacks theoretical analysis of frequency interactions for deraining (Reviewer 1). Motivations are weak: it is unclear why Mamba's sequence modeling is more effective than transformers with global attention or convolution-based frequency interaction (e.g., Fourmer) for processing Fourier frequencies (Reviewers 1, 3). The channel FFT concept was already proposed in "Revitalizing Channel-Dimension Fourier Transform for Image Enhancement"; the paper does not explain why channel Mamba outperforms a simple 1x1 convolution on channel spectra (Reviewer 1).
- **Ablations lack controlled computational-cost comparisons.** When replacing Mamba with 1x1 conv in ablation, no FLOPs or parameter counts are provided, so the improvement might stem from higher computation rather than the Mamba architecture itself (Reviewer 1). An ablation study on different frequency-domain loss functions (e.g., coherence-based distances vs. L1 norm) is missing (Reviewer 4).
- **Implementation details and efficiency concerns.** The implementation of zigzag scanning is non-trivial but lacks explanation, and its impact on inference speed is not assessed (Reviewer 4). Actual inference time comparison is needed beyond FLOPs because FFT is complex-valued and memory consumption can slow computation (Reviewer 2). A running time comparison would strengthen the evaluation (Reviewer 4).
- **Evaluation metrics limited to PSNR/SSIM.** The paper should include perceptual metrics like LPIPS, as PSNR/SSIM can be inconsistent with human perception (Reviewer 3).
- **Questions about wavelet vs. Fourier transform.** The choice of Fourier over wavelet is insufficiently justified, especially since the wavelet-based Mamba method (Zhen et al., 2024) shows close performance on several datasets (Reviewer 3).
- **Visual comparisons could be updated.** Provide zoomed-in patches or error maps for better insight (Reviewer 4). Additional visualization of intermediate features in the Fourier domain would help understand Mamba's advantages (Reviewer 3). More explanation on the difference from FreqMamba is needed (Reviewer 2).
- **Writing and presentation issues.** Typographical issues: "FCS" as abbreviation for "Fourier Channel Evolution" and "C-FFT" unclear (Reviewer 4). Duplicate words ("build correlation the correlation", line 75-76), formatting glitch in line 73, and "Fourier transformation." in Section 3.1 taking a whole line (Reviewer 3). The term "progressive training strategy" in Section 4.1 is undefined (Reviewer 3).
- **Generalizability not tested.** The zigzag scanning strategy appears applicable to other low-level vision tasks (e.g., deblurring), but the paper does not evaluate on additional tasks (Reviewer 4).
