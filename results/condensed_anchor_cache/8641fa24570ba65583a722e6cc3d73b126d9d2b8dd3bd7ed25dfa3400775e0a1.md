## Merged Review

### Summary
The paper proposes a framework for improving GUI grounding in multimodal LLMs, consisting of an automated data collection engine that generates large-scale annotated GUI screenshots, a lightweight grounding module (0.4B parameters) trained on that data, and a method to integrate this module with MLLMs. Experiments show strong performance on benchmarks including ScreenSpot, MiniWob, AITW, and Mind2Web.

### Strengths
- **State-of-the-art image-only performance** across grounding (ScreenSpot) and automation benchmarks (MiniWob, AITW, Mind2Web). (Reviewer 1)
- **Lightweight grounding model** (0.4B parameters) that achieves strong results despite being much smaller than 7B models like Fuyu and SeeClick; includes detailed ablations of modeling choices. (Reviewers 1, 3)
- **Automated and extensible dataset collection** that produces a large-scale, diverse GUI screenshot dataset with annotations, mitigating the shortage of public GUI data. (Reviewers 1, 2, 4)
- **Robust experimental evaluation**, including ablation studies that support both model design and dataset effectiveness. (Reviewers 2, 4)
- **Clear writing and well-structured presentation** of results. (Reviewer 3)

### Weaknesses
- **Novelty concerns:** The dataset collection has similarities to SeeClick (main difference: source of annotations—UI Automation framework vs DOM attributes). The model architecture is similar to LISA and SAM but with detection heads/loss instead of segmentation. (Reviewer 1)
- **Missing details in data cleaning:** The process for identifying erroneous element names and restructuring ambiguous element hierarchies is not clearly explained (e.g., what ground truth is used to identify errors). (Reviewer 1)
- **Need for error/failure analysis:** The paper notes that image-only approaches fall short of text-based ones, but provides no analysis of error patterns. Failure cases of the grounding model and agent should be included to highlight limitations. (Reviewers 1, 2, 4)
- **Overclaimed generality and evaluation scope:** The claim of "general GUI grounding" is unsupported because evaluations are limited to web-based tasks (Android-AITW, Mind2Web). The term is misleading as the dataset is collected from web pages. Additional experiments on software/OS-level benchmarks (e.g., OS-World, WindowsAgentArena, VisualAgentBench) are needed. (Reviewers 2, 4)
- **Possible domain overlap on Mind2Web:** The significant improvement on Mind2Web may stem from overlap in website domains (e.g., shopping, travel) between training and test sets, rather than generalized performance; SeeClick uses more randomly sampled Common Crawl data. (Reviewer 3)
- **Weaknesses compared to stronger baselines:** Results do not significantly outperform SeeClick on ScreenSpot, AITW, and MiniWob despite a larger training dataset. The paper lacks comparison with SeeClick-V and Set-of-Marks, which would better highlight strengths and weaknesses. (Reviewers 2, 3, 4)
- **Icon grounding limitations:** The model struggles with grounding icon elements (essential and challenging in GUI tasks) while text elements are easier via OCR; more discussion and solutions are needed. (Reviewer 3)
- **Missing ablations and experiments:** No ablation of data filtering/cleaning on ScreenSpot or other evals is provided to differentiate the pre-training dataset. (Reviewer 1) Experiments on scaling grounding performance with more training data are missing. (Reviewer 1) It is unclear whether models are separately fine-tuned per benchmark; a unified model trained on all three datasets would test robustness across UI types. (Reviewer 1)
- **Insufficient dataset analysis:** Only dataset size is reported; more detailed statistics (e.g., on data levels, characteristics) are needed. The paper must address whether NSFW content exists and discuss copyright issues from web crawling. (Reviewers 2, 4)
- **Advantage over end-to-end MLLMs not discussed:** The paper does not discuss the advantage of the proposed MLLM+AGG plugin over a better fine-tuned end-to-end MLLM like Qwen2-VL, which can already perform grounding. (Reviewer 3)
- **Presentation and clarity issues:** The experiment section lacks clarity—benchmarks and evaluation metrics are introduced in the Results section rather than a proper Experiment Setup section. (Reviewers 2, 4) Formatting issues: the overview figure is placed between the section title and content of the Introduction; missing spaces before `\cite`; incorrect usage of `\citep` and `\citet`. (Reviewer 4) In Fig. 3, recording "Click at (coordinates)" as part of action history may not be meaningful without screenshot context. (Reviewer 3)

*Note: Reviewer 4 indicated they would consider raising the overall score if writing and presentation concerns are addressed.*
