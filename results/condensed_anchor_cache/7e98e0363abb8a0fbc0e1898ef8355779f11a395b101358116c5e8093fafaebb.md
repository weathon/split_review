## Merged Review

### Summary
Existing 3D single object tracking (SOT) approaches are category-specific, requiring separate models per category. This paper is the first to propose category-unified models that track objects across all categories with a single network using shared parameters. The key contribution is AdaFormer, a point set transformer that adaptively encodes varying shape and size information from cross-category data. The model incorporates size/shape priors from template targets into inputs and objectives. Two unified models, SiamCUT (Siamese) and MoCUT (motion-centric), are constructed. Experiments on KITTI and NuScenes show performance gains over category-specific baselines (e.g., ~12% and ~3% on KITTI for Siamese and motion paradigms). The paper is well-written and technically sound.

### Strengths
- **Valuable problem:** Current 3D SOT requires multiple category-specific models; this paper is the first to unify all categories into a single network, which is novel and significant to the community (R1, R2, R3).
- **Good presentation:** The paper is well-written and easy to follow (R1, R3).
- **Technical soundness:** Modules are progressively proposed and the AdaFormer effectively learns adaptive ball regions for different categories, matching the motivation (R1, R3).
- **Strong performance:** SiamCUT and MoCUT achieve good results on KITTI and nuScenes, outperforming category-specific counterparts by a significant margin (R1, R2).
- **Minority positive view:** Reviewer 2 found no obvious weakness, indicating strong support for the contributions (R2).

### Weaknesses
- **Missing training details:** It is unclear whether the unified model is trained on the full KITTI and tested on it, or on a combination of KITTI and nuScenes. This should be clarified (R1).
- **Waymo evaluation missing:** The effectiveness of the approach should be verified on the Waymo dataset (R1, R3).
- **Missing recent comparisons:** The following state-of-the-art methods should be included for fair comparison: [1] Temporal-aware Siamese Tracker (ACCV 2022), [2] 3D Siamese Transformer Network (ECCV 2022), [3] CXTrack (CVPR 2023), [4] Lightweight and Detector-Free 3D SOT (TITS 2023) (R1). Additionally, motion-centric and other SOTA methods like [c] Beyond 3D Siamese Tracking and [d] Implicit and Efficient Point Cloud Completion should be compared (R3).
- **Missing latency/efficiency analysis:** Inference speed and FLOPs comparisons are not provided; these metrics are requested to assess efficiency (R2, R3).
- **Insufficient related work discussion:** The paper should discuss related works on encoding shape- and size-changed geometric information, such as adaptive region learning (Pyramid R-CNN [a]), dynamic ball-query selection (DBQ-SSD [b]), and ray-based grouping (RBGNet [c]) (R3).
- **Missing analysis on size diversity:** Additional statistical analysis to reveal the diverse sizes of different categories would further support the motivation (R3).
- **Extension to detection not discussed:** The potential extension of AdaFormer to general 3D detection is not addressed (R3).
