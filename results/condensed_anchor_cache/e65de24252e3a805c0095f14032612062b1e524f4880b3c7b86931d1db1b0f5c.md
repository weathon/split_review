## Merged Review

### Summary
This paper proposes a new task, Multi-event Video-Text Retrieval and Grounding (MVT-RG), and a model with two modules (RE-CVTR for retrieval using inter-/intra-video event relations, and EC-DVG for grounding via contrast-driven divergence learning). Experiments on ActivityNet-Captions and Charades-STA claim superiority over reproduced baselines.

### Strengths
1.  **Novel task setting**: Introducing a joint multi-event retrieval and grounding task (MVT-RG) that extends single-event works and is relevant to real-world applications such as online video search and recommendation.
2.  **Novel modules**: Proposing RE-CVTR (relational event-centric retrieval) and EC-DVG (event-contrast-driven video grounding) to model inter- and intra-video event relationships.
3.  **Clear motivation and methodology**: The paper clearly explains the gap in existing work and the motivation; the method section is well-written and easy to follow, covering relationship modeling of multiple queries and the grounding module.
4.  **Detailed training/inference description**: Datasets, implementation details, evaluation metrics, and training/inference procedures are clearly explained.
5.  **Outperforms current methods**: Experiments show improved results over reproduced baselines (e.g., JSG), and the reproduction effort is informative.

### Weaknesses
1.  **Evaluation metric mismatch**: The paper uses metrics different from the widely adopted R@(1,5) with IoU thresholds, which may hinder direct comparison with prior works.
2.  **Limited dataset diversity**: Only two benchmarks (ActivityNet-Captions, Charades-STA) are used; more datasets are needed to prove generalizability.
3.  **Incomplete experimental comparisons**:
    *   **Unfair SOTA baselines**: Most SOTA results are taken from JSG’s single-event retrieval setting without adapting them to the multi-query task. JSG and other VMR/VCMR methods are not designed for multi-event queries, making direct comparison not credible. Appropriate adaptation of existing methods (e.g., concatenating multiple queries) should be used as baselines.
    *   **Missing separate evaluation**: The model has both retrieval and grounding capabilities, but the paper only reports final combined results rather than separate metrics for each subtask.
    *   **Insufficient ablation**: Only three modules are ablated; many components lack ablation experiments, e.g., each loss term in Formula 11, the necessity of GCN for query relationship modeling, and the effect of the 2D map or reconstruction strategy (used for weakly supervised settings) that should be introduced and compared against prior works (e.g., [1], [2]).
    *   **No computational overhead analysis**: The model is relatively complex; tests on runtime, parameters, or memory are missing.
    *   **Lack of retrieval visualization**: No qualitative examples of retrieval results or analysis of different ranking strategies in the retrieval subtask.
4.  **Factual errors and misstatements**:
    *   Typographical errors: “MV-TRG” on line 186 and “Fig. 1” on line 276.
    *   Lines 52-53 claim MeVTR relies on time labels, but MeVTR does not require time labels.
    *   The defined MVTR task overlaps more with arxiv:2201.03639 than with MeVTR, making the task scope unclear.
5.  **Dataset annotation issue**: Not all datasets contain multi-event labels per video; the paper does not explain how this problem is addressed.
6.  **Potential localization errors**: Overview of the pipeline suggests possible errors in event localization for certain scenarios, and the method’s robustness is not sufficiently tested.
