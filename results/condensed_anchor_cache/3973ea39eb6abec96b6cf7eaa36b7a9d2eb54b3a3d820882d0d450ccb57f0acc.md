## Merged Review

### Summary
The paper introduces point offset prediction for 3D point cloud anomaly detection under the anomaly-free setting, aiming to focus on pseudo-abnormal points rather than reconstructing all points. A normal-vector-guided augmentation (NORM-AS) generates pseudo anomalies to improve training. Experiments on Anomaly-ShapeNet and Real3D-AD show average AUC-ROC improvements of 9.0% and 1.4% over prior state-of-the-art, using MinkUNet34C as backbone and offset losses (\(L_{offset}, L_{dir}, L_{dist}\)). All reviewers acknowledge the novelty of offset prediction and strong empirical results, but raise concerns about theoretical justification, experimental completeness, and generalization.

### Strengths
- **Novel approach**: First to introduce offset prediction into 3D-AD (R1, R2, R3, R4). The scheme is simple, works well, and is seen as a valuable new direction.
- **Strong performance**: Significantly outperforms existing methods on two benchmark datasets, with clear experimental evidence (R1, R2, R3).
- **Scalability and potential as framework**: The model idea is clean and the backbone can be replaced by other point prediction models; strong scalability for future work (R4).
- **Code provided**: The authors release code, which facilitates reproduction and extension (R2 – note that environment specs and README are missing, but the code itself is beneficial).
- **Clear presentation**: Well‑structured, easy to read, with detailed results and analyses (R1). Some reviewers noted minor writing issues (R4) but overall clarity is praised.

### Weaknesses
- **Lack of theoretical justification**: Reviewers note the paper is empirical; why offset prediction (vs. reconstruction) is more effective is not deeply explained. One reviewer (score 3) emphasized this gap and requested theoretical grounding or ablation on the offset prediction approach. Another reviewer (score 5) asked for a clearer motivation beyond a scheduler/focal loss analogy (R2, R3).
- **Insufficient comparison of pseudo-anomaly generation (NORM-AS)**: The paper does not compare NORM-AS with existing anomaly generation methods for 3DAD (e.g., IMRNet, R3D-AD). The novelty of NORM-AS relative to prior augmentations (like R3D-AD) is questioned (R2, R4). One reviewer asked whether the generated anomalies are realistic enough and how overfitting risk is mitigated (R3).
- **Limited datasets**: Only two datasets (Anomaly-ShapeNet and Real3D-AD) are used; more datasets (e.g., MVTec 3D) are needed to verify generalization (R1, R3).
- **Backbone selection not justified**: The paper uses MinkUNet34C without comparing to other point cloud backbones (e.g., PointMAE used in M3DM) or modifying it for the AD task. No ablation on backbone choice or explanation why no downsampling is applied (R3, R4). One reviewer noted that the design choice for the anomaly detection task is limited (R2).
- **Missing point-level results for Real3D-AD**: Only image‑level (I‑AUROC) results are reported; point‑level (P‑AUROC) results are absent, which is a standard metric for fine‑grained evaluation (R3). The paper states separate metrics but does not report them for Real3D‑AD.
- **Limited evidence that the model focuses on anomalies**: The only support is Figure 1 (c) and (d); reviewers request more explanatory quantitative indicators (e.g., heatmaps of outlier attention) or visualizations beyond qualitative examples (Figure 6). One reviewer observed that the curve in Figure 1(d) might suggest difficulty converging to anomalies (R4).
- **Ablation experiment incomplete**: Only 15 out of 40 classes were used for ablation; a full 40‑class ablation is needed to fully understand each component’s contribution (R4).
- **No comparison of training time**: Reconstruction methods often have long training times; the paper does not compare time performance of PO3AD with other reconstruction methods (R4).
- **Lack of related work on offset prediction**: No introduction to offset prediction techniques in the point cloud domain or discussion of why they apply to 3D-AD (R2).
- **Writing issues**: Abstract considered complex; description of performance could separate P‑AUROC and I‑AUROC more clearly (R4). One reviewer noted writing is “miscellaneous” (R4).
- **Role of \(L_{dir}\) not fully analyzed**: The ablation study shows \(L_{dir}\) significantly influences performance (on Anomaly‑ShapeNet), but no analysis is given for Real3D‑AD (R2).
- **One reviewer (score 3) was less convinced**: This minority view found the paper “resembles an empirical investigation” and the design “limited.” The same reviewer questioned whether the offset prediction could be applied in a KNN‑plus‑memory‑bank framework (R2). Another reviewer (score 5) expressed strong reservations about experimental sufficiency and stated a weak reject preference despite the high score (R4). This contrast indicates significant disagreement among reviewers.
