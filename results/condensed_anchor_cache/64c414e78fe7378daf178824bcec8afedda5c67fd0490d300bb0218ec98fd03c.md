## Merged Review

### Summary
The paper proposes CoSPaL (Contextual Self-Paced Learning) for Weakly Supervised Spatio-Temporal Video Grounding (WSTVG). It integrates three components: Tubelet Phrase Grounding (TPG), Contextual Referral Grounding (CRG), and Self-Paced Scene Understanding (SPS). Results are reported on VidSTG, HC-STVG-v1, and HC-STVG-v2. Reviewers agree the problem is significant and the method achieves performance gains (e.g., 3.9% on VidSTG, 7.9% on HC-STVG-v1), but raise major concerns about technical novelty, experimental fairness, missing ablations, and unclear descriptions. One reviewer (score 8) was highly positive about innovation and writing; another (score 3) considered novelty insufficient for ICLR and cited issues with claimed contributions. The remaining reviewers (scores 5, 6) acknowledge improvements but question the depth of analysis and clarity.

### Strengths
- The problem of WSTVG is significant and interesting. (R2, R3)
- CoSPaL integrates three components (TPG, CRG, SPS) motivated by observed limitations of foundation models (GroundingDINO) for WSTVG. (R1)
- The paper is well-written and easy to follow. (R1)
- Strong performance improvements over baselines on VidSTG, HC-STVG-v1, and HC-STVG-v2. (R1, R3)
- Comprehensive quantitative analysis and visualizations. (R3)
- Compared methods are state-of-the-art (e.g., CG-STVG, VGDINO, 2024). (R4)
- Figures and tables are clear. (R4)

### Weaknesses
1. **Motivation and novelty concerns**  
   - The motivation for self-paced learning is insufficiently illustrated; other methods could address the challenges. A clearer related-work discussion is needed. (R2)  
   - The technical contribution of TPG is unclear: cross-modal attention, contrastive learning, and feature reconstruction are common techniques. Why this specific implementation yields improvements is not justified. (R3)  
   - The term “self-paced” is not clearly defined; the section title “Self-Paced Scene Understanding” is essentially a self-paced curriculum learning strategy already used in prior work (Wang et al., 2022a; Soviany et al., 2022). Thus it should not be listed as a third contribution. (R4)  
   - Abstract claims “first explore the potential of state-of-the-art object detection models for WSTVG” – this is incorrect because many STVG methods already use object detectors (e.g., [1]). (R4)  
   - Overall novelty is insufficient for ICLR; most modules (cross attention in spatial/temporal grounding) are popularly used. (R4)

2. **Missing ablations and unfair comparisons**  
   - Video encoder: Using I3D as a separate encoder may lead to unfair comparison with prior methods that use ResNet-101 per frame or Grounding-DINO’s frozen image encoder. Ablation using the same features as Grounding-DINO’s backbone is needed. (R1)  
   - The pretrained tracker (BoTSORT) in TPG: GroundingDINO already provides bounding boxes, so BoTSORT appears to be only refinement. An ablation study on BoTSORT itself (e.g., different trackers) is required to measure its impact and demonstrate novelty. (R1, R2)  
   - Main ablation study is placed in the appendix; it should be in the main paper. (R4)  
   - Figure 5 (GPU memory / training time comparison) is ambiguous: fully supervised models use different resolutions, but the paper does not report the resolution settings or whether hardware (CPU, GPU, memory) was unified across models. (R2)

3. **Unclear or incomplete descriptions**  
   - In Section 3.2.1 (Spatial Grounding), the formulas for similarity and attention are confusing. For similarity: \(f_{w_m}\in\mathbb{R}^{1\times 768}\), \(f_{\tilde{T}_k}\in\mathbb{R}^{T\times 256}\); after MLPs, matrix multiplication dimension mismatch. For attention: \(\sum_{k=1}^K\) appears to sum tubelet features, producing a \(1\times D\) vector – it is unclear how per-tubelet score distribution is obtained. (R2)  
   - How sample complexity is determined in SPS is unclear (e.g., is it object number?); a rigorous definition and explanation of curriculum structure is missing. (R3)  
   - The meaning of “self-paced” vs. curriculum learning is not explained; the model’s adaptation mechanism (how it transitions from coarse to fine) lacks detail. (R3)  
   - Table 5: Adding SPS to TPG causes a drop in vIoU@0.3 (TPG+SPS vs. TPG). This negative effect needs deeper analysis, including conditions under which SPS helps or hurts. (R3)  
   - Page 10 claims an impact on actor localization but provides no corresponding quantitative results. (R3)  
   - Start and end timestamps are missing in Fig. 4. (R4)  
   - Grammar errors: e.g., “three benchmark WSTVG datasets” in Abstract. (R4)
