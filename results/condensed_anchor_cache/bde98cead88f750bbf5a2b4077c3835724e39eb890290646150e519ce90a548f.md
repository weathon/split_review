## Merged Review

### Summary
The paper introduces Cross-Block Quantization (CBQ), a post-training quantization (PTQ) method for LLMs. CBQ addresses inter-block dependencies via a cross-block reconstruction loss (L2 + KL) over a sliding window of blocks, uses a coarse-to-fine preprocessing (CFP) strategy for weight/activation outliers, and applies adaptive LoRA-Rounding for weight quantization. Experiments on LLaMA1, LLaMA2, and OPT show gains in low-bit settings (W4A4, W2A16, etc.) over prior methods like OmniQuant. Performance and efficiency tradeoffs are demonstrated.

### Strengths
- **Key insight**: Full-model quantization introduces inter-layer correlation; CBQ’s cross-block dependency directly tackles error accumulation (Reviewers 1,3,5).
- **Strong low-bit results**: Substantial improvements over OmniQuant, especially at 2-bit (Reviewer 4). Significant performance gains in W2 and W4 settings (Reviewer 1).
- **Thorough ablation**: The paper ablated multiple aspects of the objective (Reviewer 4).
- **Effective outlier handling**: Coarse-to-fine preprocessing and adaptive rounding yield flexibility across model sizes (Reviewer 3).
- **Broad experimental scope**: Tested across several LLM architectures and bit‑widths (W4A4, W4A8, W2A16, W6A6) with clear gains (Reviewers 3,5).
- **Innovation and significance**: Inter‑block dependency is intuitive and impactful; reducing overhead without retraining is valuable for deployment (Reviewer 5).
- **High confidence**: One reviewer (scoring 10, confidence 5) found the approach solid and convincing, with no weaknesses raised (Reviewer 2).

### Weaknesses
- **Writing clarity**: Notation is unclear in key equations – symbols *i*, *j*, *K* in Eq.3 and *V* in Eq.11 are not defined on first use. The term “scales” in “comparisons of the scales between adjacent layers” is ambiguous (step sizes? weight magnitudes?). (Reviewer 1)
- **Limited model coverage**: Only older models (LLaMA‑1, LLaMA‑2, OPT) are evaluated. Results on recent open‑source LLMs (LLaMA‑3, Mistral, Falcon, Gemma‑2) are missing, limiting generalizability. Also lacks evaluation across a wider range of model sizes within each architecture. (Reviewers 1,4,5)
- **Missing baseline comparisons**: CBQ is not compared with important methods: AWQ for W4 (Reviewer 1), rotation‑based methods (FrameQuant, QuIP) for 2‑bit (Reviewer 4), and recent PTQ method BiE (Reviewer 5). Such comparisons would clarify CBQ’s relative advantages.
- **No evaluation of LoRA-Rounding efficiency**: The authors claim LoRA-Rounding reduces computation but provide no FLOPs or training‑time measurements versus alternative rounding techniques. (Reviewer 1)
- **Latency impact from LoRA parameters not reported**: Adding LoRA parameters likely incurs latency; the paper should present inference latency numbers, especially for W2A16 and W4A4. (Reviewer 4)
- **Overlap size analysis missing**: The paper lacks an in‑depth study of how varying the sliding‑window overlap size affects memory footprint, latency, quantization stability, redundant computations, and convergence rate. (Reviewer 3)
- **Coarse-to-fine preprocessing not justified**: The necessity of the proposed outlier‑handling strategy over simpler alternatives (e.g., simple thresholding, percentile‑based clipping) is not demonstrated via ablation. (Reviewer 3)
- **Generalizability to other architectures**: Evaluation is limited to language models. The method should be tested on vision transformers and vision‑language models (VLMs) to show broader applicability. (Reviewer 5)
- **Missing sub‑channel weight‑only quantization results**: Since sub‑channel quantization is common in practice, the paper should report weight‑only quantization numbers with sub‑channel granularity for all bit‑widths. (Reviewer 4)
- **Minority reviewer disagreement**: One reviewer (score 10) reported no weaknesses, while other reviewers collectively raised the above issues. This contrast should be noted. (Reviewer 2 vs. others)
- **Unaddressed reviewer questions** (overlap with missing experiments/ablations):
    - Will code be released? (Reviewer 1)
    - Is *V* the rounding mask? Is it applied to each matrix separately? (Reviewer 1)
    - Has CBQ been tested for few‑shot scenarios? (Reviewer 1)
    - Does the phenomenon in Figure 1 appear in other models (LLaMA‑3, Mistral, Falcon)? (Reviewer 1)
    - How is W6A6 implemented, and what are its latency benefits? (Reviewer 4)
    - How does CFP complexity/execution time compare to OMSE or SmoothQuant? (Reviewer 5)
    - Expected quantization efficiency for models >100B parameters? (Reviewer 5)
    - Scaling to multimodal inputs? (Reviewer 3)
