## Merged Review

### Summary

The paper proposes Reconstruction-Guided Policy (RGP) for cooperative multi-agent reinforcement learning under partial observability. RGP reconstructs an *agent-wise* state (capturing inter-agent relationships) from local observations using a diffusion model during both training and execution, ensuring state consistency and preserving agent dependencies. A guidance module (attention-based) aligns the reconstructed states. Experiments on discrete (SMAC, SMACv2) and continuous (Predator-Prey, Cooperative Navigation) benchmarks show performance improvements over baselines, with detailed ablations. Reviewers appreciate the problem choice, method novelty (diffusion model in MARL), ablation studies, and strong results. However, concerns include limited evaluation scope, slight improvements often within error margins, missing comparisons with established partial-observability methods, insufficient theoretical justification for diffusion over simpler models, unclear figures, grammatical errors, and missing code/hyperparameter details. One reviewer (score 8) is more positive about clarity and motivation; another (score 5) is more critical of evaluation and presentation.

### Strengths

- **Problem importance and motivation**: Addresses partial observability and inter-agent dependencies in MARL. (R1, R2, R3)
- **Novel method**: Use of diffusion models and attention mechanisms for agent-wise state reconstruction is interesting and novel. (R2, R4)
- **Well-structured and clear (R1)**: Writing flows well; important assumptions are made explicit; enumerating research questions helps clarity. (R1) *[Contrast: R2, R3, R4 flag poor grammar and confusing figures/examples]*
- **Strong ablation study**: Disentangles components and justifies design choices. (R2, R4)
- **Good results across discrete and continuous benchmarks** (R2, R3). Strong performance on SMACv2 and continuous tasks. (R2)
- **Focus on state consistency**: Mitigates training-execution inconsistency, a key challenge. (R1, R3)

### Weaknesses

- **Limited evaluation scope**: Most conclusions drawn from SMAC; only 3 continuous tasks tested, which is not extensive. (R4) Scalability to more agents and highly dynamic environments not thoroughly evaluated. (R3) Missing plots for SMAC tasks and time horizon details. (R2)
- **Marginal improvements over baselines**: Many reported gains are small and within one standard error (e.g., Table 1: RGP+HPN overlaps HPN-QMIX on all scenarios; RGP overlaps QMIX, VDN, QPLEX). (R4) Performance Improving Ratio in Table 3 ignores error propagation; Performance Retention Ratio in Table 2 also omits error. (R4) Large standard deviations in baselines like SIDiff (Table 2) and QPLEX (Table 1) raise reliability concerns. (R3)
- **Missing comparisons with established partial-observability methods**: Ignores agent/state modelling, world models, transformer-based works that also aim to deal with partial observability; e.g., methods using state embeddings instead of reconstructed states already address inconsistency. (R2) Some baselines are not from top-tier AI/ML venues; suggested additions: [3],[4],[5] etc. (R2)
- **Insufficient justification for diffusion model**: Ablation (Fig 6) shows VAE and MLP achieve similar performance; why choose computationally heavier diffusion? (R3, R4) Theoretical foundation for diffusion over other generative models unclear. (R3)
- **Unclear figures and annotations**: Shaded regions in Fig 5 unexplained; number of runs/seeds unspecified for uncertainty quantification. (R1) Figure 2 is challenging to interpret; network design and data flow need more explanation. (R3) Illustrative example in lines 71–80 is difficult to follow (agent-wise vs dimensional-wise). (R4)
- **Grammatical and typographical errors**: Multiple Typos (e.g., “continuous” typo line 483) and grammar mistakes (e.g., lines 240-244). (R1, R2, R4) Need careful proofreading.
- **Missing code and hyperparameter details**: Attached repo (anonymous.4open.science/r/RGP-9F79) contains only a readme file; no code. (R2, R4) Hyperparameter details of baselines not included. (R2) Uncertainty measure in Table 1 not specified (standard error?). (R1)
- **Key terms not clearly defined**: “dimensional-wise state” and “agent-wise state” need better contrast, especially since “agent-wise” is central. (R4) Misleading use of epsilon in Eq (3) (diffusion noise) and Eq (6) (exploration parameter). (R3)
- **Missing limitations and robustness analysis**: No limitations section; speculative reasons (e.g., Predator-Prey results) should be summarized. (R1) Robustness to environment changes or deployment discrepancies not addressed; no online adaptation or domain randomization considered. (R3)
- **Other minor weaknesses**: Lack of explanation for how MPE Navigation (Spread) was made continuous. (R2) The guidance module’s mechanism for shifting focus from observation features to agents is unclear. (R4) The term “decentralised execution” vs standard CTDE not elaborated. (R4)

*Reviewer disagreement*: One reviewer (score 8) found the writing clear and well-motivated; others (scores 6,5,6) noted grammatical errors, unclear figures, and difficult-to-follow examples. The more negative reviewer (score 5) emphasized limited scalability, diffusion justification, and large baseline variances.
