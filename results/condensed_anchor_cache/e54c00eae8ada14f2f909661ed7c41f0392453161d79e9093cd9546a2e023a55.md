## Merged Review

### Summary
The paper proposes a neural network-based approach to predict stock trends by analyzing company interdependencies and news sentiment. It uses data from stocknewsapi.com, applies regression analysis, and constructs dependency graphs (similar to BFS) on a hypothetical scenario of companies linked by supply chains and resource dependencies. The reviewers unanimously reject the paper (all scores 1). They find it lacks scientific rigor, novelty, clarity, and evaluation. One reviewer notes the importance of the problem but all agree the paper is far below ICLR standards, with one stating it should not have passed pre-screening.

### Strengths
- The problem studied—how company interdependencies (e.g., clients, suppliers, competitors) influence stock values—is important (Reviewer 2).  
  *Note: The other two reviewers identified no strengths (Reviewer 1: “n/a”; Reviewer 3: “None”).*

### Weaknesses
- **Lacks a thorough literature review.** The paper has nearly no references (only two), and those included are not from peer-reviewed sources. This fails to situate the work within existing knowledge (all reviewers; especially Reviewer 2, Reviewer 3 W5).
- **No clear research niche or proposed novel approach.** The paper does not articulate a specific research gap or a novel solution; the methodology appears to be a simple combination of existing techniques (Reviewer 1; implied by Reviewer 3).
- **Missing model details and clarity.** Specific algorithms, neural network architecture, data collection procedures, and variable definitions are absent or vaguely described. The paper fails to clearly convey complex procedures, making assessment difficult (Reviewer 1: lacks specific algorithms; Reviewer 2: model designs/source details missing; Reviewer 3 W1, W3).
- **Lacks comparison with existing models or baselines.** Without any empirical evaluation against prior approaches, it is impossible to gauge the contribution or advancement of this work (Reviewer 3 W2; Reviewer 1 mentions insufficient evaluations).
- **Relies on a hypothetical scenario rather than real-world data.** The study is based on a simulated company ecosystem, which may not account for real-world variables and uncertainties, reducing applicability and robustness (Reviewer 3 W4).
- **No comprehensive experimental evaluation.** The paper does not present concrete results, tables, figures, or datasets to support its claims (Reviewer 1; implicit in all weaknesses above).
- **Writing and presentation are below scientific standards.** The submission is not scientific writing, lacks rigor, and should not have passed pre-screening (Reviewer 2; also Reviewer 3 Q1 notes substantial revisions needed in presentation, methodology, experiments, and discussion).
