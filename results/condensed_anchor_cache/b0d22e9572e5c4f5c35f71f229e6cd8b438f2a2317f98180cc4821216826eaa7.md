## Merged Review

### Summary

This paper proposes X-Gen, a cross-view video prediction model that generates future ego-centric frames given an exo-centric video, the first ego-centric frame, and text instructions. X-Gen first predicts hand-object interaction (HOI) masks in future ego frames via an exo-to-ego mask prediction network, then uses a video diffusion model conditioned on the first ego frame, text, and the predicted HOI masks. Experiments on Ego-Exo4D and H2O show improvements over prior video prediction models. Scores: 8, 6, 6. While one reviewer rated the paper higher (8) for strong novelty and thorough evaluation, the others (6) raised concerns about marginal gains and insufficient baselines.

### Strengths

- Well-written and clearly organized; all contributions are presented with detailed component descriptions.
- Introduces high-level novelty: using cross-view HOI mask prediction to guide a video diffusion model for ego-centric video generation.
- Thorough experimental evaluation with a detailed ablation study demonstrating the impact of cross-view HOI masks (e.g., quantitative improvements in SSIM, FVD). 
- Fully automated pipeline for generating Ego-Exo HOI masks using vision foundation models (EgoHOS, SAM2) reduces reliance on manual annotations and increases scalability.
- Strong zero-shot transfer capabilities on the H2O dataset, showing performance on unseen actions and environments.

### Weaknesses

- **Marginal improvement vs. overhead**: SSIM improvement over ConsistI2V is small (0.532 vs. 0.537). ConsistI2V only requires the first ego frame and text, while X-Gen also needs the entire exo video and cross-view HOI prediction. This raises questions about the method’s novelty and practical adoption.
- **Missing baselines**: Several baselines are not compared:  
  - Ego-only HOI mask prediction (using off-the-shelf detectors like EgoHOS+SAM2 or training a separate model).  
  - Exo-ego video prediction without exo HOI masks.  
  - Prior cross-view transformation modules (e.g., [a]) for the HOI mask prediction component.  
  - Directly using the first ego frame (or its HOI mask) as the prediction.  
  - Using only the first-frame HOI mask as the diffusion condition (metrics for this not reported).  
  - Justification is lacking that HOI masks are superior to object masks from the existing cross-view relation benchmark, even though it lacks hand annotations.
- **Limited temporal dynamics**: Videos in Ego-Exo4D average only 1 second (L268). It is unclear how much motion dynamics the model learns beyond the initial frame. No baseline uses the first ego frame as the entire output.
- **Missing implementation details**:  
  - Training/inference time, number of trainable parameters, and compute resources are not reported.  
  - The annealing schedule for `alpha` (L175) is not described, and no ablation shows its importance.  
  - Inference pipeline is underspecified: what are the exact inputs? How does Eq. (2) change? Are predicted ego frames fed back to the first component?  
  - Details of the temporal attention blocks in the video diffusion model are missing.  
  - The phrase “hand-object masks extracted from the future video frames” in Tables 2 and 3 is ambiguous – are these ground truth HOI masks?
- **Applicability concern**: The method requires a synchronized exo video corresponding to the first ego frame. Performance with non-corresponding inputs (e.g., random ego frame pairing) is untested, limiting use cases.
- **No failure case analysis**: Specific instances where X-Gen fails to generate accurate frames are not discussed.
