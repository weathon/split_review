## Merged Review

### Summary
GeoDream disentangles 2D and 3D priors for text-to-3D generation, using a multi-view diffusion model to generate posed images, constructing a cost volume as a 3D geometric prior, then refining via SDS loss on a texture decoder. The method is motivated to resolve the Janus problem and produce high-resolution (1024×1024) outputs. Reviewer opinions are split: one reviewer (score 6) finds the results good and the representation novel, while the other three (scores 3,3,3) consider the design incremental, outdated compared to CVPR 2024/ECCV 2024 works, and limited by quality and evaluation issues.

### Strengths
1. The disentangled handling of 2D and 3D priors effectively addresses the Janus problem, leading to improved 3D consistency. [R1, R3]
2. Supports rendering at 1024×1024 resolution, surpassing most SDS-based methods. [R1, R2, R3]
3. Demonstrates robustness across a wide range of prompts and asymmetric structures. [R1, R3]
4. Cost volume constructed from multi-view images acts as a native 3D prior, enhancing multi-view consistency. [R4]
5. Refinement of geometric priors via the 2D diffusion model improves both rendering quality and geometric accuracy. [R4]
6. Ablation studies validate the effectiveness of proposed components. [R1, R3]
7. The method combines NeuS and DMTet as a 3D representation. [R2]
8. One reviewer found results to be good overall. [R2]
9. Comparisons with baselines highlight improvements in 3D-consistent textured outputs. [R4]

### Weaknesses
1. **Outdated comparisons**: The paper only compares with works from 2023, missing recent CVPR 2024 and ECCV 2024 works (e.g., One-2-3-45++, RichDreamer). [R1, R3, R4] These newer methods may have better visual results. [R1, R3, R4] A comparison with RichDreamer (CVPR 2024), which uses a normal-depth diffusion model as a geometry prior, would be valuable. [R4]
2. **Rationale for second-phase SDS loss unclear**: The paper uses SDS loss to optimize the texture decoder after the multi-view stage. If the multi-view diffusion model performs well, the SDS loss seems unnecessary, as evidenced by recent papers. It is unclear whether the multi-view model in this paper fails to generate satisfactory results, making the second phase required. [R1, R3]
3. **Cost volume limitations**: 
   - (a) The cost volume fuses multi-view features; if input images have occlusion relationships, it cannot obtain a correct feature volume. [R1, R3]
   - (b) The spatial complexity is large, limiting resolution. [R1, R3]
4. **Overall design is incremental**: The method shows technical improvements over 2023 works but seems outdated compared to CVPR 2024 and ECCV 2024 contributions. [R1, R3] One reviewer specifically noted the design appears incremental relative to One-2-3-45. [R4]
5. **Generation quality concerns**: 
   - Artifacts are visible on extracted meshes. [R2]
   - Even in the teaser Fig. 1, there are artifacts around object boundaries and inconsistencies in geometry (mismatched normal maps and images). [R4]
   - Supplementary video examples (EAGLE, MUSHROOM, GIRAFFE) exhibit incomplete geometry, likely due to cost volume limitations. [R4]
6. **Multi-view diffusion model is underexplained**: The paper should present more generation results from this core model and clarify how it improves upon MVDream. The essence of 3D generation is learning a stronger 3D prior; improvements in 3D representation or training methods may not bring fundamental enhancements. [R1, R3]
7. **Training of 3D priors is unclear**: The paper does not describe in detail how the 3D prior model (multi-view generation and cost-volume construction) is trained, raising questions about generalizability and robustness for diverse prompts. Tested prompts appear narrow. [R4]
8. **Evaluation weaknesses**: 
   - User study had only 20–30 participants, likely too small for reliable comparisons. [R4]
   - Ablation study lacks quantitative metrics to substantiate design choices. [R4]
9. **Long optimization time**: Approximately 8 hours per instance, limiting practicality for broader applications. [R4]
10. **Minor issues**:
    - Table 2 has a formatting issue; unclear template used. [R1, R3]
    - Figure 3 shows generated mesh as rendered RGB rather than pure mesh, limiting ability to assess mesh quality directly. [R1, R3]
    - Comparisons in videos with baseline methods would be beneficial. [R2]
