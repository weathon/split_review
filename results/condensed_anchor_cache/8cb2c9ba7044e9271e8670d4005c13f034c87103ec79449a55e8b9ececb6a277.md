## Merged Review

### Summary
The paper proposes α-OCC, an uncertainty-aware camera-based 3D semantic occupancy prediction (OCC) method. It introduces an uncertainty propagation framework (Depth-UP) that predicts depth values and pixel-wise uncertainty, then accumulates uncertainty into 3D voxels to improve geometry and semantic predictions. A hierarchical conformal prediction (HCP) method quantifies OCC uncertainty under class imbalance, and a KL-based score function boosts recall of safety-critical classes. Experiments on SemanticKITTI and KITTI360 with VoxFormer and OccFormer show improvements in geometry completion (up to 11.58%), semantic segmentation (up to 12.95%), safety-critical recall (45% improvement with 3.4% performance reduction), and smaller prediction set sizes (up to 92% reduction).

### Strengths
- Uncertainty propagation for geometric completion is interesting and straightforward; accumulating uncertainty from pixel rays into each 3D voxel is well-motivated and experimentally effective.
- HCP is specifically designed for varied and safety-critical classes at both geometric and semantic levels; the KL-based score function enforces higher prediction confidence for those classes during training.
- Depth-UP and HCP both show significant absolute improvements: up to 11.58% geometry completion, 12.95% semantic segmentation, 45% recall improvement for safety-critical classes, and up to 92% reduction in prediction set size while maintaining coverage.
- Extensive experiments on two OCC models (VoxFormer, OccFormer) and two datasets (SemanticKITTI, KITTI360) validate effectiveness.
- The paper is well-structured, clearly identifying key challenges (depth estimation uncertainty, class imbalance) and outlining corresponding solutions. Writing is clear with detailed explanations of background and proposed method.
- This work makes the first attempt to propose an uncertainty propagation framework (Depth-UP) to improve OCC performance.
- The focus on rare but safety-critical classes (pedestrians, bicyclists) is important for real-world autonomous vehicle safety.

### Weaknesses
- The paper does not provide an explicit illustration of what uncertainty means in occupancy prediction, why it is important, and how it specifically benefits the task. A conceptual explanation is missing.
- Comparisons against state-of-the-art methods on SemanticKITTI and KITTI360 are absent. The paper should discuss or compare with recent SOTA OCC approaches.
- Experiments are conducted only on KITTI datasets (SemanticKITTI, KITTI360). More recent camera-based OCC methods evaluate on nuScenes occupancy dataset; performance on nuScenes or multi-camera setups (e.g., Waymo) would be more compelling and persuasive.
- The relationship between Depth-UP and HCP modules appears loose; they seem relatively independent. The paper should clarify how they connect and depend on each other.
- Change in the number of parameters after adding each module (Depth-UP, HCP) is not provided in the ablation study. This should be reported.
- Evaluation of how the estimated uncertainty varies with distance is missing. Does uncertainty increase with distance as expected?
- The uncertainty of the final predicted semantic occupancy is not evaluated with ECE-related metrics as in methods like PaSCo. Such calibration analysis would strengthen the uncertainty quantification claims.
- Table 4 shows that on SemanticKITTI, mIoU for "person" decreases slightly and "bicyclist" also declines a bit, even though recall for those classes improves. The authors should explain the relationship between higher recall and decreasing mIoU (e.g., trade-off due to higher false positives?).
- The value of alpha (in α-OCC) is not explained; how was it determined in experiments, and how should it be chosen in practical applications? This is an essential hyperparameter for conformal prediction.
- In Figure 2, the semantic prediction pipeline appears to take depth prediction directly as input, which seems odd. Clarification is needed on how depth uncertainty is integrated semantically.
- The ground truth in the third row of Figure 4 appears incorrect (the entire middle row is filled with cars). This raises concerns about validity or labeling.
- The depth model is retrained to regress both depth values and uncertainties (L209). The paper should report whether joint training improves the depth prediction itself relative to the original depth model.
- In experiments, it is unclear whether the base models (VoxFormer, OccFormer) use the original depth prediction network or the newly trained network with the uncertainty branch removed. This detail affects interpretability of improvements.
- Minor errors: L051 "(1 + beta%)" should be "(1 + beta)"; L356-357 in Table 1, the value 55.78 (+2.34) should likely be 55.78 (+1.60) (typo).
- The wording "Hierarchical" in HCP – does it refer to first distinguishing occupied vs. free and then classifying the specific category? This should be clarified.
