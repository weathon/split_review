## Merged Review

### Summary
The reviewers generally agree the paper addresses an important problem (deep imbalanced regression) and propose a two-level decomposition into global group classification and local instance regression with symmetric and asymmetric soft labeling. The writing is clear and the code is open-sourced. However, significant concerns are raised about novelty, theoretical justification, completeness of ablation, and methodological clarity. One reviewer (R1, score 3, confidence 5) expressed much stronger skepticism—questioning the validity of core claims and rating soundness as 1—while the other three (scores 5, confidences 3–4) were more positive but still identified substantial missing analyses, incremental contributions, and underspecified details.

### Strengths
- Addresses an important problem: label imbalance in regression.
- Proposes a novel two-level approach decomposing DIR into global cross-group classification and local within-group regression, with an end-to-end solution.
- Leverages semantic similarity via symmetric and asymmetric soft labeling and label distribution smoothing (LDS), marking an innovative shift from direct classification regularizers in feature space.
- Well-structured, logically clear, and accessible writing.
- Open-sourced code, supporting research transparency.
- Experimental results and ablation studies (on selected components) demonstrate effectiveness of the proposed soft labeling strategy.
- Clear problem definition and adequate introduction to related work.

### Weaknesses
- **Lack of theoretical justification**: No theoretical proof or analysis to support empirical findings; soundness is not well justified (R1: soundness 1; R4: requires theoretical analysis).
- **Invalid claim**: The statement that semantic similarity is “always overlooked” (lines 60–62) is incorrect, as prior works model continuity in feature spaces (R1 cites [1] Delving into DIR, [2] RankSim, [3] ConR).
- **Incremental contribution**: Only proposes two soft labeling strategies and reuses existing techniques (label distribution smoothing [1] and group contrastive representation learning [2]) (R2, R4). The symmetric descending soft labeling is interesting but not sufficient to demonstrate full novelty (R2).
- **Unclear motivation and mechanism**:
  - Description of deep imbalanced regression is too simple; visual examples needed (R4).
  - How/why distorting geometrical structure affects performance is not clarified (R4).
  - How semantic similarity directly addresses label imbalance is not well justified; soft labeling may introduce noise without explicit noise handling (R4).
  - How does asymmetric soft labeling leverage “knowledge from the whole dataset” when Eq. 3 only uses group statistics? (R3)
  - How does semantic similarity \(\hat{p}(y)\) assist in imbalanced regression? Is there a relation to Daso? (R3)
- **Missing ablation and experiments**:
  - Ablation studies incomplete: need ablation excluding classification term, excluding contrastive learning, and necessity of group classifier (R1, R3).
  - No ablation on group label generation process; how are group labels assigned to each class within a group? (R3)
  - Missing comparison to [4] Group-aware Contrastive Regression (R1).
  - Missing advanced baselines on IMDB-WIKI-DIR and STS-B-DIR datasets (R2).
  - Need explanation of peaks in Fig. 3(b)(c) when group number = 40 (R3).
  - What is the experimental setting of cross-entropy? (R4)
  - Why not train feature encoder and multiple regressor heads jointly (as in ConR [1])? (R4)
  - What is performance of features without normalization? (R3)
- **Methodological and writing issues**:
  - How is \(g_{\text{hard}}\) selected? (R1)
  - How is LDS in Sec. 3.3 different from [1]? (R1)
  - How to ensure generation of high-quality soft labels? (R2)
  - Missing hyperparameter details: \(\beta\) in soft label, batch size for contrastive learning, temperature coefficient not shown in equation (R2).
  - Does group classifier operate independently from instance regressor? Group label generation process not shown (R3).
  - Final training objective needs clarification (R3).
  - Missing definition of sign \(k\) in Eq. 5 (R3).
  - Line 247: missing part of \(p(g|)\) (R3).
  - Line 324: space missing after ‘/’ (R3).
  - Missing definition of \(c(\psi)\) (R4).
  - “accurate estimating” should be “accurate estimation” (R4).
  - Figures are unclear; legend in Fig. 1 requires clearer color interpretation (R3, R4).
- **Scope limitation**: Extension to multi-dimensional label spaces not investigated or experimented (R1).
- **Training suboptimality**: Two-stage training (feature encoder then multiple regressor heads) may lead to suboptimal model; direct joint training may be better (R4).
