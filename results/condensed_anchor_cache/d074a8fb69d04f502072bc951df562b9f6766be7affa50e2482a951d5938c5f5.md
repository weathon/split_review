## Merged Review

### Summary
The paper proposes TIPS, a text-image pretraining method that enhances spatial awareness for both dense and global vision tasks. It combines contrastive image-text learning with self-supervised masked image modeling, and uses a dual-embedding approach to leverage both noisy web captions and synthetically generated captions. The model is evaluated on 8 tasks and 16 datasets. Reviewer 2 is notably more positive (rating 8) than others (ratings 6,6,6), praising the approach as effective and the experiments as comprehensive, while other reviewers find the technical contributions limited and the improvements incremental.

### Strengths
- **General**: The paper is well-written and well-structured, with detailed experimental records (Reviewers 3, 4). Extensive experiments on dense prediction tasks (16 datasets) demonstrate strong off-the-shelf performance, outperforming DINOv2 on several tasks (Reviewer 3). The model shows good generalizability and broad applicability across visual tasks (Reviewer 4).
- **Approach**: The dual-embedding technique that enhances utility of both synthetic and noisy web captions is effective (Reviewers 2, 3). Combining contrastive image-text learning with self-distillation and masked image modeling (simultaneously) encourages spatial coherence (Reviewer 2). Combining DINOv2 and CLIP is intuitive and promising for improving spatial awareness (Reviewer 3). The large-scale synthetic caption dataset creation is a solid contribution (Reviewer 1). Ablation studies help develop new text-image models (Reviewer 1). The core idea of using synthetic captions to enhance spatial awareness, though not entirely novel, shows improvements (Reviewer 4).
- **Results**: Good simultaneous performance on dense prediction and classification/retrieval (Reviewer 1). Ablation results are provided (Reviewer 1, 4).

### Weaknesses
- **Novelty and impact**: The technical contributions are limited. The general idea of combining contrastive image-text learning with masked image modeling (MIM) is not new, as prior work (e.g., EVA-CLIP) has already shown MIM improves spatial awareness/locality of CLIP features (Reviewers 1, 3). This paper lacks a thorough discussion and comparison against such works (Reviewer 3). The only novel contribution is the dual-embedding technique; the rest is a combination of existing methods (Reviewer 3). The improvements over CLIP and DINOv2 are incremental and sometimes marginal or context-specific; the method's performance plateaus quickly and does not suggest a paradigm shift (Reviewer 4).
- **Missing comparisons and analyses**: 
  - It is unclear if improvements come from better data (proprietary WebLI dataset) rather than method. A fair comparison on public datasets (LAION, COYO, DataComp) is needed (Reviewer 1).
  - The paper should compare simultaneous vs. successive combination of MIM and CLIP, analyzing pros and cons (e.g., training cost, stability) (Reviewer 1).
  - Only I→T and T→I retrieval are evaluated; the model's fine-grained spatial awareness under multimodal settings is not sufficiently tested. Open-vocabulary segmentation experiments would be beneficial (Reviewer 3).
  - Insufficient analysis of how the two paradigms (SSL and image-text) interact; e.g., how SSL design choices like mask ratio affect image-text representation learning (Reviewer 3).
  - The dual-embedding idea needs more empirical analysis: what are the different roles of the two [CLS] embeddings? Visualization of attention maps to see their focus areas would help (Reviewer 3).
  - Ablation studies are insufficient to isolate contributions: how much gain comes from synthetic captions vs. masked image modeling? Is spatial coherence of captions the primary driver? More detailed ablations are needed (Reviewer 4). Alternative ways of introducing spatial awareness beyond synthetic captions and masking should be discussed (Reviewer 4).
- **Practical concerns**: The proprietary WebLI dataset raises reproducibility concerns. Will the pretrained model and curation dataset with synthetic captions be released? (Reviewer 2).
- **Writing**: Formatting needs improvement – empty spaces around Fig1 and Fig2 (Reviewer 2).
