## Merged Review

### Summary
The paper proposes Mol-StrucTok, a method to tokenize 3D molecular structures for language models. It converts molecules to sequences using SMILES/SELFIES for 2D topology and spherical coordinates for 3D geometry, then applies a VQ-VAE to discretize the coordinates. A GPT-2 model is trained for 3D molecular generation. Experiments on QM9 show competitive performance on validity, uniqueness, PoseBusters, and quantum property prediction; additional analysis covers inference speed and temperature effects. The method is also used with Graphormer for property prediction, yielding improvements.

### Strengths
- **Performance**: Achieves strong results, especially on conditional generation tasks (Reviewer 1); state-of-the-art across most benchmarks including validity+uniqueness with different bond assignment, PoseBusters tests, quantum mechanical properties, and MAE for QM9 property prediction (Reviewer 3).
- **Extensive experiments**: Includes thorough ablation studies (Reviewer 1), additional analysis on inference speed and generation temperature’s effect on quality vs. diversity (Reviewer 3).
- **Novel tokenization approach**: The combination of spherical line notation with vector quantization enables language models to process 3D data while preserving SE(3)-invariance; augmented tokens with generation and understanding descriptors (bond lengths, angles) capture molecular topology and spatial arrangements effectively (Reviewer 2).
- **Clarity and presentation**: Paper is well-written and easy to follow, with clear and informative tables and figures (Reviewers 1, 4). Sufficient theoretic analysis and proofs are provided (Reviewer 4). Significance for leveraging LLMs in 3D molecule modeling is noted (Reviewer 4).

### Weaknesses
- **Limited novelty**: The core ideas (spherical coordinates for SE(3)-invariance, VQ-VAE tokenization) are similar to existing methods such as FoldSeek and FoldToken, which use distances/angles from reference nodes and VQ-VAE (Reviewer 1).
- **Insufficient baselines and comparisons**:
  - Only evaluated on QM9 (small molecules); should be tested on larger molecules like GEOM-Drug (Reviewer 1).
  - Missing key LM-based baselines: Geo2Seq (also uses spherical coordinate tokenization, with higher reported stability) and BindGPT (Reviewers 1, 4).
  - Missing comparisons with other tokenization schemes (e.g., BPE-based tokenizers) and diffusion/MPNN-based methods; unclear whether gains are from spherical coordinates or the specific tokenization method (Reviewer 3).
  - Should compare with structures expressed in other coordinate systems to justify choice of spherical coordinates and analyze impact on sequence complexity (Reviewer 3).
- **Methodological details unclear or unjustified**:
  - Rationale for choosing exactly four neighbors per atom; how atoms with fewer than four neighbors are handled is not specified, raising concerns about generality for molecules with varying coordination environments (Reviewer 2, Major).
  - How the order of atom tokens (Equation 2) is determined—by BFS or DFS traversal on the 2D graph? (Reviewer 4)
  - Notation inconsistency: \(\mathbf{x}_i\) in line 266 vs. \(\mathbf{g}_i\) in Equation 7; sign of torsion angle \(\text{sign}(\varphi_i)\) appears in generation descriptor (Eq. 7) but not in combined descriptor (Eq. 9) without explanation (Reviewer 4).
  - Necessity of local atomic environment (lines 266–267); negative impact of removing it not discussed or ablated (Reviewer 4).
  - Advantage of VQ-VAE over simpler discretization (e.g., binning floating numbers into fixed intervals) needs ablation and justification (Reviewer 4).
  - Motivation for purely MLP-based VQ-VAE architecture vs. GNN or sequence-based; tradeoffs between element-wise discretization and message propagation not discussed (Reviewer 3).
- **Minor presentation issues**:
  - Notations are inconsistent across sections (e.g., \(\mathcal{M}\) used only in Section 3.1; Appendix C notation differs) (Reviewer 2).
  - Placeholder references (“Section X”, “Figure X”) in lines 248 and 268; ambiguous phrase “Inspired by previous works” in line 239 (Reviewer 2).
  - Line 52: SE(3) invariance excludes reflection—should be clarified (Reviewer 1).
  - Model size not reported (Reviewer 1).
  - Line 177 phrasing should be reworded (Reviewer 2).
- **Questions overlapping with criticisms**:
  - Should include PoseBusters energy ratio test, which is more challenging than the tests in Table 2 (Reviewer 3).
  - Lack of analysis on learned structural alphabet—e.g., which local structures map to the same code (like FoldSeek’s Figure S2/S3) (Reviewer 1).
