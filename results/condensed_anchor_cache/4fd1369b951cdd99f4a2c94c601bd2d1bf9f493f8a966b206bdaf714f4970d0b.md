## Merged Review

### Summary

The paper proposes SPACETGN, a continuous-time dynamic graph (CTDG) learning framework with an augmented hard negative sampling mini-batch (AMNS) strategy and two feature extraction methods (spatial-temporal subgraph and historical occurrence). Three reviewers rated the paper 3 (weak reject/neutral) and one reviewer rated it 5 (weak accept). The majority found the research question important and the experimental results promising, but all four reviewers raised serious concerns about clarity, writing quality, missing analyses, and inadequate presentation, with the minority reviewer (score 5) also noting significant limitations.

### Strengths

- The paper addresses an important and underexplored problem: improving negative sampling for training in temporal link prediction. (Reviewers 1, 2, 3, 4)
- The AMNS strategy is original and differs from existing random, historic, or independent negative sampling methods. (Reviewers 2, 3, 4)
- Experimental results show the proposed negative sampling strategy (and SPACETGN) leads to significant improvements over standard random negative sampling across multiple baselines and datasets. (Reviewers 1, 2, 3, 4)
- The negative sampling approach was also tested in combination with existing baselines (not only in SPACETGN), demonstrating its general applicability. (Reviewers 1, 4)
- The evaluation was conducted on seven real-world datasets and includes MRR and AP/AUC metrics. (Reviewer 4 partially positive, though noting AP saturation)

### Weaknesses

- **Lack of clarity and poor writing quality.** The paper is very difficult to follow and contains numerous typos, grammatical errors, and incomplete sentences. Specific examples:
  - Many notations and concepts are unclearly defined (e.g., “hard negative criteria”, “positive/historical/random candidates”, “rating function” (Eq. 1) including λ, “soft negative samples”, “information discrimination”). (R1, R3)
  - The example in line 211–213 mentions t₁₁ which does not appear in Fig. 2, and the sentence appears incomplete. (R1, R2, R3)
  - Fig. 3 caption is missing the method name: “Framework of the proposed _____”. (R1, R2, R3)
  - Incorrect statement: line 186 claims that TGB (Huang et al. 2024) uses “predetermined random negative samples for each training epoch”, which is false (TGB only fixes evaluation negatives). (R4)
  - Unclear sentences in Section 5.1: “We optimized all Adam models” (likely means Adam optimizer), “We train the models 100 times over time” (unclear if 100 seeds or 100 epochs), “We run these methods with 0 to 4 seeds five times and report the average performance to eliminate bias” (contradictory range of seeds). (R1, R4)
  - The text mentions “accuracy” when Table 3 shows average precision (AP). (R1)
  - Missing space before “Detailed” in line 292. (R3)
  - “space-temporal” should be “spatial-temporal”. (R4)
  - Fig. 5 axes have different scales and the x-axis is not labeled. (R1, R2) The density can appear >1.0.
  - Table 3 columns are too close together, making it hard to read. (R1, R2)
  - Line 152 incorrectly states that the link prediction task itself is about representations h_u and h_v (temporal link prediction existed before graph representation learning). (R3)
  - Fig. 4 and Fig. 5 have a layout issue with a single isolated line of text (line 387) that could be mistaken for a caption. (R2)
  - Citation formatting inconsistent (whitespace before some \cite). (R2)
  - “a augmented” should be “an augmented” (line 18). (R3)

- **Missing key architectural details in the main paper.** Essential explanations of the SPACETGN architecture (components (c) Encoding Features and (d) Mixing Features) are only found in the appendix, which is optional for readers. The main text merely references “Detailed descriptions of SPACETGN are available in Section A.” (line 293). The bullet points in lines 262–285 only describe (b) Extracting Key Sequences. (R3)

- **Insufficient statistical evidence for performance claims.** The paper claims “significant performance improvement” without any statistical significance tests (e.g., confidence intervals, p-values) across multiple runs. (R1)

- **Abstract overstates contributions on model bias.** The abstract mentions “ameliorating model bias” but the paper does not define, analyze, or discuss model bias. (R1)

- **Missing discussion of false negatives in the negative sampling.** Since real-world dynamic graphs are incomplete, a “hard negative” edge could actually be a positive edge that has not been observed yet (false negative). The paper does not justify how the method avoids sampling truly positive edges. (R1)

- **Cold-start problem for the rating function.** The rating function (Eq. 1) depends on learned node embeddings, which may not be reliable early in training. This could cause a cold-start: a minimum number of observations may be needed before hard negatives can be generated. (R1)

- **Missing analysis of computational efficiency.** The AMNS strategy adds significant overhead compared to random sampling, but the paper provides no theoretical complexity (O(·) notation) or experimental runtime comparisons. (R3, R4) The trade-off between training time and performance improvement is not addressed. (R1: also questions truncation method for neighbor/sequence lengths)

- **Missing state-of-the-art baselines.** Recent relevant methods such as FreeDyG (discussed in the paper but not evaluated) and CTAN (Gravina et al., ICML 2024) are not included in the comparison. (R4)

- **Limited evaluation on TGB datasets.** The experiments only use the smallest TGB benchmark (tgbl-review is missing; tgbl-coin is also omitted). The AP metric is highly saturated (near-perfect) on several datasets (e.g., Social Evo). (R4)

- **Code is not available.** Only a few hyperparameter settings are provided in Appendix F, insufficient for reproducibility. (R3)

- **Question regarding the validity of hard negatives over time.** Q1: If node pair (u,c) is a hard negative at time t₁₁, but they were connected earlier, might they be a positive pair at a later time t₁₂? (R2) – this relates to the false-negative issue above (R1).
