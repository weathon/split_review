## Merged Review

### Summary
The paper proposes Vector-Quantized Continual Diffuser (VQ-CD) for continual offline RL, combining Quantized Spaces Alignment (QSA) via VQ-VAE to unify different state/action spaces and Selective Weights Activation (SWA) via task-specific masks in a diffusion model. Evaluated on 15 continual learning tasks (including conventional and general settings with different observation/action spaces) against 16 baselines, achieving SOTA performance.

### Strengths
- Novel combination of VQ-VAE to learn a unified state/action space, enabling joint training of diffusion-based RL on spaces of different dimensionality (Reviewers 1, 3).
- SWA effectively addresses catastrophic forgetting by isolating task-specific weights; masks can be merged after training (Reviewers 1, 3).
- Comprehensive evaluation: thorough baselines, diverse environments (Ant-dir, CW10, D4RL), ablation studies confirm importance of combining VQ with CD (Reviewers 1, 2, 3).
- Ablation and parameter importance investigation are valuable (Reviewer 4).
- Strong empirical evidence with SOTA results on both conventional and varied space tasks (Reviewer 3).
- The idea of aligning perceptual and action spaces in a common representation is worthy of attention (Reviewer 4).

### Weaknesses
- **Lack of crucial algorithmic details**:  
  - How is the “return” used? Is it return or Q-function? How is target return determined during inference? (Reviewer 2)  
  - What role does the inverse dynamics model ψ play? How are the diffusion model and inverse dynamics model used to generate actions? (Reviewers 3, 4)  
  - Input/action space dimensions and how DDPM is applied to non-image data (e.g., actions) are missing (Reviewer 4).  
  - “State sequences” and τ_s^{0:K} are unclear (Reviewer 4).  
  - “Tailored state sequences to facilitate training” is imprecise (Reviewer 4).  
  - w in line 193 is undefined (Reviewer 3).  
  - What are “nonsignificant masks” (line 352)? (Reviewer 3)  
- **Continual learning validity**: QSA pretraining processes all tasks’ data jointly (Alg.1), which is not continual—it looks into the future. If all data is available, why use CL at all? (Reviewer 4)  
- **Reliance on task identifiers and boundaries**: Method assumes explicit task boundaries, limiting use in task-free scenarios. Task-aware mask selection prevents task-agnostic usage (Reviewers 1, 3).  
- **Missing baseline**: L2M [ref] constructs unified state/action spaces and incorporates task matching to train separate weights (similar to QSA+SWA) but is not compared. L2M could also serve as a simpler ablation for VQ (Reviewer 3).  
- **Scalability and complexity**:  
  - Training scales linearly with number of tasks (separate model checkpoints with different masked weights) (Reviewer 3).  
  - Combination of VQ-VAE, diffusion, and masking introduces multiple layers of complexity; computationally expensive, hyperparameter sensitive (Reviewer 1).  
  - Not clear how method scales once masks are filled; why not train separate DDPM per task? (Reviewer 4)  
- **Presentation and clarity**:  
  - Figure 1 is cluttered, lacks data flow; labeling could help (Reviewers 2, 4). Algorithm (in appendix) should be in main text or figure improved (Reviewer 4).  
  - Figure 2 is never referenced in text (Reviewer 3).  
  - Figure 4 needs visible task boundaries (Reviewer 3).  
  - Figure 7 labels are unclear—explicit elaboration needed (Reviewer 3).  
  - Title overclaims (“solving”); “any CL task sequence settings” is ill-defined (Reviewer 3).  
  - Language is often imprecise or slightly incorrect (Reviewer 4).  
- **Implementation reproducibility**: No information on how SOTA baselines are implemented (parameter settings, libraries) (Reviewer 4).  
- **Missing limitations discussion**: No mention of linear scaling with tasks, reliance on task info, or inability to detect non-periodic task changes (Reviewers 1, 3).  
- **Core techniques not fundamentally new**: VQ-VAE, diffusion, task masks are established; integration is novel but paper does not clearly demonstrate a departure (Reviewer 1).  
- **Additional unaddressed questions**:  
  - Why is the Gaussian constraint in eq. (2) important? Why clipping instead of loss? (Reviewer 3)  
  - How does reconstruction look for VAE-CD in Table 2? Only VQ-CD and AE-CD shown (Reviewer 3).  
  - Why does multitask baseline performance decrease with VQ spaces (Fig. 11)? (Reviewer 3)  
  - What is the data source for CW10 and MuJoCo Ant-dir? (Reviewer 3)  
  - How can VQ-CD be better than Multitask on two D4RL tasks? (Reviewer 3)  
  - Can masks be visualized to reveal task similarities? (Reviewer 1)  
  - Benefit of a single controller for different state/action spaces not clearly justified (Reviewer 4).  
- **Minority reviewer (3) is much more positive (score 8)** than the other three (scores 5, 3, 3), but still raised several missing baselines, limitations, and presentation issues.
