## Merged Review

### Summary
This paper proposes two ways to incorporate depth signals (from a pretrained monocular depth estimator DPT) into self-supervised learning (SSL): (1) RGB+depth input to the encoder, and (2) generating novel 3D views as an augmentation. Evaluations on BYOL, SimSiam, and SwAV using ImageNette, ImageNet-100, and ImageNet-1k claim improved robustness and generalization. **Reviewer disagreement**: Reviewers 1 and 2 (both rating 3) find the experiments insufficient in scale and novelty limited; Reviewer 3 (rating 5) appreciates the biological motivation and clarity but also notes serious weaknesses.

### Strengths
- Novel combination of depth-as-input and 3D-view augmentation within SSL, aiming to learn geometry-aware representations.
- Biologically inspired motivation with a well-written introduction correlating to biological vision (Reviewer 3).
- Interesting reasoning: mutual information between depth and image is high, so depth alone has limited effect on large datasets, but novel 3D views introduce genuinely new information (Reviewer 3).
- Simple, clear, and reproducible method with explicit ablation studies (Reviewer 3).
- Empirical evidence that adding depth signals improves existing SSL methods (BYOL, SimSiam, SwAV) over baseline in several classification settings (Reviewers 1, 2; but Reviewer 3 notes some results are absent or contradictory).

### Weaknesses
- **Insufficient experimental scope** (Reviewers 1, 2, 3):
  - Only small-scale datasets: ImageNette, ImageNet-100, ImageNet-1k. Missing results on larger or more diverse datasets.
  - Only small models (ResNet-18, ResNet-50); no Transformer or larger architectures.
  - Only linear-evaluation classification; no downstream tasks like 2D/3D detection, segmentation, reconstruction, human pose estimation (Reviewer 1).
  - On ImageNet-100, the proposed method shows no improvement (actually worse) than baselines, and the combination (depth+3D) results are missing (Reviewer 3).
- **Reliance on a supervised depth estimator** (Reviewers 2, 3):
  - DPT is pretrained on multiple datasets with ground-truth depth, so the overall pipeline is not fully self-supervised (Reviewers 2, 3).
  - Generalizability is questionable: depth estimation may fail on domains far from its training distribution (Reviewer 2).
  - Robustness gains on ImageNet-C and ImageNet-3DCC may reflect the depth network’s robustness rather than the SSL method (Reviewer 3).
- **Limited novelty** (Reviewer 2, partially Reviewer 3):
  - Using depth as augmentation in SSL has been done before (e.g., DepthContrast) (Reviewer 2).
  - Most improvement comes from simply adding the depth channel as input, which is considered a minor contribution (Reviewer 3).
- **Unfair comparison and missing details** (Reviewer 3):
  - Depth is dropped from the input at test time to avoid over-reliance, yet depth is used to compute error metrics. Previous methods had no depth input, making comparisons potentially biased.
  - Table 1 claims SwAV improvements, but no SwAV results are actually shown (Reviewer 3).
  - Figure 1 lacks details: “2D augmentation” is unclear, and the SSL objective is not visually represented (Reviewer 3).
- **Clarity issues** (Reviewers 1, 3):
  - Scope is too general; the method would be better evaluated on RGB-D specific downstream tasks (Reviewer 1).
  - Minor typos (e.g., "a approach", "an conceptually") (Reviewer 3).
  - Casual language (e.g., “we take seriously two insights”) could be improved (Reviewer 3 question).
  - Metrics comparing against SOTA are missing from the introduction (Reviewer 3 question).
