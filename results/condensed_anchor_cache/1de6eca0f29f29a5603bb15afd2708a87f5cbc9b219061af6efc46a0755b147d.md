## Merged Review

### Summary
This paper proposes Distillation for In-Context Planning (DICP), a model-based in-context RL framework where the Transformer simultaneously learns environment dynamics and improves policy in-context. DICP uses a learned dynamics model for planning to deviate from the source algorithm's suboptimal behavior. Experiments on Darkroom (discrete) and Meta-World (continuous) show state-of-the-art performance with fewer environmental interactions compared to model-free in-context and meta-RL baselines.

### Strengths
- **Clear writing and thorough experiments**: The paper is clearly written and provides comprehensive experiments and ablations demonstrating DICP's effectiveness against model-free counterparts (Reviewer 1).
- **Novel integration of model-based planning into in-context RL**: Simultaneously learning dynamics and policy in-context is a new and important innovation; as far as the reviewers know, this is the first demonstration of incorporating model-based planning into transformer-based in-context RL (Reviewer 3).
- **Mechanism to overcome suboptimal source behavior**: DICP provides a mechanism to avoid the suboptimal behaviors inherited from source RL algorithms (Reviewers 2, 3).
- **State-of-the-art performance on Meta-World**: DICP achieves SOTA on Meta-World compared with a large variety of RL and in-context RL algorithms (Reviewer 3).
- **Straightforward but impactful contribution**: The paper is well-done with a straightforward contribution that advances the performance of in-context RL (Reviewer 3).
- **Sample efficiency gains**: DICP yields improvements in both sample efficiency and overall score (Reviewer 3).

### Weaknesses
- **Limited empirical support for main motivation**: The claimed advantage of model-based planning ("deviation from source algorithm behavior") is not strongly supported by the results. In Appendix B, DICP-AD improves over AD only from 68% to 69% success rate, and DICP-IDT improves from 75% to 80% – modest gains that do not convincingly demonstrate the benefit of planning (Reviewer 1). The core argument is not convincingly demonstrated by these reported improvements (Reviewer 1). *Contrast: Reviewer 3 finds no major weaknesses and views the contribution as impactful despite these numbers.*
- **Lack of discussion on model errors**: A key flaw of model-based planning is that a learned world model can be imperfect, leading to suboptimal policies. This issue is not discussed in the paper (Reviewer 2). Questions: What happens when the world model is incorrect? How does this affect performance? What steps are taken to ensure model-based planning can be accurate? (Reviewers 2, 3).
- **Missing analysis of suboptimal behavior correction**: No analysis is provided on how many times the source algorithm exhibited suboptimal behavior and whether DICP successfully learned the optimal policy in those cases. This analysis is needed to justify the extra computation of model-based planning (Reviewer 2).
- **No trade-off analysis (performance vs. computation)**: The paper does not discuss the computational cost of DICP (e.g., beam search planning tree) versus baseline methods, nor whether the performance gains outweigh the additional computation (Reviewer 2).
- **Unclear action sampling and sensitivity**: The paper does not explain exactly how actions are sampled during planning or how sensitive results are to the sampling approach (Reviewer 3).
- **No ablation on separate dynamics model**: It is unclear what would happen if the imitation loss and dynamics loss trained two separate models instead of a single Transformer (Reviewer 3).
- **Limited online learning discussion**: The paper lacks discussion of how DICP might extend to online learning, e.g., interaction with intrinsically-rewarded exploration, dependence on offline dataset quality, or adaptation to changing dynamics (Reviewer 3).
- **No comparison with MCTS**: The paper uses beam search for planning; it is unclear whether MCTS would offer benefits or whether the entire planning tree must be built upfront (Reviewer 3).
- **Worse performance on Pick-Out-Of-Hole**: DICP performs worse on the Pick-Out-Of-Hole task in Meta-World; no explanation is provided (Reviewer 3).
- **Limited benchmark difficulty**: The evaluations are on Darkroom variants and Meta-World; evaluation on more challenging benchmarks like ML10 is missing (Reviewer 1).
