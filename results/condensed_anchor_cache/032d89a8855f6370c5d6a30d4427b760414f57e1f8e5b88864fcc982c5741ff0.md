## Merged Review

### Summary
The paper proposes KG4D, a framework for generating 4D representations from a single image or video, using Harmonic Spatio-temporal Encoding (HSE), Keypoint Feature Calibration (KFC), and Wasserstein Gradient Flow (WGF) to decouple spatial/temporal dimensions and enhance consistency. The idea of using keypoint-guided Gaussians and separate spatial-temporal generation is considered reasonable and interesting. However, the paper suffers from severe writing and formatting flaws, missing citations, unclear equations, insufficient experiments, and unconvincing evaluation. While one reviewer (score 5) found the visual results impressive and the contributions significant, two other reviewers (scores 3, 3) deemed the paper unacademic, rushed, and filled with errors; the most negative reviewer considered it “totally generated by an AI” and incompetent as an academic paper.

### Strengths
1. The idea of endowing Gaussians with keypoint features and incorporating regularization into generation is interesting (R1, R2, R3).
2. The emphasis on decoupling spatial and temporal dimensions, along with keypoint guidance, is a significant contribution (R2).
3. The visualization results are impressive and effectively demonstrate the method’s capabilities (R2).
4. Generating spatial and temporal dimensions separately and using keypoints to avoid inconsistencies seems helpful and reasonable (R3).

### Weaknesses
1. **Severe writing and formatting issues**:
   - Missing/invalid citations: no citation in lines 91–107 (R3); invalid citation in line 147 (R2); missing citation in line 260 (R2, R3); no correct reference for 3D Gaussian Splatting (R1).
   - Equations hard to follow: equation in line 257 difficult to understand (R2); “z” in equation line 243 not defined (R3); symbols $K^{ref},P^{ref},I^{ref}$ in line 262 not defined (R3); what does “C” in line 259 mean? Does it represent J? (R2).
   - Vague descriptions: section 4.3 is vague, especially lines 324–341 (R2); general lack of clear meaning, motivation and solutions lack precise meaning (R1).
2. **Insufficient methodological detail**:
   - How are spatial and temporal components decoupled? A detailed description is missing (R2).
   - Will the design of KFC introduce a large number of parameters ($R^{N,J}$)? Could a self-supervised approach (like SC-GS) be better? (R2)
   - The section on motion capturing via Wasserstein gradient flow needs more explanation to clarify the meaning of optimal transport (R2).
   - The paper mentions ground truth keypoints $P^{sup}$ in line 215, but the DG4D dataset does not contain keypoint ground truth. How are these keypoints obtained – manually labelled? (R3)
   - Why are different sets of keypoints used for the spatial and temporal generation stages? (R3)
3. **Inadequate experimental evaluation**:
   - Ablation studies are insufficient: the specific role of spatial-temporal decoupling and visualization of KFC need better explanation (R2).
   - The abstract mentions “evaluation on various benchmarks” but only one dataset is evaluated (R3).
   - The experiment section mentions comparisons with various SOTA methods (lines 403–404, 420–421), but only a few are evaluated (R3).
   - Dataset is not clearly stated in table titles; numbers in “ours” row between Table 1 and Table 2 do not match (probably different datasets, but not mentioned) (R3).
   - Section 5.4 contains no result; all tables and image results are not referred to or discussed (R3).
   - The authors did not provide video results (R2).
4. **Minority extreme criticism**: One reviewer (R1) states the manuscript is “full filled with nonsense sounds totally generated by an AI” and “lacks precise and clear meaning, making it incompetent for an academic paper.” This stark contrast with the other reviewers’ more moderate assessments is explicitly noted.
