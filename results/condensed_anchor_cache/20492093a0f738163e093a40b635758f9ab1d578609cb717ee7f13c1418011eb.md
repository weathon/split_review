## Merged Review

### Summary
Dice-GAN is a single-stage text-to-image GAN incorporating two modules: a Diversity Injection (DI) module that injects learnable noise multiple times during generation, fuses it with text features, and uses self-attention to maintain global structure; and a Consistency Enhancement (CE) module that combines word vectors with a hybrid (channel and spatial) attention mechanism to dynamically weight image regions. The model is evaluated on CUB and MS-COCO.

### Strengths
- The idea of adding learnable noise at different stages of generation and correcting with self-attention to improve diversity is novel and interesting (R1). The DI and CE modules mark a significant advancement in text-to-image synthesis (R3).
- Improved quantitative performance: higher IS and FID on CUB, and better FID on MS-COCO (R1). The method achieves state-of-the-art performance (R3) and is superior to other GAN-based methods (R4).
- Ablation study confirms that adding DI and CE modules improves results (R1).
- Enhanced diversity: noise and text vector injection multiple times yields a broad range of outputs without sacrificing structural consistency (R2).
- Improved semantic consistency: the CE module dynamically focuses on image regions, aligning visuals closely with text descriptions (R2).
- Clear and well-organized presentation (R4).

### Weaknesses
- **Limited novelty:** The feature fusion operation in Eq. 1 of the DI module has been explored before (R1). The core idea of adding noise to improve diversity is not entirely novel (R4). Condition-dependent masking (as in the CE module) also has limited novelty (R1).
- **Lack of clarity in writing and figures:** Section 3.2 and Fig. 4 are unclear. The motivation behind the conditional channel attention mask (M_c) and spatial attention mask (M_s) is not explained; why both average and max channels are used is unclear. Quantities G_max and G_avg are missing from Fig. 4, hampering understanding of the pipeline (R1).
- **Missing comparisons:** No comparison with multi-stage methods such as StackGAN++ (R1). Only one diffusion model (ShiftDDPMs) is compared; recent diffusion-based models should be included (R2, R4). There is insufficient discussion of how other generative models (flow-based, StyleGAN) could be adapted or combined with Dice-GAN (R4).
- **Missing ablation studies:** Why are two feature fusion layers needed in the DI module, and how was that hyperparameter determined? How does the learnable noise σ vary from lower to higher layers in the trained model? The CE module’s design choices—using both average and max features, and conditional channel attention versus spatial attention—are not ablated (R1).
- **Lower IS on MS-COCO:** The authors claim the drop is because IS uses an ImageNet‑pretrained Inception model, but they should provide results on ImageNet or an ImageNet subset to back this claim (R1).
- **Insufficient experimental analysis:** The paper lacks detailed table/figure captions (R2). Qualitative results are too limited (R2). There is a need for performance on more diverse datasets and additional metrics such as CLIP score (R2). Overall, there is insufficient experimental analysis demonstrating the effectiveness of the proposed model structure (R2).
- **Text complexity analysis missing:** No quantitative analysis of performance (IS, FID) across different levels of text complexity (simple vs. nuanced descriptions); only qualitative examples are given (R3).
- **Computational efficiency not analyzed:** Training time, inference time, and memory requirements are not reported, nor are they compared with existing methods (R3).
- **Lack of attribute‑level evaluation:** The model’s ability to handle specific textual attributes (color, size, object positioning) is not evaluated; quantitative accuracy of these attributes in generated images is not assessed (R3).
- **Unanswered questions that overlap with missing analysis:** (i) How does Dice‑GAN perform under different noise levels? (ii) What measures ensure that the DI module does not excessively degrade visual quality due to noise injection? (iii) Does the CE module have limitations in maintaining semantic consistency for longer, more detailed text descriptions? (R3).
