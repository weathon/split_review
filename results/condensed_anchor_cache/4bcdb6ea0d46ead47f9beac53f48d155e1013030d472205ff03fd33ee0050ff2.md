## Merged Review

### Summary
The authors introduce DriveE2E, a closed-loop benchmark using digital twins of 15 real-world intersections (with 800 traffic scenarios from a 100-hour video dataset) embedded in CARLA for end-to-end autonomous driving evaluation. The paper evaluates several SOTA E2E models. All reviewers acknowledge the engineering effort but raise significant concerns about novelty, validation, data/code availability, and closed-loop realism. Reviewer 2 was notably more positive (score 5) than the others (scores 3 each). Reviewer 2 emphasizes timeliness and clarity, while other reviewers find the contributions limited and the technical merits insufficient.

### Strengths
- Combining digital twin with CARLA is a promising approach to reducing the sim-to-real gap for end-to-end autonomous driving.
- Closed-loop simulation and evaluation is a timely topic in the AV community; the paper correctly identifies many difficulties with current simulators and benchmarks.
- Bringing real-world traffic scenarios into simulation enables a more realistic evaluation scheme than prior closed-loop approaches.
- The paper is written clearly and the core ideas are easy to follow.
- A comprehensive closed-loop simulation pipeline has been developed; solid engineering effort is evident.
- Evaluation of several state-of-the-art end-to-end models (including UniAD, VAD, and others) within the simulator demonstrates its capabilities and validates its effectiveness.
- The exploration of the correlation between open-loop metrics and closed-loop performance is insightful and adds depth to the discussion.
- The extra digital towns and traffic scenes may have some use for the community (Reviewer 4).
- Reviewer 2 specifically notes that a lot of work went into development and the broader community will certainly be able to leverage it.

### Weaknesses
- **No data or code samples were provided for review**, and no plans for open-source release were mentioned. This is critical for a dataset/benchmark paper; without them, contributions are difficult to evaluate (Reviewer 2 primary reason for rejection-leaning; Reviewer 4 would maintain reject without data/code).
- **The closed-loop nature is questionable.** It is unclear whether traffic agents are purely replayed from logs or can react to the ego vehicle. If interaction relies on CARLA's traffic manager, realism concerns arise. The paper does not clarify agent interactivity, and no validation of agent behavior realism is provided (Reviewers 2, 3, 4).
- **Lack of novelty:** The concept of building digital twins from real-world data is well-established. Existing works (MetaDrive with larger dataset and learning-based traffic control, Interaction dataset/simulator, CARLA's own Town15 digital twin) have implemented similar approaches. Crucially missing are comparisons and discussions on how DriveE2E advances beyond these (Reviewers 1, 3, 4).
- **No quantitative evidence of sim-to-real gap reduction** or advantages over CARLA's default environments (e.g., Town12, Town13). The paper lacks validation experiments (e.g., statistical analysis, metrics showing enhanced realism) comparing against existing simulators or demonstrating value over default CARLA towns (Reviewers 1, 3).
- **Missing experiments:**
  - No evaluation with diffusion-based end-to-end methods (already in literature for waypoint generation) (Reviewer 1).
  - No comparison with RL-based expert data collection like Roach (CARLA-based) (Reviewer 1).
  - Unanswered question: does UniAD have closed-loop results now? (Reviewer 4).
  - No analysis of what improvements or insights the benchmark provides beyond reporting success rate and driving score numbers (Reviewer 1).
- **The visual quality of the digital twins is low** compared to latest CARLA towns (e.g., Town12, Town13) in material diversity and map size. The technical contribution of creating digital twins is limited, as CARLA already provides similar functionality (Reviewer 4).
- **Figures 3 and 4 are not referenced in the text**, indicating incomplete presentation (Reviewer 1).
- **The paper mentions a city name**, violating double-blind review requirements (Reviewer 1).
- **Ethics concerns:** Privacy requirements for real-world driving data (blurring license plates and faces) were not addressed (Reviewer 2).
- **Venue fit:** The work may be more suited for a robotics/AV conference or technical report accompanying code release than for ICLR (Reviewer 2).
- **Simulator not tailored for end-to-end driving:** The design appears generic rather than specifically catering to E2E model evaluation needs (Reviewer 3).
- **Overall, three reviewers (1, 3, 4) find the contribution limited**, while Reviewer 2 would raise their score if data samples are provided. Reviewer 4 states that "barely describing curation of digital twins from some real-world data in CARLA, especially rather naive ones compared to latest CARLA, has enough contributions."
