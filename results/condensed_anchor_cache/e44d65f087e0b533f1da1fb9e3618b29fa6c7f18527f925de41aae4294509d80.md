## Merged Review

### Summary
The paper proposes an extension of the Symbolic Pattern Forest (SPF) algorithm for time-series clustering that automatically determines the number of clusters without prior specification. The method uses SAX to transform time series, constructs bag-of-words or TF-IDF vectors, and selects the clustering that maximizes the Silhouette Coefficient. Experimental results on multiple UCR datasets are reported. Reviewers disagree on contribution and rigor: two assign a score of 3, one a score of 1.

### Strengths
- **S1.** Timely and important problem, especially relevant for IoT and unsupervised data exploration.
- **S2.** Simple and intuitive ideas; combining BoW/TF-IDF with SPF is a sensible approach.
- **S3.** Results support the overall claims in the paper.
- **S4.** Empirical evaluation uses a large number of benchmark datasets.
- **S5.** The paper is well-written and provides an extensive overview of the relevant formulas.
- **S6.** The problem of automatically finding the number of clusters is significant; SPF’s low complexity makes it well-founded, and introducing k-estimation mitigates a known weakness of SPF.

### Weaknesses
- **W1. Novelty overclaimed and prior work omitted.** The abstract states the method is the first time-series clustering approach that does not require a priori cluster number, but several existing methods perform k-estimation for time series (e.g., Spectral Clustering for Time Series [Wang & Zhang 2005], HMM+DTW [Oates et al. 1999], Trendlets [Johnpaul C I et al. 2020] which also uses Silhouette). These should be discussed in related work or used as competitors. The combination of SAX, TF-IDF, and Silhouette lacks a theoretical framework justifying its effectiveness over alternative combinations or individual methods. (R1 W1, R3 W1)
- **W2. Silhouette Coefficient choice unjustified and misaligned with SPF.** The paper does not justify why the Silhouette Coefficient is selected over other internal metrics (e.g., Davies-Bouldin index, Calinski-Harabasz index). Moreover, the Silhouette uses Euclidean distance to cluster centers, which is not aligned with the clustering objective of SPF. This is a major concern for one reviewer (R2 W1). Other internal validation metrics should also be considered (R3 W3). The impact of different distance metrics within the TF-IDF framework is not discussed (R1 W2).
- **W3. Missing important baselines.** The evaluation does not include SPF itself as a baseline, only weak baselines constructed from the proposed method. Simple baselines (e.g., optimizing k-means-like objectives) are omitted. Existing k-estimation methods for time series (see W1) are not compared. The set of performance metrics (accuracy, near-miss-rate) differs from the SPF paper (which uses NMI), making direct comparison with prior work impossible. Specific numeric values for all metrics should be in the main text, not only in supplementary material. Statistical significance testing is missing. (R1 W3, R2 W2, R2 W3, R3 W2)
- **W4. Parameters and reproducibility not addressed.** The ranges of parameter \(w\) and \(\alpha\) are not specified; code is unavailable, hampering reproducibility. An analysis of parameter sensitivity and stability is missing; intuition behind chosen parameters should be given. (R3 W3, R3 W6)
- **W5. Failure analysis lacking.** The paper does not analyze under what conditions k-estimation fails for each variant (BoW, TF-IDF). The “Relative Improvement” section is redundant and could be replaced with a deeper result analysis, including specific datasets where the method performs poorly and the reasons. (R3 W4)
- **W6. Incomplete and out-of-context related work.** The related work discussion is overly brief and fails to include important references (e.g., Keogh et al. 2005, Rakthanmanon et al. 2012, Paparrizos et al. 2015). References UTSAD and STGAT do not address time-series clustering and appear out of context. (R2 W4, R2 W5)
- **W7. Redundant content.** The description of SAX is redundant. (R2 W6)
- **W8. Reference and formatting errors.** Duplicate references: Li et al. 2019 listed as both 2019a and 2019b; Yang and Leskovec 2011 duplicated. The reference Paparrizos et al. 2022 is incorrect. Missing citations for BoW and TF-IDF; UCI should be cited on first mention outside the abstract. Formatting issues: lists not comma-separated (lines 291, 314-315), variables not separated (line 305), broken subscripts in equations (22), (23). The near-miss metric formulation incorrectly includes correctly determined cluster counts. (R1 W4, R2 W7, R3 W7, R3 minor)
- **Reviewer disagreement.** One reviewer (score 1) was significantly more critical, viewing the method as lacking novelty, the Silhouette choice as fundamentally misaligned, the baselines as insufficient, and the reproducibility as unaddressed. The other two reviewers (score 3) acknowledged potential but agreed on the major weaknesses listed above.
