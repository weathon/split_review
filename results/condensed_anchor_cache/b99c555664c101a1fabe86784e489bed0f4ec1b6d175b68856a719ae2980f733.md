## Merged Review

### Summary

MV-Adapter is a plug-and-play adapter for multi-view consistent image generation, built on the core idea of T2I-Adapter. It adds a condition guider (processing camera pose or geometry information) and decoupled attention (duplicated self-attention layers and parallel attention architecture) to pre-trained T2I models (SD2.1, SDXL) without modifying the original network. It supports multiple conditioning inputs (text, image, geometry) and can be integrated with derivatives like ControlNet, generating up to 768-resolution 6 views. Experiments show promising visual fidelity and view consistency. However, most reviewers (4 out of 5 gave scores of 6, one gave a 5) considered the technical contributions limited, as the core components are largely borrowed from existing methods (T2I-Adapter, Era3D, Zero123++). One reviewer noted no major weaknesses, while others highlighted significant limitations, including fixed 6-view output, modest quantitative gains, and missing evaluations. One reviewer initially leaned toward weak rejection but said after rebuttal they would recommend borderline acceptance.

### Strengths

- Well-written, clearly organized, and easy to follow (Reviewers 2, 3, 5).
- Straightforward and intuitive motivation; explores various design choices and makes progress in all examined aspects (Reviewer 2).
- Efficient training and reduced computational costs by updating fewer parameters; no full fine-tuning required (Reviewers 1, 4).
- Seamlessly integrates with various derivatives and extensions of the base T2I model, such as personalized models and plugins like ControlNets (Reviewers 1, 3).
- Supports diverse conditioning inputs: text, images, geometry, and camera pose guidance, broadening applications (Reviewers 1, 3, 4).
- Comprehensive experiments on multiple base networks: SD2.1, SDXL, and fine-tuned models derived from them (Reviewer 3).
- Solid qualitative results with visually pleasing outputs and improvement over existing methods (Reviewers 2, 5).
- Quantitative evaluation demonstrates competitive performance compared to baselines (Reviewer 2).

### Weaknesses

- **Limited novelty**: The core components are heavily borrowed from prior works, making the technical contribution thin. The condition guider is directly inspired by T2I-Adapter, and the decoupled attention borrows from Era3D. The image cross-attention mechanism is already employed in Zero123++ without explicit comparison to the proposed variant. One reviewer characterized the paper as an extension of T2I-Adapter with incremental modifications, lacking significant new insights (Reviewers 2, 4, 5). Another reviewer found the main contribution is essentially the application of self-attention, and requested clearer differentiation from existing self-attention methods in multi-view generation (Reviewer 1).

- **Fixed 6-view output**: The model generates only a fixed set of 6 views, which is restrictive compared to video-diffusion models like Emu-Video (used in im-3d and vfusion3d, 16 views), SV3D (20 views), and Cat3D (which supports arbitrary view synthesis). The inability to synthesize novel views from arbitrary angles limits applications requiring comprehensive 3D understanding (Reviewer 2).

- **Modest quantitative improvements**: The gains over baselines on the GSO image-to-multi-view task are modest. The authors should provide more compelling evidence, such as a user study or side-by-side video comparisons hosted online, to demonstrate the real impact (Reviewer 2).

- **Missing 3D reconstruction evaluation**: The paper does not evaluate downstream 3D reconstruction using the generated multi-views, which is the most direct metric for multi-view consistency. Standard metrics such as volumetric IoU or Chamfer distance should be reported to validate the quality of the outputs (Reviewer 4).

- **Missing ablation study on parallel vs. serial architectures**: The paper proposes a parallel attention architecture but does not compare it with a serial architecture in terms of generation quality, multi-view consistency, and computational efficiency. Such an ablation would strengthen the paper (Reviewer 1).

- **Lack of detail on the T2I-UNet for image cross-attention**: It is unclear whether the UNet used for image cross-attention is identical to the diffusion UNet and what timestep is used for feature extraction (Reviewer 3).

- **No discussion on combining MV-Adapter with a learned T2I-Adapter**: Given the structural similarity to T2I-Adapter, it would be useful to know whether the MV-Adapter can be combined with a separately trained T2I-Adapter in a manner similar to ControlNet integration (Reviewer 3).

- **CFG calculation with multiple conditions not explained**: The classifier-free guidance (CFG) computation at inference time for this multi-condition setup is not specified (Reviewer 3).

- **Text caption source unclear**: The paper does not clarify whether the training text captions come directly from Cap3D or were separately annotated (Reviewer 4).

- **Potential for full-model fine-tuning**: It is unknown how the model would perform if all parameters were fine-tuned while keeping the proposed architectural design. Showing that comparable results can be achieved without full fine-tuning would better validate the design’s effectiveness (Reviewer 4).

- **Reviewer disagreement**: Reviewer 3 stated no major weaknesses and gave higher soundness (4 vs. 2–3) and contribution (3 vs. 2). Reviewer 5 rated the paper 5 (lowest) and considered it publishable but not at a top conference like ICLR. Reviewer 2 initially leaned toward weak rejection (score ~4) but said after rebuttal they would recommend borderline acceptance.
