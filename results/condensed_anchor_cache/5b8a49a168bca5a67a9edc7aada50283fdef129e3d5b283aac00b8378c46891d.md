## Merged Review

### Summary
This paper proposes UOT-UPC, an unpaired point cloud completion method using unbalanced optimal transport (UOT) to map incomplete point clouds to complete ones. It formulates completion as an OT problem and applies UOT to handle class imbalance between the two sets. The InfoCD cost function is identified as particularly well-suited. Experiments on the dataset from Ma et al. 2023 show competitive or superior performance in single- and multi-category settings, especially under class imbalance.

### Strengths
1. **Novelty**: First application of unbalanced optimal transport to unpaired point cloud completion (Reviewers 1,2).
2. **Robustness to class imbalance**: UOT-UPC maintains consistent performance across various imbalance ratios, outperforming USSPA and OT-UPC (Reviewer 2).
3. **Better performance**: Achieves better results on both single- and multi-category settings on the dataset proposed by Ma et al. 2023 (Reviewers 1,2,3).
4. **Clear and carefully designed pipeline**: The paper is easy to follow and the pipeline is well-thought-out (Reviewer 1; *minority positive view – other reviewers note clarity issues (e.g., source mixture trick not well-explained)*).
5. **Cost function analysis**: InfoCD is identified as well-suited for unpaired completion; ablation studies compare it with l2, L2-Chamfer, and one-directional L2-Chamfer distances (Reviewer 2).

### Weaknesses
1. **Limited dataset evaluation**: Experiments are primarily on one dataset (Ma et al. 2023 / Scan2CAD). Missing evaluation on PCN, Completion3D, MatterPort3D, KITTI, ModelNet, 3D-FUTURE, etc. Generalizability to varying point cloud density, categories, and missing data types is not demonstrated (Reviewers 1,2,3).
2. **Missing comparisons to important SOTAs**: The paper omits comparisons with ASFM-Net, 3D Shape Generation and Completion Through Point-Voxel Diffusion, ACL-SPC, and CloudMix. The advantages over diffusion-based methods are not discussed (Reviewers 1,3).
3. **Cost function exploration insufficient**: Only four cost functions are compared; claiming the “optimal” cost function is not justified. Other cost functions (e.g., learned feature spaces, adaptive weighting) could yield better performance. The study relies primarily on ShapeNet for this analysis (Reviewer 2).
4. **OT assumption validity not discussed**: The optimal transport map assumes that the set of incomplete and complete point clouds forms a complete set. In the Scan2CAD data (partial scans from ScanNet aligned with ShapeNet models), this assumption may be violated. No discussion or validation is provided (Reviewer 3).
5. **Computational cost not analyzed**: Training and inference times are not reported or compared with other methods, which is critical for practical applications (Reviewer 1).
6. **Code not provided**: Absence of code hinders reproducibility and further building upon the method (Reviewer 1).
7. **Source mixture trick not well-explained**: More explanation and insights are needed to understand this component (Reviewer 2).
8. **Performance drop in some categories**: The method shows comparable or lower performance than USSPA on certain categories; reasons are not explained (Reviewer 1, question).
9. **No experiments on unseen categories**: It is unclear whether the model can complete categories not seen during training (Reviewer 1, question).
10. **Metric choice not justified**: The paper does not discuss whether Chamfer Distance or F-Score are the best metrics for 3D shape completion; alternative metrics could be considered (Reviewer 1, question).
11. **Limited exploration of class imbalance diversity**: Only a few object categories are tested for imbalance effects; more varied and challenging imbalance scenarios are needed to fully assess robustness (Reviewer 1).
