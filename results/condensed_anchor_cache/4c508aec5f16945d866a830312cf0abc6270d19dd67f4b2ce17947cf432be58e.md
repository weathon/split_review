## Merged Review

### Summary
VideoGuide is a training‑free framework that improves the temporal consistency of pretrained text‑to‑video diffusion models by interpolating the denoised samples (predicted ) from a guiding model (any pretrained VDM or itself) into the sampling model’s denoising process during early inference stages. The method combines multiple DDIM steps with CFG++ and a low‑pass filter, and is validated on models such as AnimateDiff, LaVie, and VideoCrafter2.

### Strengths
- The idea of mixing two  is interesting and effective (R2), and the method is easy to implement (R3).
- VideoGuide combines existing diffusion models with a novel interpolation technique, leveraging the strengths of different models (e.g., temporal consistency of VideoCrafter2, personalization of AnimateDiff) without additional training (R1).
- Extensive experimental validation on multiple state‑of‑the‑art T2V models (VideoCrafter, AnimateDiff, LaVie), with thorough quantitative metrics (subject consistency, background consistency, imaging quality, motion smoothness) and qualitative user studies (R1). The results show significant improvements in temporal consistency and overall video quality (R1, R4).
- The method outperforms FreeInit in both speed and quality (R2).
- The framework is versatile and can be applied to various base models, enabling underperforming models to benefit from superior models (R4).
- Interesting idea of making low‑performing models generate good results or accelerating high‑performing models (R3).
- The paper reads well and is easy to follow (R2). (Note: R3 reports some writing issues; see Weaknesses.)

### Weaknesses
- **Lack of theoretical justification**: Why the interpolation method improves temporal consistency is not clearly explained; the method may appear ad‑hoc. (R1, high confidence; R3 also asks about the necessity of the low‑pass filter and why CFG++ is used instead of CFG.)
- **Missing visualizations**: The paper lacks plots of sampling trajectories, which would provide an intuitive understanding of how interpolation affects temporal consistency. (R1)
- **Insufficient comparison to prior work**: The relationship and differences with previous approaches (e.g., FreeInit, UniCtrl, I4VGen) are not adequately discussed. The improvement over FreeInit is not significant (R1, R4). Missing discussion of necessary related works (UniCtrl, I4VGen) (R4).
- **Missing modern DiT‑based models**: Experiments should include DiT‑based models such as OpenSora (R2, R3). This would strengthen the claim of general applicability.
- **Self‑guidance saturation**: For the self‑guidance scenario, it is unclear whether VideoCrafter2 shows similar improvement as AnimateDiff and LaVie; the benefit might saturate with a better video foundation model (R2).
- **Cross‑model guidance justification**: When using a different model as guidance, why not directly use the guidance model? Other than personalization, the paper mentions super‑resolution and interpolation models of LaVie, but they could also be used with other T2V foundation models. The claim that “any pretrained VDM can be used as guidance” needs more comprehensive argumentation; currently only VideoCrafter2 is tested, and there may be necessary constraints on the type of VDM (R2, R4).
- **Missing evaluation metrics**: The paper should report temporal consistency and dynamic degree metrics from VBench, as improving temporal consistency may compromise motion degree. A balance between these is important (R2, R4).
- **Evaluation‑correlation concern**: The used quantitative metrics may not correlate well with true video generation performance; human evaluations (already performed) are better (R3). (Note: R1 mentions user studies, but R3 questions the metric correlation.)
- **Writing issues**: Variable “I” is used without introduction in the experiments section (R3: “we set I = 5, β = 0.5, and τ = 10”). (R2 finds the paper readable overall.)
- **Missing details**: Ablation results on the frequency filtering component are not reported (R2). The inference time of the baseline model is missing from Table 2 (R4).
- **Limited novelty**: The proposed method is essentially a combination of multiple denoising‑diffusion iterations and high‑low frequency decoupling, consistent with FreeInit; the paper does not provide sufficient contributions or new insights (R4). (R2 and R3 find the idea interesting, so there is reviewer disagreement on novelty.)
- **Specific questions not addressed experimentally**: What happens if CFG is used instead of CFG++? (R3); can the guidance model have a different VAE from the sampling model? (R2).
