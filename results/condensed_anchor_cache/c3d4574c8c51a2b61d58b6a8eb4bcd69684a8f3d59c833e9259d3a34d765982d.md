## Merged Review

### Summary
The paper proposes TimeAutoDiff, a latent diffusion model combining VAE and DDPM for heterogeneous time series data generation. It encodes continuous and discrete features into a continuous latent space via an RNN encoder, then models the latent codes with DDPM using cyclic encoding and a bidirectional RNN denoising network. Conditional generation on time-varying metadata is supported. Evaluation on unconditional and conditional generation tasks across multiple datasets claims superiority over existing approaches. Reviews diverge strongly: one reviewer (score 1, confidence 1) finds the work lacking in originality, soundness, clarity, and experimental validity; two reviewers (scores 3, confidence 2 and 4) note good presentation and comprehensive evaluation but raise concerns about novelty, practical tasks, and scaling; one reviewer (score 5, confidence 3) sees the method as timely and clear but notes missing comparisons and design details.

### Strengths
- **Clear writing and structure** (R1, R4): The paper is well-written, well-structured, and easy to follow. Technical details are discussed in detail, and the method seems reproducible (R1).
- **Comprehensive evaluation** (R3, R4): Experiments cover unconditional and conditional generation tasks, considering both lower-order and higher-order statistics. Hyper-parameter variations and ablations reveal the impact of different components. The proposed approach shows superior performance over existing approaches across datasets (R3, R4).
- **Timely problem** (R1): Synthetic time series generation is critical for foundation model development, as mentioned in the discussion. The methodological contribution is novel in combining VAE and DDPM specifically for heterogeneous time series tabular data.

### Weaknesses
- **Lack of comparison to key prior diffusion-based models** (R1): Missing comparisons to Diffusion-ts, TimeDiff, and TSGM (including metrics like consistency and diversity). One reviewer (score 1) also notes that citing unpublished/prior venue rejections as SOTA (e.g., TSGM in line 156) is unconvincing.
- **Limited novelty and methodological originality** (R2, R3): DDPM/VAE for time series is not new; most techniques are from prior works. A similar latent diffusion framework for tabular data has been explored (R3). The motivation in lines 144–154 (VAE as 1-step DDPM) is unconvincing: why DDPM is good for time series and why VAE adds value remain unclear (R2).
- **Insufficient discussion of limitations and fairness** (R1): Potential bias in conditional generation with respect to conditional metadata is not addressed.
- **Design choices insufficiently justified** (R1): Details on the decoder and ε_θ architectures are lacking; the impact of alternative architectures on performance is not explored. Appendix J does not provide the promised details on decoder architecture selection.
- **Clarity issues** (R2): Captions of Figures 2 and 3 provide zero information. Many notations (e.g., x_cont in line 192) are introduced without definition. The description of the sampling time column (“lower numbers indicating faster sampling”) needs expansion (R1). The line about MSE/BCE/CE (line 249) appears disconnected from context (R1).
- **Textual metadata example unclear** (R1): Lines 171–173 suggest the model generates data for textual metadata, but this is not demonstrated; if unsupported, the example should be removed.
- **Experimental evaluation concerns** (R2, R3, R4):
  - Most experiments are on regression datasets; it is unclear how these validate the heterogeneous “tabular” time series property (R2).
  - Section 4.2 is titled “Utility Guarantees” but provides no theoretical analysis (R2).
  - Lacks summary statistics for generated data (e.g., for nasdaq100: volatility, moving averages). A generated example from the trained nasdaq100 model should be provided (R2).
  - Majority of experiments are limited to unconditional generation; it is unclear how the model adapts to practical tasks like forecasting and imputation (R3).
  - The model is primarily tailored for continuous data; its effectiveness on heterogeneous datasets with categorical features is questionable (R4).
  - Performance drops when feature sizes increase, requiring latent space reduction. This reliance on dimensionality reduction raises scalability and robustness concerns (R4).
- **Missing rationale for RNN encoder** (R3): It is unclear why an RNN encoder is needed for capturing temporal dependencies when the denoising network itself could capture them.
- **Lack of interpretability** (R4): The model cannot generate interpretable results, which is critical in high-stakes fields like finance and healthcare.
- **Lack of practical significance discussion** (R3): The value of unconditional generation and time-varying metadata conditional generation tasks is not sufficiently motivated.
- **Soundness** (R2): The overall correctness of the experimental evaluation is questioned.
- **Minority reviewer strongly negative** (R2, score 1, confidence 1): This reviewer found no strengths, identified severe originality, soundness, clarity, and significance issues, and concluded the paper is not ready for publication. Other reviewers (scores 3 and 5) were more positive about the presentation and evaluation.
