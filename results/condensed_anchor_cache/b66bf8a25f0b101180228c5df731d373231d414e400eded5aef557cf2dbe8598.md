## Merged Review

### Summary
The paper proposes χPO, a variant of Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) that replaces KL-based regularization with χ²-divergence regularization to combat overoptimization in offline preference alignment. The key change is a minimal modification to DPO’s logarithmic link function. The authors prove that χPO achieves sample-complexity guarantees based on single-policy concentrability (replacing the all-policy concentrability required for DPO) and demonstrate robustness to overoptimization empirically on a summarization task.

### Strengths
- **Novel, simple modification to DPO.** χPO is a one-line change that replaces the logarithmic link function in DPO with a χ²-based link function, making it easy to integrate into existing systems. (R1, R4)
- **Strong theoretical guarantees.** The paper provides sample-complexity bounds under single-policy concentrability (Theorem 3.1), a gold standard in offline RL, and does not require the convex set assumption of DPO+SFT. (R1, R2, R4, R5)
- **Addresses a timely problem.** Overoptimization in offline alignment is an important and active area of research. (R1)
- **Sound and comprehensive analysis.** The theoretical development is rigorous, well-cited, and clearly presented (especially before Section 4). One reviewer (score 8) found the results surprising and appreciated the work. (R2, R3, R4)
- **Provably robust to overoptimization.** The χ² divergence better quantifies uncertainty than KL divergence, leading to theoretically grounded robustness. (R3, R4)

### Weaknesses
- **Limited empirical validation.** Experiments are conducted on only one summarization task and only against standard DPO, not against other common baselines (e.g., DPO+SFT, KPO, IPO). The improvement over DPO is marginal (roughly one point in best results) and χPO still overoptimizes when training epochs increase. Additional benchmarks (e.g., MMLU, GSM8K) and ablations over β are requested. (R1, R3, R4, R5)
  - **Reviewer disagreement:** The reviewer who gave score 8 considered the experiments adequate and found no major weaknesses, whereas the other four reviewers (scores 6) strongly request more comprehensive comparisons.
- **Gap between theoretical algorithm and practical implementation.** The pure χPO algorithm was not used in experiments due to instability; instead a modified link function \(\tilde{\phi}(z)=\exp(\operatorname{clip}_{[-88,20]}(\alpha z))+\gamma\log z\) was used, which is far from the theoretical \(\phi\). (R2, R5) Additionally, the practical algorithm includes a mixed regularization (KL + χ²), but the necessity and role of the KL term, the effect of its weight, and whether it can be discarded remain unexplained. (R2, R3, R4, R5)
- **Overoptimization not fully resolved.** Despite the theoretical claims, χPO still exhibits performance degradation with increased training epochs, and the advantage over DPO is not substantial. (R1)
- **Clarity and presentation issues.**
  - Two different definitions of the estimator \(\widehat{\pi}\) are given (Eq. 7 and Eq. 9), causing confusion; only Eq. 9 is used in analysis and experiments. (R5)
  - Minor errors: Line 131 should be \(x \in \mathcal{X}\); Line 138 should be \(y \sim \mathbb{P}(a \succ b \mid x)\); full name of SFT not given at first appearance; \(\mathcal{C}_{\infty}^{\pi}\) not defined; Eq. (5) unclear on whether \(n\) is the size of \(\mathcal{D}_{\text{off}}\); Eq. (4) yields \(-\infty\) if \(\pi(a_+ \mid x) \ll \pi_{\text{ref}}(a_+ \mid x)\). (R2, R5)
  - Section 4.3 appears to give a looser bound than Theorem 3.1 and may be unnecessary; its contribution should be clarified. (R5) R1 also wanted more elaboration on how pessimism leads to single-policy concentrability.
  - Lower bound inconsistency: In line 507, the lower bound may be \(\frac{\pi^{\star}_{\beta;\text{KL}}(a \mid x)}{\pi_{\text{ref}}(a \mid x)} \gtrsim \exp(-R_{\max}/\beta)\) rather than the claimed value, which would prevent the optimal mixed χ²-regularized policy from simultaneously satisfying two desired properties. (R5)
- **Missing analyses and comparisons.**
  - No comparison with online variants (e.g., PPO-χ², PPO-KL, PPO-Clip) to validate theory-practice alignment. (R3)
  - No plots of reward (win-logps) vs. epochs to track overoptimization trajectory. (R1)
  - The exponential dependence on \(R_{\max}\) in the sample-complexity bound is not addressed empirically or theoretically; its unavoidability is unclear. (R2)
  - The practical implications of using only χ² regularization (without the KL term) are not explored. (R2, R3, R5)
  - Ablations over key hyperparameters such as \(\alpha \in \{\frac14, 1\}\) and \(\gamma \in \{0.1, 1\}\) (as mentioned in Appendix E.2) are suggested. (R5)
