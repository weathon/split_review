## Merged Review

### Summary
The paper studies the minimum width required for deep neural networks with unlimited depth to be universal approximators. It shows that for \(L^p\) approximation of \(L^p\) functions from \([0,1]^{d_x}\) to \(\mathbb{R}^{d_y}\) with ReLU-like activations (e.g., ReLU, GELU, Softplus), the minimum width is exactly \(\max\{d_x, d_y, 2\}\). This improves on previous results for \(\mathbb{R}^{d_x}\) domain, and generalizes the Leaky-ReLU case of Cai (2023) to a broader class. The paper also proves a lower bound \(w_{\min}\ge d_y+1\) for uniform approximation with general activation functions when \(d_x<d_y\le 2d_x\), showing a dichotomy between \(L^p\) and uniform approximations. Two reviewers (scores 8, confidence 5) found the results solid, clear, and valuable, while one reviewer (score 5, confidence 4) considered the contribution incremental and not very significant.

### Strengths
- **Solid theoretical results**: The paper makes a solid contribution to understanding the universal approximation ability of neural networks, with mathematically correct proofs (Reviewers 1,2,3).
- **Clear presentation**: The paper is clearly written, well organized, and easy to follow (Reviewers 1,2).
- **Wide coverage**: Theorem 2 applies to a wide class of ReLU-like activations (e.g., ReLU, GELU, Softplus), not just ReLU, which is a non-trivial generalization (Reviewer 3). Previously only Leaky-ReLU was covered (Cai, 2023). The generality is valuable despite the proof being based on a simple reduction (Reviewer 3).
- **Tight bounds**: The work provides an exact characterization of the minimum width for general \(L^p\) norm and a broad activation class, concluding a line of research that started with Lu et al. (2017) (Reviewer 3).
- **Dichotomy result**: The lower bound for uniform approximation shows a non-trivial separation between \(L^p\) and uniform approximations, extending previous results (Reviewer 1).
- **Fundamental questions**: The results answer fundamental questions about the validity of practical MLP implementations (Reviewer 2).
- **Adequate citations**: Related works are adequately cited (Reviewer 1).

### Weaknesses
- **Incremental contribution (minority view)**: One reviewer (score 5) found the contribution incremental and not very significant. The main result generalizes the known width \(\max\{d_x,d_y,2\}\) from Leaky-ReLU (Cai, 2023) to ReLU-like activations, but the core techniques are mostly based on previous work and do not introduce fundamentally new concepts. The gap between upper and lower bounds is an additive constant, making the improvement minor (Reviewers 1,3). The same reviewer questioned whether this is suitable for top-tier conferences. The other two reviewers (scores 8) acknowledged the incremental nature but still valued the result.
- **Lack of proof detail in Lemma 6**: The proof of Lemma 6 is presented as a sketch/recipe rather than a full proof, relying heavily on another paper’s lemmata. More detail and rigor is needed to make the paper self-contained (Reviewer 2).
- **Missing analysis of domain geometry**: The paper does not discuss how the metric entropy or capacity of the compact domain \(X\) affects the minimum width. For example, if \(X\) is a singleton (\(\{x_0\}\)) with \(d_y=1\), a width smaller than \(\max\{d_x,d_y,2\}\) may suffice, suggesting room for improvement for general \(X\) (Reviewer 2).
- **Missing implications for \(C(\mathbb{R}^{d_X},\mathbb{R}^{d_Y})\)**: The results imply minimal widths for universality in the topology of uniform convergence on compact sets, which could be noted via extension theorems (Tietze/McShane); this is not discussed (Reviewer 2).
- **Potential improvement for general nonlinearities**: The paper does not use recent results showing that most MLPs with continuous activation functions can approximate ReLU networks (Zhang, Lu, Zhao 2023, arXiv:2307.06555). This could sharpen the statement for general activation functions (Reviewer 2).
- **Limited practical scope**: The paper does not study the exact minimum width for more architectures used in practice, limiting immediate applicability (Reviewer 1, also implied by Reviewer 3).
- **Contextualization of separation result**: The separation between whole-space and compact-subset approximation was previously noted in Lu (2011, arXiv:2101.11286); the paper could better acknowledge this intuition (Reviewer 3).
