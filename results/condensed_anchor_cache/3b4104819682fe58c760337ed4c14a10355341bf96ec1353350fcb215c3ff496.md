## Merged Review

### Summary
Reviewers generally found the paper's use of bisimulation metrics for task similarity in meta-RL to be novel and interesting, with sound experimental results on sparse reward MuJoCo and panda-gym tasks, including OOD generalization. One reviewer (score 8) was more enthusiastic, praising the formulation, theoretical validation, and strong empirical results. The other three reviewers (score 6) shared many concerns, including clarity issues, insufficient motivation for key design choices, lack of ablations, outdated baselines, weak proof writing, and reproducibility concerns. There is disagreement on clarity: the higher-scoring reviewer found the method well-presented, while others found the method section difficult to follow and notationally inconsistent.

### Strengths
- Novel use of bisimulation metrics to measure task similarity in meta-RL; interesting and worth exploring (R1, R3, R4).
- Theoretical guarantee for the latent task belief metric (R1, R2).
- Strong empirical performance: outperforms baselines on sparse reward MuJoCo and panda-gym, especially on OOD testing tasks (R1, R4; R3 also notes sound experimental setup and results, though with modest improvements).
- Well-formulated and well-constructed according to some reviewers (R1, R2); however, note clarity disagreement (see Weaknesses).
- Potential to advance community understanding of latent embeddings for task generalization (R2).
- Sparse reward setting is important and timely (R4).

### Weaknesses
- **Clarity and notation issues:** Several notations are confusing or used before definition. Examples: $z_l$ and $z_r$ not clearly explained; the role of latent $h$ in Figure 2 inconsistent with equations (R1, R2). Inverse dynamics and $N$ in Section 2.1 undefined before use (R2). Method Section 3.2 difficult to understand (R3). Figures 4 and 5 hard to interpret (R3). Proofs contain typos and lack detail: e.g., in lines 714-715 value function update should be $V_{n+1} = ... V_n$; lines 719-710 $d^n$ seems to be $d$; lines 733-739 using Lipschitz property without discussion of constant; proof of Theorem 2 poorly written and needs thorough polishing (R4).
- **Motivation and design choices insufficiently justified:** Combining $z_r$ (variational task belief) and $z_l$ (task belief similarity) via a Gaussian mixture does not combine them but samples from one or the other; unclear what $z_l$ adds since $z_r$ already models dynamics from trajectory reconstruction. The task belief similarity $z_l$ is not modeled as a distribution (R3). Key details in Section 3.3 (using Q-function to train offsets, minimizing KL divergence to $z_r$ when predicting $z_l$) are mentioned but not explained; the need for KL divergence suggests $z_l$ alone may be insufficient (R3). The difference between SimBelief and VariBAD (which also infers task beliefs using a world model) is not sufficiently elaborated (R1). The bisimulation metric is originally for deterministic MDPs; applicability to stochastic MDPs is not justified (R1).
- **Related work and baselines outdated:** Baselines are old (2019-2021), e.g., MetaCURE, HyperX; newer methods for sparse reward meta-RL should be discussed and compared (R1, R4). Insufficient discussion of MetaCURE, which tackles the exact problem (R3). Evaluation environments may not be standard; unclear if previous works used them (R4).
- **Experimental evaluation incomplete:** No ablation studies provided (R3). Insufficient discussion of why comparison methods underperform or overperform (R3). No theoretical analysis justifying why the method works in extremely sparse reward settings (e.g., reward only at terminal step) (R4). Results are modest rather than dramatic (R3).
- **Reproducibility concerns:** The framework is complex with many interacting components (context encoder, latent dynamics, bisimulation loss), but no detailed algorithm or pseudocode in the main paper (R2, R3). Hyperparameters and training procedure not thoroughly described (R2). Code not mentioned (R2 asks about release).
- **Theoretical concerns:** Proof of Theorem 1 contains typos and insufficient justification (e.g., Lipschitz constant for value function) (R4). Proof of Theorem 2 poorly written and needs thorough revision (R4). The inverse dynamics in Definition 2 introduced without ablation comparing to standard bisimulation (R3).
- **Minor/other:** Topic of online meta-RL is somewhat old (R1).
