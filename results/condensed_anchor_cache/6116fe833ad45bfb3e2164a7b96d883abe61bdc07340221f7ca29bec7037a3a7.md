## Merged Review

### Summary
This paper presents a broad literature review on LLMs, covering their types (Generative, Masked Language, Sequence-to-Sequence, Hybrid), architectures, applications (text generation, translation, summarization, etc.), limitations, benchmarks, and future directions. The reviewers uniformly find the paper superficial, outdated, and lacking novel contribution.

### Strengths
- Provides an overview of technologies used in LM development (Reviewer 1).
- The topic is important given the rapid development of LLMs (Reviewer 3).
- Summarizes LLM architecture, application areas, limitations, practical usage, and future directions, which could give a quick idea to new LLM researchers, though more suitable as a blog post than a research survey (Reviewer 4).

### Weaknesses
- **No novel or meaningful contribution:** The paper does not offer insights beyond those already in existing surveys (e.g., Bommasani et al. 2021, Zhao et al. 2023) and acknowledges this in Section 3.11 (Reviewer 2, Reviewer 4). It is a straightforward summary lacking critical review (Reviewer 4).
- **Outdated content:** The models discussed (e.g., BERT, GPT, T5) are not considered “large” by contemporary standards (e.g., LLaMA-2, OLMo) (Reviewer 1). The discussion is around typical transformer-based language models without new insights (Reviewer 4). The insights are not valid in the current state of the field (Reviewer 3).
- **Poor organization and superficiality:** Organization, especially Section 3, is very poor; most subsections contain a single paragraph with at most one reference (Reviewer 2). The content is superficial and not a systematic review (Reviewer 3, Reviewer 4). The paper lacks in-depth critical review (Reviewer 4).
- **Missing or wrongly cited references:** Some citations have “?” marks, demonstrating lack of attention to detail and potentially indicating the paper was written without comprehensive research (Reviewer 4). Reviewer 2 also notes missing/wrong citations.
- **Poorly addressed future directions:** The paper claims to explore future directions but does not adequately address them; e.g., does not discuss specific future applications in new fields or emerging challenges (Reviewer 1). The discussed limitations and future directions also offer nothing new (Reviewer 4).
- **Unclear objectives:** The paper’s objectives are not clearly defined; it purports to review LLMs but examines models not considered large by current standards (Reviewer 1, citing Bommasani et al. 2021; Li et al. 2023).
- **Figure quality:** Figure 2 looks poor and caption text size is disproportionately large (Reviewer 4).
- **Scope too broad:** The topic is too large for such a small paper; a truly systematic review would need to focus on a smaller aspect (Reviewer 3, review question incorporated as weakness).
