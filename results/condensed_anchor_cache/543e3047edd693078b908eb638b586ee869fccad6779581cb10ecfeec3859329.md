## Merged Review

### Summary
The paper studies the neural tangent kernel (NTK) trace evolution during neural network training in three settings: supervised classification, the grokking phenomenon (sudden test accuracy improvement long after training accuracy plateaus), and semi-supervised learning. It proposes an efficient approximation for the NTK trace (Theorem 4.1), links the trace to the margin in an equivalent kernel SVM problem, and provides empirical observations from ResNet18 experiments.

### Strengths
- **Originality**: The idea of studying the NTK trace as an integral scalar characteristic is novel, as is the efficient approximation formula (Theorem 4.1). One reviewer (score 6, confidence 3) highlights the thorough exploration across different training settings.
- **Significance**: The paper covers diverse scenarios (supervised learning, grokking, semi-supervised learning) and the experimental connection between NTK trace and grokking (Figures 5, 6) is considered interesting by one reviewer (score 3, confidence 3), assuming experimental correctness.
- **Theoretical connection**: The link between the NTK trace and the margin of a kernel SVM problem provides a mathematical foundation (reviewer 1, score 6).
- **Clarity**: One reviewer (score 3, confidence 4) found the paper easy to follow.

### Weaknesses
**Foundational and theoretical issues**
- **Conflict between theoretical and empirical NTK** (reviewer 2, score 3, confidence 4): The theoretical NTK derivation assumes gradients from Gaussian-initialized parameters, but the empirical NTK uses trained parameters and does not follow a Gaussian distribution. Therefore Theorems 4.1–4.3 do not hold for standard (empirical) NTKs, limiting the significance of the convergence analysis.
- **Error in Theorem 4.1** (reviewer 4, score 3, confidence 3): The approximation formula is wrong: with \(\Delta \sim \epsilon \mathcal{N}(0,I_d)\), \(\mathbb{E}[\Delta \Delta^\top] = \epsilon^2 d\), so the expectation \(\mathbb{E}_\Delta\) is missing in lines 141 and 306, and the factor \(d\) is omitted; the impact on experimental results is unclear.
- **Theorem 4.3 is trivial** (reviewer 4): It is a straightforward consequence of Cauchy–Schwarz and does not merit theorem status.
- **Assumptions for margin link are too strong** (reviewers 3 and 4, both score 3): The simplification that after-training kernel makes all same-class examples equally similar and different-class examples orthogonal (lines 192–197) is unjustified. No experimental evidence supports these assumptions, and it is unclear if the cited work (Seleznova et al., 2024) holds under the homogeneity assumptions needed for Theorem 4.2.
- **Theorem 4.2 citation incomplete** (reviewer 4): The paper cites (Lyu & Li, 2019) without specifying the key assumption of model homogeneity. The statement “under some technical assumptions” is too vague.

**Experimental and interpretative shortcomings**
- **Lack of solid insights** (reviewer 3): Observations (trace increases in supervised learning, first decreases then increases in semi-supervised) are generic; contributions (lines 60–69) and conclusions (Section 6) provide no concrete actionable insights. The paper does not explain why the NTK trace is an important object to study or what specific insights its evolution provides.
- **Figure 1 evidence is unconvincing** (reviewer 4, supported by reviewer 2’s theoretical objection): In Figure 1(a) and (c) the NTK trace does not clearly plateau after training accuracy stabilizes; the curves are noisy and need smoothing or longer runs (as in Figure 2b). Moreover, the claim that the network is “implicitly maximizing the margin” (lines 207–208) was already proven in [1] Corollary 4.5, reducing novelty.
- **Grokking claims not well-supported** (reviewer 4): For Figure 5, line 311 states the NTK trace stabilizes only when test accuracy reaches its maximum, but the trace appears to have similar variability between \(10^2\)–\(10^4\) steps and after \(10^4\) steps (only a short plateau after a sudden drop). No clear plateau is visible.
- **Semi-supervised results unclear** (reviewer 4): In Section 5.3, Theorem 5.2 gives a lower bound on the square root of training error; Figure 9(b) shows the ratio decreases with \(\tau\) and tends to zero, but the paper claims this matches test accuracy decreasing with \(\tau\). The connection is missing: the bound is on training loss, not test loss, and is not tight.

**Writing and presentation problems**
- **Notation conflicts and inconsistencies**:
  - \(K\) is used both for the kernel matrix and the output size (line 132) (reviewer 3).
  - \(x\) is used for an input and for a kernel diagonal value (reviewer 3).
  - The variable \(w\) denotes both neural network parameters and the optimal margin vector in the SVM context (reviewer 1).
  - Transition from vector notation \(\|\cdot\|_F\) (Frobenius norm) to trace notation \(\operatorname{Tr}(\cdot)\) in Theorem 4.1 needs clearer explanation (reviewer 1).
  - In Theorem 4.1, \(\sim\) should be specified as “equivalent as \(\epsilon \to 0\)” (reviewer 3).
- **Missing definitions and unclear statements**:
  - “Logit perturb distance” used in the grokking section is not defined (reviewer 1).
  - “Grokking” is introduced but could benefit from a formal definition or context explaining why it occurs (reviewer 1).
  - Augmentation methods \(\omega(\cdot), \Omega(\cdot)\) and the notation \(prob(\cdot)\) in equations (5),(6) are not specified (reviewer 4).
  - Line 197: “Collect the dual variable in \(\alpha\) which corresponds to \(y_i = +1\) as \(\alpha^1\) and \(y_i = -1\) as \(\alpha^2\)” is confusing (reviewer 4).
  - Figure 3 describes ResNet18 layers as “Layer 1,2,3,4” but each is actually a concatenation of multiple convolution layers; a proper architecture figure or explanation is needed (reviewer 4).
- **Missing explicit mapping**: Theorems in the main text should be explicitly linked to those in Appendix A (reviewer 3).

**Missing experiments and comparisons**
- **Direct applicability** (reviewer 1): It is unclear how NTK trace insights can directly improve neural network training or architecture design.
- **Universality** (reviewer 4): Only one experiment on three datasets for non-grokking and two experiments for grokking; results may not generalize to different tasks, models, or datasets.
- **Deeper analysis needed** (reviewer 1): The implications of NTK trace behavior, especially in semi-supervised learning and grokking, require more detailed discussion.

**Reviewer disagreement**  
One reviewer (score 6, confidence 3) considered the paper a strong contribution with thorough exploration and a novel approximation (Theorems 4.1). The other three reviewers (scores 3, with confidences 4, 4, 3) were more critical, citing fundamental theoretical errors, weak experimental support, lack of insights, and poor presentation. The positive reviewer’s concerns about notation clarity and deeper analysis overlap with the criticisms of the others.
