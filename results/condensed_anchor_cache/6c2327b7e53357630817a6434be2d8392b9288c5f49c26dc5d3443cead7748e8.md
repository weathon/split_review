## Merged Review

### Summary
This paper investigates how autoregressive transformers perform integer addition. Through causal analysis, the authors identify attention heads in middle layers controlling carry propagation and show that localized attention distributions limit long-sequence performance. Inference intervention on Mistral-7B improves accuracy on 20-digit additions from 2% to 38%. Fine-tuning with carries up to length 10 improves accuracy but fails to generalize beyond that length. The paper aims to provide insights into whether models are merely statistical.

### Strengths
- Carefully designed tasks and intervention techniques to understand how the model performs addition, including testing long carry chains (R1).
- Natural story from ablation study to fine-tuning, making it understandable to a general reader (R2).
- First ablation study for addition in a model as large as Llama2, contributing to mechanistic interpretability of LLMs for logical tasks (R2).
- Experiments are thorough and use three models (Mistral-7B, Llama-2-7B, Gemma-7B) for comprehensiveness (R3, R4).
- Important research focus on limitations of Transformers for mathematical tasks with implications for quantitative applications (R3).
- Proposed intervention method is simple and effective (R4).
- Paper written in an easy-to-understand way (R4).

### Weaknesses
- **Missing experimental details:**
  - Input/output format and temperature setting not specified. Reviewer 2 prompted “233335 + 566667” at temperature=1 and got varying output formats (e.g., “Sure! Here is the result…” vs “Sure! Here is the calculation:…”). How was output parsed? Was temperature set to zero for deterministic decoding? (R2)
  - Fine-tuning parameter settings (e.g., number of epochs) not provided; dataset description lacking. Could grokking occur with sufficient fine-tuning steps? (R3, R4)
  - No discussion of the effect of fine-tuning on tasks unrelated to addition (e.g., typical benchmark scores for Llama2 or Mistral) (R2).
- **Notation and exposition issues:**
  - Equations (1)-(3) are unclear: σ, γ, and m_i are not explained (R2).
  - Variable v used without definition in Section 3.3, hindering understanding of the core ablation study (R2).
  - In line 177, notation x_{d+1} + y_{d+1} initially appears to mismatch indexing; the reasoning for the chosen indexing is not explained (R2).
  - The formula h_i^0 = d_i + pos(i) is incorrect because Mistral-7B uses RoPE (R4).
  - The notation pos_i is incorrect; in the original Transformer and RoPE papers, position is denoted as pos or m, not pos_i. This indicates the author did not read the original papers (R4). The authors refused to correct this and did not distinguish between GPT and LLaMA models; a reviewer called this a very low-quality oversight (R4).
- **Missing comparisons and positioning:**
  - No comparison with prior work on carry propagation, e.g., Sabbaghi et al., “Explicitly Encoding Structural Symmetry is Key to Length Generalization in Arithmetic Tasks” (R2).
  - The related work section mentions similar studies (lines 90–93) but does not clearly explain how this paper differs from them (R3).
  - Reviewer 1 asks about similar experiments for subtraction and about reverting the input to the order humans use – these are not discussed (R1).
- **Incomplete analysis and unsupported claims:**
  - Fine-tuning experiments were done with only one of the pre-trained models (R1). Contrast: another reviewer notes three models were used in the analysis, but fine-tuning itself was on a single model.
  - The abstract claims “the formation of the attention heads is crucial to the length generalization,” but this claim is not supported by evidence in the main text. Position encoding, a critical factor in length generalization, is not discussed; the paper lacks discussion of RoPE, position interpolation, or extrapolation (R4).
  - In Figure 6, there is no improvement in accuracy for lengths up to 5, despite the ablation study identifying carry propagation as the main error cause. This should be discussed in Section 4.2 (R2).
  - Figure 6 is not referenced in the main text; it only shows accuracy up to length 20, but the text mentions length 60 (R2).
  - Table 1 is not cited in Section 4.2 (R4).
  - Large variation in baseline scores for CC4, CC6, CC10 across models; Llama2-7B is very low for CC10. The sequence length of CC10 may exceed max_position_embeddings (Mistral: 32k with window 4k, Llama2: 4k, Gemma: 8k). This difference could affect the results and needs discussion (R4).
  - Reviewer 1 asks for the authors’ thoughts on whether the model is merely statistical or exhibits deeper reasoning – this philosophical point is not addressed.
  - No implementable algorithm/circuit for addition is provided that a Transformer could directly execute (R3).
- **Minority reviewer contrast:** Reviewer 4 was significantly more negative, repeatedly emphasizing that the model description is incorrect (position encoding, RoPE notation) and that the authors refused to make simple corrections, indicating a lack of basic knowledge. This reviewer concluded that “papers that neglect description in favor of simplicity should not be accepted to top conferences” and that the paper is “not worthy of acceptance.” Other reviewers (R1, R3) gave higher scores but noted the need for additional details.
