## Merged Review

### Summary
The paper proposes S3, a staleness-based subgraph sampling method for historical-embedding GNN training. It uses graph partitioning with edge weights defined as the sum of staleness scores of the two nodes, aiming to minimize the approximation error caused by stale embeddings. A local refinement heuristic is designed to avoid re-partitioning from scratch each epoch. Experiments on three historical embedding methods (GAS, GraphFM, LMC) show that S3 improves accuracy, with a 3× speedup on ogbn-products relative to full re-partitioning.

### Strengths
1. **Novel and well-motivated.** Leveraging periodic graph partition to reduce staleness error is an interesting direction (R1). The motivation that simple methods like METIS are unsuitable for historical embeddings is reasonable (R3). The proposed method makes sense overall (R4).
2. **Consistent accuracy improvements.** S3 improves the accuracy of GAS, GraphFM, and LMC across datasets (R1, R2, R3, R4). The ablation experiments confirm the effectiveness of the staleness-based sampling (R3).
3. **Clear presentation.** The paper is well-written and easy to follow (R4).
4. **Sufficient experimental scope.** Experiments use common datasets and three backbone methods (R2). The re-sampling overhead is not excessive, and performance without re-sampling is still acceptable (R3).
5. **General applicability.** The analysis and method are designed to benefit most historical embedding methods (R2, R3).

### Weaknesses
1. **Theorem 1 is flawed.** (Raised by R1 and R4; not commented on by R2 or R3.) The proof in Appendix B establishes an upper bound of the original expression, but minimizing an upper bound does not guarantee minimization of the original quantity—the theorem statement should be significantly weakened. There is also an inconsistency between squared approximation error and non-squared approximation error (pages 16–17) that needs clarification. Additionally, it is unclear whether the theorem holds for non-mean aggregations (e.g., Max/Min in PNA, R1 Q6), and how the constant \(C\) in equation (3) is determined (R1 Q7).
2. **Accuracy gains are marginal.** (Noted by all reviewers.) Improvements often appear only in the third decimal place (R1, R4). On Reddit the improvement is very small (R3). This limits the practical impact of the method (R1, R4, R2).
3. **Algorithm 1 requires full-neighborhood forward propagation, causing exponential explosion.** (Raised by R2; R1 Q1 alludes to the computational cost.) Computing staleness scores for every node requires full-neighbor aggregation, which suffers from the same neighbor explosion problem the paper aims to avoid. The \(L\) rounds of full-graph aggregation for refinement likely add significant time and memory overhead. Reviewers question whether this step is affordable and how its cost compares to graph partitioning (R1 Q1, R2 Q2).
4. **Experimental evaluation needs improvement.** (Raised primarily by R1 and R4; R2 and R3 also note missing comparisons.)
   - (a) Table 1 is missing results for many cases; it should be clarified which are not reported in original papers vs. difficult to reproduce, the difficulties should be explained, and at least the proposed method’s results should be given for every dataset.
   - (b) Datasets used are relatively small; larger graphs (e.g., Papers-100M, MAG240, IGB) should be evaluated, but the cost of graph partitioning on such graphs is a concern.
   - (c) The necessity of periodic partition adjustment is not directly validated; measuring the proportion of nodes that change partition would be informative.
   - (d) The influence of the number of partitions (i.e., mini-batch size) on accuracy and efficiency should be reported.
   - (e) Convergence comparisons between each backbone method with and without S3 are missing (R1 Q5).
   - (f) Efficiency analysis of S3+GAS and S3+GraphFM is missing, and the reason why S3 improves LMC efficiency in some cases (Table 3) is unexplained (R1 Q4).
   - (g) Table 2 is not informative enough; a comparison of staleness score trends (e.g., as in PipeGCN Fig. 5) would be more useful (R1 Q8).
5. **Section 3.3 (refinement algorithm) is too brief.** (R3 w2; also noted by R2 a that the algorithm is based on the Kernighan-Lin method.) More detailed explanation is needed to improve readability.
6. **Missing comparison with LLCG.** Since S3 performs periodic full-graph aggregation, an explicit comparison with LLCG, which also uses a form of full-graph aggregation, is warranted (R1 Q2).
7. **No comparison with the full-neighborhood model.** The accuracy obtained by S3 should be compared to the full-neighborhood model to quantify the impact of staleness-based sampling. Also, the gap between embeddings computed under the updated parameters vs. those from a full-neighborhood model is not studied (R2 Q3, Q4).
8. **Hyperparameter tuning unclear.** It is not stated whether the reported hyperparameters are optimal or if other settings were tried (R2 Q1).
