## Merged Review

### Summary
AnalogGenie uses a GPT-based generative model to automatically discover analog circuit topologies. It contributes a pin-level sequence-based graph representation (each node is a device pin) and a large-scale dataset of over 3,000 topologies covering LDOs, bandgap references, comparators, PLLs, LNAs, PAs, mixers, and VCOs—manually implemented and simulated in an industry-standard tool with performance metrics. An Eulerian circuit sequentialization (edges as two directed arcs, starting/ending at VSS) and data augmentation via multiple Eulerian trails reduce memory and overfitting. The model reframes topology generation as next-device-pin prediction, using a customized tokenizer and pre-training without requiring performance metrics. Reported results claim AnalogGenie generates large-scale, valid, unseen, and high-performance topologies, outperforming previous methods (CktGNN, LaMAGIC, AnalogCoder). Reviewers acknowledge the work as a promising step but raise concerns about missing circuit performance evaluation, potential fine-tuning data overlap, generalizability to RF circuits, and lack of real-world validation.

### Strengths
- **Novel pin-level sequence-based graph representation** for analog circuits, enabling a unique one-to-one mapping between graph and topology, and making scalable generation possible with a generative model. [R1, R2, R4]
- **Large-scale, comprehensive dataset** of over 3,000 topologies covering LDOs, bandgaps, comparators, PLLs, LNAs, PAs, mixers, VCOs, with manual implementation and simulation in an industry-standard tool. Expected to be open-source. [R1, R2, R4]
- **Eulerian graph representation** avoids adjacency matrices, reducing memory requirements. By treating undirected edges as two directed arcs, it ensures at least one Eulerian circuit from any vertex, then sequentializes the graph into a trail visiting each edge exactly once, starting and ending at VSS. [R2, R4]
- **Creative application of NLP techniques**: reformulates analog topology generation as a next-device-pin prediction task with VSS as the starting token, using a customized tokenizer and pre-training that does not require performance metrics. [R3, R4]
- **Data augmentation** by learning multiple unique Eulerian circuits for the same topology, mitigating overfitting and reducing inductive bias. [R2, R4]
- **Claimed strong generation performance**: AnalogGenie can produce a broader variety of large-scale, valid, unseen, and high-performance topologies than prior arts (CktGNN, LaMAGIC, AnalogCoder). [R3, R4]
- **First work to apply generative AI models to analog circuit design** (R2 explicitly states this). [R2, R4? note reviewer disagreement: R1, R3, R4 do not challenge this but R2 is specific]
- **Valuable, practically significant circuits in the Appendix**, according to a reviewer with analog circuit design experience. [R4]
- High confidence from R4 (confidence 5) that the dataset will greatly advance analog design automation.

### Weaknesses
- **No performance evaluation of generated circuits.** The experiments focus on model metrics (accuracy, validity, count) but do not analyze the electrical performance of generated topologies (e.g., for OpAmps: slew rate, GBW, CMRR, Aol, Ib, Vos; for LDOs: Dropout Voltage, PSRR). This is essential for real-world use. [R2] (Reviewer 2, score 6, is particularly critical of this omission; R1 also asks if sizing/performance optimization is performed.)
- **Unfair comparison of figure-of-merit (FoM).** The paper does not propose methods to optimize circuit FoM, yet compares FoM with prior methods. The benefit may come from the large dataset and the model may reuse known circuits; previous methods could not access this dataset, making the comparison unfair. [R1]
- **Fine-tuning dataset overlap unclear.** Fine-tuning improves accuracy significantly. It must be shown that validation circuits have no overlap with the fine-tuning dataset; otherwise the improvement could stem from seen structures. The authors should provide examples where fine-tuning is necessary for success. [R1]
- **Eulerian circuit definition inconsistency.** In Appendix A.7 (Figure 7), the first generated sequence visits VOUT-PMI-G twice, but the definition requires visiting each edge exactly once. The authors need to explain this discrepancy. [R1]
- **Confusion about inductive bias from multiple Eulerian circuits.** It is unclear whether different Eulerian circuits representing the same topology share or differ in inductive bias. The authors should provide a detailed, easy-to-understand explanation, preferably with proofs. [R2]
- **Pin-grained representation may be just data augmentation.** If the main innovation is changing to a lower-level representation, applying this strategy to older methods (e.g., GNN-based models) might also improve performance. The novelty of the model itself is then diminished. [R2]
- **Limited circuit types in experiments.** The dataset includes RF circuits (LNAs, mixers, PLLs), but experiments focus only on commonly used analog circuits (OpAmps, LDOs, bandgaps). It is unclear whether AnalogGenie is fully qualified to generate RF circuits with strict performance requirements (e.g., PLL filters), or whether the generated circuits meet existing performance standards. [R2, R4]
- **No handling of process technology or practical design constraints.** The work only generates functional topologies; it does not incorporate process technology or optimize for performance and efficiency in real fabrication processes. [R1]
- **No discussion of limitations or future research directions.** The paper claims untapped potential but does not detail its own shortcomings or suggest areas for future work. [R3]
- **Customized tokenizer and fine-tuning effects unclear.** It is not shown how the tokenizer and fine-tuning improve generation quality, nor whether they could reduce the inherent reasoning ability of the language model when applied to other tasks. [R3]
- **Lack of real-world validation.** The evaluation would benefit from review by senior analog designers and, if possible, tape-out to confirm silicon feasibility. [R4]
