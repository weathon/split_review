## Merged Review

### Summary
The paper proposes MaGIC, a framework for multi-modality guided image completion that supports text, canny edge, sketch, segmentation, depth, pose, and arbitrary combinations. It uses a two-stage approach: first, modality-specific conditional U-Nets (MCU-Nets) are fine-tuned for single-modality guidance; second, a training-free Consistent Modality Blending (CMB) method aggregates signals via gradient guidance in latent space. Experiments on COCO, Places2, and in-the-wild data show improvements over SOTA in inpainting, outpainting, and editing.

### Strengths
- **Innovative flexible approach**: Addresses multi-modality guided image completion with a training-free blending procedure (CMB) that allows arbitrary combination of modalities (text, edge, sketch, segmentation, depth, pose) without retraining (R1, R2, R3).
- **Strong qualitative results**: Large and consistent improvements over SOTA methods (e.g., T2I-Adapter, ControlNet) in image quality; convincing demonstrations across completion tasks (R1, R3). Reviewer 2 finds the qualitative comparisons very intuitive.
- **Neat integration into diffusion process**: CMB extends classifier-guidance ideas to multiple modalities in a scalable, training-free manner, preserving the feature distribution of the original SD U-Net denoiser (R2, R3).
- **Well-organized presentation (majority view)**: Reviewer 2 and Reviewer 3 state that the paper is easy to follow with good illustrations and substantial appendix details (e.g., MCU-Net architecture). Reviewer 3 specifically calls it “very well written.”
- **Broad interest**: Dealing with large missing regions is a critical task of broad interest in ML and image processing (R3).

### Weaknesses
- **Clarity and typos (disagreement)**: Reviewer 1 finds the paper challenging to follow with multiple typos (e.g., “Denoising” after Eq. 2), while Reviewer 2 and Reviewer 3 find it clear. The discrepancy indicates presentation issues that hinder some readers.
- **Non-standard update scheme (Eq. 5)**: The update performs gradient descent w.r.t. \(z_t\) but updates \(z'_{t-1}\). This choice is not discussed or justified, leaving questions about its validity (R1).
- **Missing quantitative evaluation vs. ControlNet/T2I-Adapter**: The paper provides only qualitative results when comparing with ControlNet and T2I-Adapter. Reviewer 1 requests quantitative metrics; Reviewer 2 specifically asks for P-IDS, U-IDS, and FID on multi-modality cases. Reviewer 1 also asks about CLIP score and reconstruction performances.
- **Inadequate dataset and modality descriptions**: Datasets and conditioning modalities are presented briefly; more detailed description and rationale would be beneficial (R1).
- **Missing ablations**: 
  - The impact of weights \(\delta_c\) in Eq. (4) is not explored; tuning could be cumbersome (R1).
  - No justification why simple feature-level addition (as in T2I-Adapter) is not good. The argument that the denoiser is trained only on \(\hat{F}_c = F_{enc} + F_c\) is unconvincing, especially considering ControlNet’s zero-convolutions, which avoid distribution mismatch (R2).
- **Inconsistent results in Table 3.b**: For fixed \(P=30\), FID(\(Q=1\)) > FID(\(Q=10\)) > FID(\(Q=5\)), which does not follow expected patterns and raises concerns about CMB’s stability and hyperparameter tuning (R1).
- **MCU-Net identity and novelty concerns**: Reviewer 2 argues that MCU-Net is essentially the same as T2I-Adapter; thus the only contribution is CMB. Reviewer 2 rates contribution as 1 (poor) while others rate 3 (good). This strong disagreement should be noted.
- **Claim about T2I-Adapter/ControlNet multi-modality capability**: The paper states that T2I-Adapter and ControlNet “fail to simultaneously use multi-modality as guidance.” Reviewer 2 points out that T2I-Adapter can combine multiple modalities (see its Section 4.3.2). This claim needs clarification and acknowledgment (R2).
- **Modality definition**: Reviewer 3 is not fully convinced that different image channels/features (depth, sketch, edge) should be called modalities.
- **Failure cases**: Multiple reviewers ask for failure cases to understand limitations of CMB versus ControlNet/T2I-Adapter and where each method falls short (R2, R3).
- **Minor issues**: Equation (4) subscript typo (c vs. C) (R2). No quantitative comparison for increasing number of modalities on image quality, realism, and spatial consistency (R2).
