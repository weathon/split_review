## Merged Review

### Summary
The paper proposes OCEAN, an offline framework for evaluating and aligning chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning in LLMs using knowledge graphs (KGs). It models CoT as an MDP, trains a KG preference policy via RL, and introduces a KG-IPS estimator for unbiased offline evaluation. The authors provide theoretical proofs (unbiasedness, variance lower bound) and conduct extensive experiments on multi-hop QA, knowledge-intensive tasks, and commonsense reasoning across several model scales.

### Strengths
1. **Novel and important problem**: The concept of aligning CoT reasoning with knowledge graphs via a KG-IPS estimator is well-motivated, addressing the high cost of human feedback and the challenges of online deployment. The use of KGs for LLM alignment is novel and interesting. (R1, R2, R4, R5)

2. **Theoretical contributions**: The paper provides a thorough theoretical foundation for the KG-IPS estimator, proving its unbiasedness and providing a variance lower bound, which adds robustness to the evaluation framework. (R1, R2, R3)

3. **Comprehensive experiments**: The authors conduct extensive experiments across multi-hop QA (e.g., HotpotQA, 2WikiMultihopQA), knowledge-intensive tasks, and commonsense reasoning, using multiple LLMs (e.g., LLaMA-2, Mistral) and baselines (SFT, direct prompting, etc.), demonstrating OCEAN’s ability to align reasoning paths while maintaining general capabilities. (R1, R2, R3, R4, R5)

4. **Clarity of presentation**: Several reviewers found the paper well-written and enjoyable to read (R2, R5). (Note that other reviewers raised significant clarity concerns, see Weaknesses.)

### Weaknesses
1. **Limited reasoning scope**: The framework relies on a specific KG (Wikidata5M) and may not generalize to abstract or conceptual reasoning where LLMs use non-fact-based reasoning; the paper does not evaluate OCEAN on such tasks. (R1, R2, R4)

2. **Handling multiple correct paths**: It is unclear how the KG preference policy represents multiple valid reasoning trajectories, and it may penalize correct reasoning paths that are not present in the KG. (R2)

3. **Challenge to offline necessity**: One reviewer (R4) questions the offline focus, citing recent work on online optimization of reasoning (Setlur et al. “Rewarding Progress”, Zelikman et al. “Quiet-STaR”, Hosseini et al. “V-STaR”), arguing that reasoning steps can be optimized online rather than requiring offline evaluation.

4. **Insufficient performance evidence**: The experimental tables do not clearly show that OCEAN significantly outperforms baselines; a more detailed discussion of statistical significance or effect size is needed. (R2, R4)

5. **Missing ablation studies**: The paper lacks ablations to isolate contributions of the objective in Eq. 3, the verbalized CoTs from GPT-4, the policy update in Eq. 4, and the effect of context (w/ctx vs. w/o ctx). Missing comparisons include: direct method (DM) vs. IPS for evaluation, offline evaluation without subsequent policy optimization, SFT + normal CoT, training SFT on reasoning steps vs. only questions/answers, computation of value for SFT/base models, and the dependence on index \(k\) in policy gradient. (R1, R4, R5)

6. **Limited model scale**: Validation on smaller LLMs is not included, which would strengthen claims of adaptability. (R1)

7. **Accessibility and complexity**: The framework involves multiple components (MDP modeling, KG-IPS estimator, verbalized KG policy) which may hinder practical adoption; suggestions for modularization or simplification. (R1)

8. **Lack of efficiency analysis**: No report on computational overhead compared to baseline CoT methods. (R1)

9. **Adaptation to different KGs**: The paper does not discuss performance with KGs of different structure or domain (e.g., smaller or domain-specific KGs) nor what modifications would be required. (R1, R2)

10. **Knowledge conflicts**: No discussion of how OCEAN resolves conflicts between LLM parametric knowledge and KG feedback when both are valid but disagree. (R1)

11. **Statistical method justification**: The choice of sub-Gaussian concentration inequalities for confidence intervals is not explained, and comparison with other measures is missing. (R1)

12. **Process reward ignored**: The KG reference policy relies solely on binary outcome (answer accuracy); the potential benefit of process-based reward signals (e.g., step-level reasoning quality) is not explored. (R3)

13. **Unclear role of base policy \(\pi_0\)**: The role of the base LLM policy is not clearly explained; a figure illustrating the pipeline (reference policy \(\mu_\phi\), target policy \(\pi_\theta\), base policy \(\pi_0\)) would improve clarity. (R3)

14. **Notation and formulation issues**:  
    - In Eq. 3: relation between \(e\), \(a_t\), and \(c_t\) is unclear; \(c_t^{(i)}\) appears outside the summation with an unassigned subscript \(t\); inconsistent use of \(e \in c_t\) vs. \(e \in c_t^{(i)}\); term \(T_i|c_t^{(I)}\) needs expansion. (R3, R5)  
    - The symbol \(e\) is used inconsistently to denote both edges (line 169) and entities (line 172). (R5)  
    - The reward function at token level ( \(\log \pi_0(e|s_t^{(i)})\) in line 221) conflicts with the step-level formulation in line 159 (\(r_t = r(s_t, c_t)\)). (R5)

15. **Missing examples of data generation**: No concrete examples of sampled KG trajectories \(h\) or of the verbalized transformations to \(c\) using GPT-4. (R5)

16. **Incomplete details on KG exploration**: Transition dynamics, sampling strategy, criteria for selecting next nodes, and offline dataset statistics (trajectory length distribution, entity/relation counts) are not reported. (R5)

17. **Training paradigm ambiguity**: It is unclear whether the KG preference policy is trained in an online or offline manner. (R5)

18. **Presentation clarity concerns**: Several reviewers (R3, R4, R5) note that writing improvements are needed: missing full name for KG-IPS on first use, unclear x-axis in Figures 1(b) and 1(c), limited discussion of reference model performance and its impact on target model evaluation. (R3, R4, R5)
