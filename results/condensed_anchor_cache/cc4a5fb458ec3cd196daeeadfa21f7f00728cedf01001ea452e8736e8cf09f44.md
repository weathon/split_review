## Merged Review

### Summary
The paper proposes FISTAPruner, a layer-wise post-training pruning method for LLMs using a LASSO-like convex optimization model solved via FISTA (Fast Iterative Shrinkage-Thresholding Algorithm). It includes an intra-layer error correction mechanism and extends to 2:4 semi-structured pruning. Experiments on OPT, LLaMA, LLaMA-2, LLaMA-3 (125M to 70B parameters) show improvements over SparseGPT, Wanda, DSnoT, and PERP on perplexity (WikiText-2-raw, PTB, C4) and zero-shot task performance. Scores: three 6s (confidence 3-4) and one 3 (confidence 5, minority reviewer). The minority reviewer finds novelty and writing quality insufficient despite high accuracy, while majority reviewers praise the approach but note limited novelty relative to SparseGPT and insufficient ablation.

### Strengths
- **Novel integration of FISTA with a LASSO-like model** for LLM pruning, providing theoretical convergence guarantees (Reviewers 1, 2, 3; Reviewer 4 disagrees on novelty).
- **Substantial accuracy gains**: up to 5% higher average accuracy on zero-shot tasks when pruning LLaMA-3 70B; removes 50% of parameters while retaining 98.6% (unstructured) and 95.6% (2:4) zero-shot performance (Reviewers 1, 2, 4).
- **Strong results in structured 2:4 pruning**, enabling practical hardware acceleration (Reviewers 1, 3).
- **Reduction of heuristic reliance** by using LASSO to identify pruned weights with targeted sparsity (Reviewer 1).
- **Intra-layer error correction mechanism** that mitigates cumulative errors across decoder layers (Reviewers 1, 2; Reviewer 4 notes this idea is not new).
- **Extensive experiments** covering diverse models (OPT, LLaMA, LLaMA-2, LLaMA-3) and sparsity patterns, consistently showing comparable or best perplexity/accuracy (Reviewer 4; minority reviewer acknowledges thoroughness).
- **Figures are straightforward to understand** (Reviewer 4).
- **Well-written and easy to understand** (Reviewer 3; Reviewer 4 disagrees on writing quality).

### Weaknesses
- **Lack of novelty**: The approach closely resembles SparseGPT but uses a different optimizer (FISTA instead of iterative weight updates) for the same pruning-as-optimization formulation; error correction is already present in prior work; L1 regularization for pruning is a prevalent idea; adaptive hyperparameter tuning algorithm has no novelty and is not compared with existing tuning methods like BOHB (Reviewers 3, 4). The minority reviewer (score 3) strongly emphasizes insufficient originality; majority reviewers (score 6) note the method is innovative in LLM pruning context but acknowledge similarity to SparseGPT.
- **Insufficient explanation of FISTA vs. alternatives**: No clear explanation of why FISTA is better than traditional gradient descent (e.g., SGD with momentum) or L1-regularized training for this pruning task (Reviewers 1, 4).
- **Missing computational cost comparison**: FISTAPruner’s layer-by-layer retraining (finding W*) has higher cost than SparseGPT/Wanda, which do not retrain. Paper does not compare under similar computational budgets (e.g., same number of passes per layer) (Reviewer 1).
- **Intra-layer error correction mechanism is under-explained**: Could benefit from more detailed analysis; it is unclear whether other methods (SparseGPT, Wanda) could achieve similar gains if integrated with this mechanism. Also, error correction is applied only intra-layer, not globally across layers (Reviewers 2, 4).
- **No proper ablation studies**: Sections “Amount of Calibration Data” and “Warm Start” are not actual ablations (e.g., removing specific features). No ablation on 2:4 structured sparsity to explain why substantial improvement occurs there (Reviewer 3).
- **Limited competitor selection**: Missing comparisons with structured pruning algorithms such as LLM-Pruner and SLEB, without justification (Reviewer 4).
- **Writing and presentation issues** (minority reviewer): Insufficient background on FISTA; claim in line 148 (“Instead of pruning each operator in isolation like existing works”) is inaccurate because prior works also consider layer-wise error; missing reproducibility statement; inconsistent equation labeling (e.g., “Theorem 3.3” should be “Theorem 1”); formatting issues (bold text for matrices, table captions below tables, too many bold entries in Table 6); outdated models (OPT, LLaMA-1) used instead of newer ones (Phi, Gemma, Mistral) (Reviewer 4).
- **Ambiguity about DSnoT and PERP**: Unclear whether they are competitors or compatible algorithms; recommended to compare FISTAPruner alone vs. FISTAPruner+DSnoT/FISTAPruner+PERP to show compatibility (Reviewer 4).
- **Parallel pruning claim unclear**: Paper states parallel pruning across decoder layers (line 306) but method is described as sequential; explanation needed (Reviewer 1).
- **Warm start takeaway unclear**: What is the main point of the Warm Start section? (Reviewer 4).
