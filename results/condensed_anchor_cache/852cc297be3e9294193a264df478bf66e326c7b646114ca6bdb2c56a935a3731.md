## Merged Review

### Summary
The paper proposes a semi-parametric retrieval framework (SiDR) that supports both an embedding-based index (parametric) and a binary token index (non-parametric). It aligns masked term prediction with bag-of-tokens (BoT) representations, allowing a single encoder to be used for both forms. The approach reduces index preparation time from 30 GPU hours to 2 CPU hours and storage from 31 GB to 2 GB compared to embedding-based indices. Evaluations on Wikipedia-based QA benchmarks and BEIR show improvements: 3% higher top-1 accuracy than DPR with embedding index, 9% higher than BM25 with token index.

### Strengths
- **Novelty of re-using representations across two retrieval modes.** The same parametric embedding is aligned with BoT to enable both vector retrieval and non-parametric token retrieval. This addresses industry problems of index cost and freshness, and is considered novel by some reviewers (reviewers 2,3). (Reviewer 1 also notes the static binary BoT document representation is first of its kind.)
- **Simple, cost-effective index.** The binary token index is static, requires no parameter updates, and can be set up quickly (2 CPU hours, 2 GB storage). Retrieval can be carried out on GPU. (Reviewers 1,3)
- **Substantial evaluation.** The paper uses BEIR datasets plus multiple QA benchmarks, and compares against strong baselines (DPR, BM25, ANCE). Results are promising. (Reviewer 3)
- **Hybrid retrieval approach.** Combining the two forms (parametric re-ranking over non-parametric candidates) shows gains over either alone. (Reviewers 2,3)
- **Clear method description** for the loss and training procedure. (Reviewer 4, though Reviewer 3 disagrees on clarity)

### Weaknesses
- **Collision problem.** The number of documents per token grows with collection size and document length; performance on larger collections (e.g., MS-Marco) is unclear. (Reviewer 1)
- **Negative mining on Wikipedia.** The proposed negative mining strategy is specific to Wikipedia; this may invalidate the results in Table 1. (Reviewer 1)
- **No test of orthogonal SOTA training techniques** (e.g., knowledge distillation) despite authors claiming they are orthogonal. (Reviewer 1)
- **Unjustified activation choice.** Why elu1p instead of softplus? (Reviewer 1)
- **Unused loss term.** The second term L(V_BoT(q), V_θ(p)) in the semi-parametric loss is never used; no ablation shows its importance. (Reviewer 1)
- **Missing definition of VDR_β.** Table 1 includes a baseline VDR_β that is not defined in the VDR paper. (Reviewer 1)
- **Unfair efficiency comparison to BM25.** BM25 results are based on single-thread CPU, which is a huge disadvantage. BM25 can be implemented on GPU and optimized with tools like PISA. Reported speedup is not meaningful. (Reviewers 1,3) Moreover, Table 3 is confusing (BM25 takes 40 seconds but 2 minutes overall?); needs thorough revision. (Reviewer 3)
- **Lack of interpretability analysis.** BoT retrieval is naturally interpretable, but the paper does not demonstrate how it tackles standard IR problems like polysemy and synonymy. (Reviewer 2)
- **No study of term-weighting impact.** The document side uses unweighted BoT (binary), unlike BM25’s weighted terms; how does this affect retrieval? (Reviewer 2)
- **Apples-to-oranges comparison with BM25.** BM25 does not have query-side synonym expansion, while SiDR’s query is an aligned version of the parametric embedding, giving an unfair advantage. (Reviewer 2)
- **Missing comparisons to hybrid retrieval systems.** Natural baselines like Gao et al. (2021) and Kuzi et al. (2020) are not included. (Reviewer 2)
- **Missing comparisons to other late parametric methods.** The late parametric mechanism is not new, yet no comparison to other late parametric approaches is provided. (Reviewer 4)
- **Bolding of best results is misleading.** In Table 1 (parametric scenario), SiDR does not surpass ANCE, yet the authors bold it as best. (Reviewer 4)
- **Poor non-parametric performance.** In Table 2, SiDR fails to surpass BM25 in most datasets. (Reviewer 4)
- **No analysis of why SiDR performs better in the late parametric scenario.** (Reviewer 4)
- **Presentation is hard to follow.** Several sections are confusing:
  - Section 3.1 (revisiting MLM) is unclear; the phrase “consistencies between semi-parametric alignment and masked language model pre-training” should be “connections”. (Reviewer 3)
  - The distinction between parametric, non-parametric, and semi-parametric representations is blurry; better to explain as learned vs hand-crafted token weights. (Reviewer 3)
  - BoT is introduced abruptly; should be motivated from a bag-of-words perspective first. (Reviewer 3)
  - No explanation for the need of VDR (the prior work this extends). (Reviewer 3)
- **BM25 with re-ranking is a strong baseline** that should be highlighted and connected to prior work (e.g., Leonhardt et al. 2022). (Reviewer 3)
- **Disagreement among reviewers.** Some reviewers praise the approach as novel and well-motivated (reviewers 2,3), while others question the novelty of late parametric mechanism and the fairness of comparisons (reviewers 1,4).
