## Merged Review

### Summary
The paper proposes AugKD, a knowledge distillation method for image super-resolution that uses unpaired data augmentations (zoom-in/zoom-out) to generate auxiliary distillation samples and enforce label consistency regularization via invertible transformations. The method is designed to address the limitation that teacher outputs in SR lack extra information beyond the ground truth. Experiments show improvements over scratch training and existing KD methods across multiple SR networks and datasets.

### Strengths
- Clear motivation: teacher guidance in SR is shaded by ground truth, justifying a tailored KD training paradigm (Reviewer 1, 2, 3, 4).
- Method is simple, effective, and easy to follow; code provided for reproducibility (Reviewer 2, 4, 5).
- Comprehensive ablation studies verify that both auxiliary distillation samples and label consistency regularization contribute to performance gains, and test various setups (Reviewer 1, 3, 4).
- Outperforms scratch training and 7 baseline KD methods across multiple SR tasks (Reviewer 1).
- Demonstrated adaptability to different SR models (EDSR, RCAN, SwinIR) and scales; e.g., RCAN ×4 improves by 0.25 dB on Urban100 compared to scratch (Reviewer 4, 5).
- Well-organized structure and clearly described method facilitates reproducibility (Reviewer 4, 5).
- Deep insight into SR-specific KD mechanics (Reviewer 2, 4).
- Extensive quantitative and qualitative analysis provided (Reviewer 5).

### Weaknesses
- The paper’s contribution is considered modest by some reviewers; gains shown in Figure 2 and Tables 2–4 are slight, questioning practical value (Reviewer 3). One reviewer finds improvements substantial (Reviewer 5). *[Contrast preserved]*
- **Applicability to other low-level tasks** (denoising, deblurring) is not explored; the paper would benefit from discussion on how augmentation strategies could be adapted (Reviewer 1, 4).
- The evaluation uses older models (EDSR 2017, RCAN 2018); SwinIR (2021) is included but only compared to Scratch and basic KD. More comprehensive evaluation with recent architectures and advanced KD methods is needed to substantiate model-agnostic claims (Reviewer 1). Performance with SwinIR suggests possible architecture limitations (Reviewer 3). Would the method adapt to backbones like Mamba? (Reviewer 4).
- **Lack of deep analysis of performance gains**: It is unclear *why* AugKD improves teacher-student fidelity. Is the gain from data expansion (augmentation increasing training set size)? How does augmentation strength affect fidelity and final distillation results? (Reviewer 2, 3). The mechanism by which augmentations help representation learning is not explained, nor why prior methods failed to capture these benefits (Reviewer 3).
- **Motivation clarity issues**: The paper states teacher output contains barely extra information exceeding GT (Line 76–78), yet Section 3.2 and Figure 2 use PSNR between teacher and student outputs to evaluate KD quality. This appears contradictory (Reviewer 2). The motivation for label consistency regularization is unclear (Reviewer 4).
- **Ablation of label consistency is insufficient**: Only invertible transformations (flipping, rotation) tested. Would non-invertible augmentations (random cropping, patch shuffling) work? (Reviewer 5). The adaptive selection of zoom-in samples is claimed ineffective (Lines 241–244) but lacks sufficient experimental support (Reviewer 4).
- **Choice of augmentations not justified**: Why zoom-in/zoom-out specifically? Would other augmentations (translation, elastic transformations, perspective changes) provide complementary benefits? (Reviewer 4, 5).
- Inverse augmentation design question: What if inverse augmentation is dropped and augmentation is applied at the end of the teacher model during training (same architecture in inference)? (Reviewer 2).
- Visualization in Figure 2 is unclear; a bar plot may be more effective (Reviewer 4).
- Additional unanswered questions: Why does combining FAKD with AugKD yield lower performance than AugKD alone (Table 9)? Why is AugKD on RCAN ×4 better than heterogeneous distillation in Table 5? Does \(L_{dukd}\) in Figure 1 correspond to \(L_{augkd}\) in Figure 4? (Reviewer 5).
- Training efficiency comparison with other KD techniques is missing (Reviewer 4).
