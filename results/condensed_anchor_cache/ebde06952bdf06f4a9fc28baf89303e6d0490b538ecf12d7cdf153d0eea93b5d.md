## Merged Review

### Summary

The paper proposes Hi-Mamba, a hierarchical Mamba network for efficient image super-resolution. It introduces the Hierarchical Mamba Block (HMB) combining Local SSM (L-SSM) and Region SSM (R-SSM) with single-direction scanning, and Direction Alternation Hierarchical Mamba Groups (DA-HMG) to enrich spatial relationship modeling. The goal is to reduce computation overhead from multi-direction scanning in SSM-based SR. The method is evaluated on five benchmarks (Set5, Set14, BSD100, Urban100, Manga109) at ×2, ×3, ×4 scales, trained on DIV2K and Flickr2K.

### Strengths

1. **Effective techniques with ablation validation** (Reviewer 1, confidence 5): Various proposed techniques improve Mamba-based SR modeling ability, and comprehensive ablation studies validate their effectiveness. Hi-Mamba-L strikes a better performance–cost balance compared to MambaIR.

2. **Clear presentation and strong visual results** (Reviewer 2, confidence 5): The paper is well-structured with clean, well-organized figures. Visual comparisons on Urban100 show Hi-Mamba restores better local textures than competitors while maintaining higher LAM attributions and area of contributions.

3. **Dual feature extraction and computational efficiency** (Reviewer 3, confidence 4): L-SSM (local) and R-SSM (regional) combine to enhance feature expression. Superior performance and efficiency: e.g., in Urban100 ×2 SR, Hi-Mamba-S reduces FLOPs to 294G while improving PSNR. Higher visual quality with reduced blur artifacts and distortion in ×4 SR.

4. **Creative architecture addressing high computation cost** (Reviewer 4, score 5 – minority more positive, confidence 4): The hierarchical structure and directional alternation were praised as a creative approach to enhance Mamba in SR tasks, directly addressing the high computation cost of multi-direction scanning in SSM-based SR models. This reviewer considered the contributions valuable despite other reviewers finding them incremental.

### Weaknesses

1. **Limited novelty and incremental improvements** (Reviewers 1–4): All reviewers noted the contributions appear incremental over prior works such as MambaIR, LocalMamba, Vmamba, and MSVMamba. Reviewer 2 (confidence 5) stated the motivation largely draws from these works without adequate elaboration on why L-SSM and R-SSM enhance modeling. Reviewer 4 (score 5) acknowledged the changes as effective but not fundamentally new, limiting overall impact. Reviewer 3 (confidence 4) noted the improvement looks relatively simple.

2. **Missing comparisons with state-of-the-art methods** (Reviewers 1, 3, 4):
   - Reviewer 1 (confidence 5) asked for comparison with Transformer-based HAT, ATD, IPG, and for LAM visualization comparison with GRL-B and SRFormer (enlarged window-size).
   - Reviewer 3 (confidence 4) noted newer methods DAT and HAT are not compared, and Hi-Mamba does not show a great advantage over them.
   - Reviewer 4 (confidence 4) requested broader comparisons, additional evaluation metrics, and visual comparisons on challenging datasets.

3. **Missing complexity, latency, and memory analysis** (Reviewers 1, 2, 4):
   - Reviewer 1: SRFormer results appear in Table 3 but its model complexity and GPU latency are missing from Table 4 and Figure 5.
   - Reviewer 2 (confidence 5): The directional alternation (similar to MambaCSR) reduces computation but may introduce performance trade-offs that should be discussed.
   - Reviewer 4: Lacks thorough analysis of complexity trade-offs (memory usage, inference latency) relative to MambaIR and Transformer-based models; additional ablations on how design changes impact efficiency would strengthen the paper.

4. **Insufficient explanation of key design choices** (Reviewers 2, 4):
   - Reviewer 2 asked for a theoretical perspective on how L-SSM and R-SSM enhance contextual modeling (should be addressed before block structure).
   - Reviewer 4 asked how DA-HMG specifically enhances spatial relationship modeling and whether other combinations of scan directions were tested.
   - Reviewer 1 additionally asked whether Avgpool in Region SSM could be replaced with other downsampling methods (e.g., strided conv).

5. **Unclear role of model variants** (Reviewer 3, confidence 4): Hi-Mamba-T and Hi-Mamba-S do not show significant PSNR/SSIM differences aside from parameters and FLOPs; their distinct advantages are not clearly stated. As variants increase in depth/width, optimization performance may not be evident.

6. **Minor presentation issues** (Reviewers 1, 2):
   - Reviewer 1 (confidence 5): Most citations in parentheses are not properly formatted, impacting readability.
   - Reviewer 2 (confidence 5): Figure 6(c) appears to be misplaced.
   - Reviewer 2 also asked for visual comparison results beyond the Urban100 benchmark.
