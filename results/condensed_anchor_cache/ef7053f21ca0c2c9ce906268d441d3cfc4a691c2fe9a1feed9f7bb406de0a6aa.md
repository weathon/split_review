## Merged Review

### Summary
Spatial-Mamba proposes a structure-aware state fusion (SASF) mechanism that uses dilated convolutions after the Mamba scan to directly establish neighborhood connectivity in the state space. The method is evaluated on image classification (ImageNet), detection, and segmentation, and shows gains over existing SSM-based models with a single scan.

### Strengths
- The motivation is sound: existing SSMs (e.g., ViM, VMamba) struggle with 2D spatial structure; adding direct spatial context via dilated convolutions is a complementary and intriguing idea.
- Experimental results are strong – ImageNet accuracy improves while speed remains competitive because convolutions are well optimized.
- The paper is clearly written, well‑organized, and explains related work well.
- Ablation studies are clear and convincing; the fusion operation yields a decent performance gain.
- The analysis linking Spatial‑Mamba, original Mamba, and linear attention under a matrix multiplication framework is a useful theoretical contribution.

### Weaknesses
- **Novelty concerns (Reviewers 3, 2):** The fusion essentially re‑introduces convolution on the state‑space feature maps; components like LPU, overlapped stem, re‑parameterization, and MESA are adopted from previous works. The method is a straightforward combination of Mamba + depthwise conv without an in‑depth redesign of Mamba’s weakness. Reviewer 2 notes the method does not clearly improve over simply adding more scanning directions (e.g., bi‑directional Vim or cross‑scan VMamba).
- **Missing results and comparisons:** Results for Cascade Mask R‑CNN are missing (Reviewer 1). Reviewer 2 recommends validating the approach on both Vim and VMamba baselines to see if the improvement is consistent.
- **Ambiguous notation and missing definitions:**
  - In Eq. (2), the parameter `alpha_k` is used but not defined until line 212 (only as “weights”) – the actual weighting calculation is unclear (Reviewer 1).
  - In Eq. (2), the same symbol `x_t` is used for both the scanning output and the fused input; Reviewer 3 suggests using `x_t'` to clarify the fusion step.
  - Line 204 mentions “temporal and spatial information” – it is unclear what “temporal” refers to; likely a typo (Reviewer 1).
- **Missing details on state space modeling:**
  - Number of hidden states per SSM layer is not reported, and there is no ablation on different state counts (Reviewer 4).
  - The paper does not clarify whether Spatial‑Mamba is implemented with Mamba’s original hardware‑scanning algorithm or Mamba2’s SSD framework (Reviewer 4).
  - Reviewers 4 and 3 ask for a clearer explanation of how state variables are modeled as “2D local observation points” to justify the structure‑aware fusion; currently the hidden states are not naturally spatial.
- **Limited exploration of fusion operators:** The choice of depthwise convolution has a fixed inductive bias; Reviewer 2 would like to see alternative spatial fusion methods experimented with.

*Contrast between reviewers:* Reviewers 1 and 2 (scores 8) are overall very positive, praising the idea, clarity, and results, while Reviewers 3 and 4 (scores 6) raise stronger concerns about novelty and missing details. All reviewers agree the method is simple and effective, but the latter two see limited technical contribution.
