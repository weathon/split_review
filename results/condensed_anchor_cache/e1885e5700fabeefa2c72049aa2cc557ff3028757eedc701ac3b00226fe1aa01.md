## Merged Review

### Summary
This paper proposes UAICRL for inverse constrained RL, addressing aleatoric uncertainty (stochastic dynamics) via distributional Bellman updates and risk-sensitive constraints, and epistemic uncertainty (limited knowledge of OOD samples) via mutual information quantification and flow-based generative data augmentation (FTG). Experiments in continuous (MuJoCo, highway) and discrete (gridworld) environments with stochastic dynamics compare against several ICRL baselines. Ablation studies evaluate the risk-sensitive constraint and data augmentation components.

### Strengths
- Addresses **both aleatoric and epistemic uncertainty** in ICRL, while previous methods primarily focus on epistemic uncertainty (Reviewers 1,2,4).
- **Distributional critic for aleatoric uncertainty** is a good idea, makes intuitive sense, and its effectiveness is validated by ablation studies (Reviewers 1,2,4).
- Works in **both continuous and discrete spaces** and supports **stochastic training environments**, unlike many prior works (Reviewer 1).
- Paper is **well organized and easy to follow** (Reviewer 2; Reviewer 4 generally happy up to experiments section).
- Provides **intuition for the math with real-world examples** (Reviewer 4).
- **Extensive experimental validation** with a wide range of baselines and tasks (Reviewer 3).
- Topic is **noteworthy**, with promising potential for real-world applications (Reviewer 3).

### Weaknesses
- **Presentation and clarity of Section 6 (Empirical Evaluation) are poor**: main points unclear, figures not explained step-by-step; the paper should state the main questions at the start of Section 6 (Reviewer 4; major issue, Reviewer 4 gave Presentation score 1 while others gave 3).
- **Figures are illegible** due to small size and font; this must be fixed before publication (Reviewer 4, echoed by Reviewer 4 on many specific figures: Fig 1,2,3,4,5,6,7, Table 1, D.3/D.4/D.5/D.6; all need larger text, better legends, clearer captions).
- **Conclusion is very short and feels rushed** (Reviewer 4).
- **Effectiveness of data augmentation (FTG) is questionable**:
    - In theory, the objective (Eq.8) includes OOD trajectories $\bar{\tau}$; how are they generated? (Reviewer 2)
    - In practice, Fig.3 shows UAICRL performs similarly to UAICRL-NDA (w/o data augmentation) (Reviewer 2).
    - Fig.7 (top MEICRL, bottom UAICRL) is unclear; need explanation of generated trajectories and which parts are OOD (Reviewer 2).
    - Reviewer 1: the flow function could still generate OOD data; ablation of mutual information term (Eq.7) yields a configuration where only risk-sensitive constraint (Eq.4) is used – this setup is not discussed.
    - Question: can FTG be replaced by other conditional generative models (e.g., diffusion)? How sensitive is performance to FTG hyperparameters? (Reviewer 1)
- **Missing experimental details**:
    - Target cost limits $\epsilon$ for experiments in Table 2, Fig.3&5 (Reviewer 2).
    - Why are some experiments early stopped when they have not converged? (e.g., Fig.3&4) (Reviewer 2).
    - The divergence trend of UAICRL in Blocked Walker task (Section 6.2): explain reasons and provide results with extended epochs? Were curves smoothed? (Reviewer 3).
    - What factors make Blocked Swimmer task challenging? Most methods fail to learn low-cost-violation policy (Reviewer 3).
    - GACL performs well in Block Ant but not in other tasks (Figs D.3,D.4); explain discrepancy (Reviewer 3).
    - Effect of noise scale: some baselines have higher violation rates with smaller noise (e.g., D3 vs D4 in Fig.3), which is counterintuitive (Reviewer 2).
    - Generalizability to multi-cost settings (Reviewer 3).
- **Theoretical support is insufficient** as acknowledged by authors (Reviewer 3).
- **Minor presentation/structure issues**:
    - Baseline name error: in Fig.4, should be "GACL" not "GAIL" (Reviewer 2).
    - Many textual citations in parentheses (use \citet instead) (Reviewer 4).
    - In Section 2, $\mathcal{M}^{c_{\omega}}$ is not defined (Reviewer 4).
    - Fig.1: text too small, unclear where to start reading; caption should mention starting from $\mathcal{D}_{e}$ (Reviewer 4).
    - Page 3 footnote missing full stop (Reviewer 4).
    - Section 4.2: $\mathcal{T}$ never formally introduced; $F(\cdot)$ overloaded (state flow vs trajectory flow) (Reviewer 4).
    - Algorithm 1: clarify "sample nominal trajectories" (interacting with env); clarify if augmented data is discarded each iteration (Reviewer 4).
    - Table 1 text too small (Reviewer 4).
    - Figures 3/4/5/D.3/D.4/D.5/D.6: need one legend, x-axis ticks too close, plot titles don't all need "with noise $\mathcal{N}(0,0.1)$" (Reviewer 4).
    - Figure 6: not clear; each column = grid world scenario, should be on figure or caption; constraints locations, agent start/end missing; text too small (Reviewer 4).
    - Figure 7: each column = grid world scenario; top row = ICRL? bottom = UAICLR? needs clarity (Reviewer 4).
    - Bolding in tables: what does it indicate? Statistical significance? Clarify (Reviewer 4).
    - Code supplement has no README or notebook (Reviewer 4).
- **Reviewer disagreement**: Reviewer 1 is slightly more critical (score 5 vs 6) – specifically questions the ablation of mutual info and the risk of OOD from flow function; also concerned about FTG hyperparameters. Reviewer 4 is much more critical on presentation (score 1 vs 3) and states the paper cannot be published without improvements in experiments, figures, and conclusion. (These contrasts are preserved above.)
