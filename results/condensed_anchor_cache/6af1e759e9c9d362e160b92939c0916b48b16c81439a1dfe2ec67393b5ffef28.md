## Merged Review

### Summary
NovelQA is a benchmark for evaluating LLMs on long-context QA using English novels averaging over 200k tokens. It features human-annotated questions across three complexity levels (multi-hop, single-hop, detail) and seven aspects, with evidence provided. Five large-context LLMs (three commercial, two open-source) are evaluated. Results show performance degradation after 100k tokens, difficulty with multi-hop reasoning, detail-oriented questions, and low evidence recall.

### Strengths
- Well-written and clear (R1).
- Longest context benchmark to date (R1). Experiments are well-designed (R1).
- Unique features: extremely long average length, human annotation efforts (R2).
- Interesting findings (performance degradation after 100k tokens, low evidence recall) that may enlighten future research (R2).
- Well-organized and easy to follow, with comprehensive appendix on data details (R2, R3).
- Huge annotator work by experts (all hold/pursue degrees in English Literature); questions, golden answers, and evidence are carefully crafted (R3).
- The contribution is clear and high (R3). Valuable resource for evaluation (R4).
- Addresses an important challenge in long-context LLMs: emphasizing extended context comprehension within complex narratives (R5).
- Carefully constructed with expert annotations, comprehensive documentation on question types and evaluation metrics (R5).
- Evaluates narrative comprehension, multi-step reasoning, and retrieval-based tasks, marking a substantial contribution (R5).
- One reviewer (rating 8) was more positive, praising the substantial contribution and careful construction, while other reviewers (ratings 6) noted limitations.

### Weaknesses
- **Genre limitation**: Only uses novels; other long-context genres (e.g., nonfiction books) should be included for comprehensive evaluation (R1, R2).
- **Question design**: Templates focus mainly on information extraction; more complex reasoning questions are needed (R1). Most questions are single-hop/detail; more discussion on multi-hop questions and relative evidence distances is required (R2).
- **Evidence annotation and position**: How evidence was annotated is not clearly described (R1 question). The analysis based on evidence position may be invalid because evidence may not be unique in the long context (R2). Distribution of evidence positions should be shown (R2 question).
- **How benchmark assesses multi-chapter spanning information** is unclear (R1 question).
- **Missing sections**: No ethical consideration or Limitations section. Limitations should include: data leakage (novels, especially public domain, may appear in model training data like GPT-4), annotator biases, truncation effects, and use of templates based on hardest cases for GPT-4/Claude2.1 (R3).
- **Efficiency problems** with running the benchmark for long-context models (R3). Consider using VLLM to reduce costs (R3 suggestion).
- **Bias from GPT-4 in distractors and evaluation**: Multiple-choice distractors generated by GPT-4 and then evaluated on the same API models likely gives GPT-4 an advantage; authors cannot claim GPT-4 performs better (R3). GPT-4 is also used as an evaluator with limited details on its evaluation process, and it may score its own answers higher (R5). Human evaluators' backgrounds are not provided (R5).
- **Evaluation methods insufficiently justified**: Automated scoring with GPT-4 is concerning for complex reasoning; two human evaluators mentioned but expertise unclear (R5).
- **Lack of baselines**: No human baseline in Table 2 (R3). No extractive step baseline (e.g., tf-idf) to highlight benchmark difficulty (R4). Some questions (e.g., counting) may test reasoning weakness, not long-context processing (R4).
- **Missing experiments**: No results for Llama 3.1 (R4 question), other open-source models (Mistral, Llama), or smaller closed-source models (GPT-4o-mini) (R5 question). Performance of these models would be insightful.
- **Dataset accessibility**: Unclear whether questions and sample sentences are publicly available for verification (R5 question).
- **Presentation issues**: Table 7 and Figure 5 misplaced above Appendix; missing table numbers; inconsistent spelling of "multichoice"/"multi-choice"; best scores not bolded in tables (R3).
