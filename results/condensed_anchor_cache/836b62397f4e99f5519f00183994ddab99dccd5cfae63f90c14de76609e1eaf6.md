## Merged Review

### Summary
The paper proposes *Annealing Flow* (AF), a continuous normalizing flow (CNF) sampler for high-dimensional, multi-modal unnormalized distributions. It defines a sequence of annealed distributions between a base and target, then trains CNFs sequentially to transport samples between neighbouring pairs using a dynamic optimal transport loss with KL relaxation. An extension, *Importance Flow* (IF), learns the importance ratio via a density estimator. The method does not require target samples. Experiments on synthetic benchmarks (up to 50 dimensions) compare against MCMC, particle, and some NN-based samplers. Reviewer opinions range from very positive (score 6) to strongly critical (score 1). Core concerns are limited novelty, missing comparisons with directly related annealing-flow and diffusion samplers, theoretical/notation errors, and questionable training procedure without importance correction.

### Strengths
- Addresses mode exploration in high-dimensional multi-modal distributions, demonstrated in experiments.
- Sampling cost after training is low, scaling linearly with sample size and dimension, and without needing MCMC chains.
- The Importance Flow extension is considered original by one reviewer.
- Several reviewers found the idea straightforward and the numerical results convincing (Reviewers 1, 4), with favourable comparisons against several baselines.
- The method does not rely on target samples for training.

### Weaknesses
- **Limited novelty**: The combination of annealing with CNFs and an optimal transport loss is seen as a trivial combination of existing techniques (Reviewers 2, 3, 5). The approach is very close to Annealed Flow Transport (Arbel et al. 2021) and Liouville Flow Importance Sampler (Tian et al. 2024), and these are not directly compared.
- **Missing comparisons**: Crucial related works and baselines are omitted:
  - Flow/annealing-based samplers: AFT, CRAFT, FAB (Reviewer 2). Also Liouville Flow Importance Sampler [1] and Path-Guided Particle-based Sampling [2] (Reviewer 5).
  - Score/diffusion-based samplers: DDS, PIS, PDIS, iDEM (Reviewers 2, 5). Also Denoising Diffusion Samplers, Path Integral Sampler, Improved Sampling via Learned Diffusions (Reviewer 5).
  - NN-assisted MCMC samplers like Neural IS, Flow MC (Reviewer 3).
  - Stochastic normalizing flows (Reviewer 4: Wu et al. 2020, Hagemann et al. 2022).
  - Score-based samplers without NNs: RDMC, SLIPS (Reviewer 3, minor).
- **Training procedure concerns**:
  - Training K separate CNFs sequentially is computationally expensive, and error accumulates due to bootstrap-style training (Reviewer 2, 3, 5).
  - The method lacks importance sampling in the loss; samples from the previous flow are used as approximate samples from the annealed distribution, which can be miscalibrated and miss mode weight changes (Reviewer 5).
  - No clear strategy for choosing hyperparameters K (number of intermediate distributions) and S (discretization steps), nor analysis of trade-offs (Reviewers 1, 2, 3).
- **Theoretical and notation issues**: 
  - KL divergence is written between unnormalized densities, which is not defined (Reviewers 4, 5).
  - Proof of Proposition 1 contains multiple errors: first equality wrong, expected value should be over pushforward of tilde f_{k-1}, and derivation from line 774 to 776 is incorrect (Reviewers 4, 5).
  - Misuse of notation (switching between f_k and ρ_k, calling integral value T, missing x(t) in expectations) (Reviewer 4).
  - Proposition 2 is folklore; Proposition 3 proof is insufficient (Reviewer 4).
  - The claim that Importance Flow can achieve zero variance is only true if the density ratio is predicted exactly (Reviewer 3).
- **Limited experimental evaluation**:
  - Only tested on synthetic problems; no real-world applications (Reviewers 1, 2, 4).
  - Experiments rely heavily on symmetric target densities; effect of asymmetry/shifting not tested (Reviewers 2, 4, 5). One reviewer suggests shifting Figure 6 target would likely cause mode missing.
  - HMC baseline comparison may be unfair; starting chains from the same latent distribution as AF could perform better (Reviewer 4).
  - Missing metrics focused on multimodal distributions (e.g., Blessing et al. 2024) and per-experiment mean/std (Reviewer 5).
  - 1D toy experiment (Figure 2) uses unbalanced modes? PT result imbalance unexplained (Reviewers 2, 3).
  - Table 3 ground truth values not given (Reviewer 3).
- **Other issues**:
  - GitHub link reveals authors' identity (Reviewer 2).
  - No related works section, poor discussion of limitations (Reviewers 3, 5).
  - Scalability to dimensions >>50 and distribution-free problems not addressed (Reviewer 1).
  - The method requires keeping all K intermediate velocity fields for sampling unless a single network is used; unclear if a single network suffices (Reviewer 5).
