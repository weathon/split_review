## Merged Review

### Summary
This paper systematically investigates key design choices for diffusion planning in offline reinforcement learning: guided sampling, network architecture, action generation, and planning strategy. Through experiments on over 6,000 diffusion models (Maze2D, AntMaze, Franka kitchen, MuJoCo locomotion), it identifies that unconditional sampling with selection can outperform guided sampling and Transformer outperforms U-Net. Based on these insights, the authors propose a simple baseline (Diffusion Veteran, DV) that achieves state-of-the-art results on standard offline RL benchmarks.

### Strengths
- **Very comprehensive empirical study** with over 6,000 models, controlled variable methods, and rich data support (Reviewers 1, 2, 3).
- **Well-organized and easy to follow** (Reviewer 2).
- **Innovative insights** that contrast with common practices in diffusion planning: unconditional sampling outperforms guided sampling, Transformer outperforms U-Net (Reviewer 3).
- **Simple yet effective baseline model (DV)** achieving state-of-the-art results on D4RL benchmarks (Reviewer 3).
- **Wide applicability** across tasks (maze navigation, robot manipulation) (Reviewer 3).
- **Engineering value:** Provides practical insights on effective design choices in a unified framework, which had not been sufficiently explored (Reviewer 4).
- **Each conclusion is accompanied by decent explanations** (Reviewer 2).

### Weaknesses
- **Limited generalizability of findings:** The investigation uses only a few tasks (Maze2D, AntMaze, Franka kitchen; MuJoCo locomotion added in section 4.6). No separate validation set is used to verify insights on different tasks, leaving uncertainty whether findings are overfitted to the examined tasks. Reviewer 4 notes this is fewer than typical ICLR studies (e.g., 30 tasks in Ball et al. 2023). Reviewer 2 also questions generalizability beyond D4RL and suggests more diverse datasets would strengthen claims.
- **Lack of theoretical innovation:** The paper is purely empirical; readers would still like original theoretical contributions to better apply diffusion models to decision-making tasks (Reviewer 1).
- **Insufficient analysis of long-term dependencies and attention patterns:** The paper lacks detailed analysis of how the Transformer handles long-term dependencies across tasks. Attention weight visualizations are missing to show which parts of the trajectory the model focuses on and how patterns vary across environments (Reviewer 3).
- **Missing explanation for key finding:** The paper does not explain why unconditional sampling outperforms guided sampling, which contrasts with typical image generation results (Reviewer 3’s question).
- **No extension to vision-based or goal-conditioned RL tasks**, which would further validate findings (Reviewer 3’s question).
- **Potential typo in Equation 2.1:** The right-hand side contains an incorrect S(t-1) notation (Reviewer 3).
- **Minor technical issues:** Unclear breakdown of the 6,000 diffusion models (grid search vs. manual tuning definition in Sec 3.2) (Reviewer 4). Figure 5 missing confidence interval for Kitchen-M; unclear what confidence intervals represent (500 episode seeds?) (Reviewer 4). Typos: line 101 missing closing parenthesis, line 158 extra parenthesis, line 479 missing space before “(Sect. 4.6)” (Reviewer 4).
