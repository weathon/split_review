## Merged Review

### Summary
This paper proposes denoising diffusion variational inference, which uses diffusion models to fit complex posterior distributions in latent variable models. The method augments a variational posterior with auxiliary latent variables via a user-specified noising process and optimizes a lower bound inspired by the wake-sleep algorithm. The instantiation DiffVAE is evaluated on unsupervised and semi-supervised learning, as well as clustering/visualization, with a specific application to inferring latent ancestry from the 1000 Genomes dataset. Results show improvements in negative log-likelihood and clustering quality over adversarial and flow-based baselines.

### Strengths
- Novel variational lower bound motivated by the wake-sleep algorithm, enabling a prescribed forward process for the latent sequence (all reviewers).
- Using a denoising diffusion model as the encoder provides high expressivity for modeling complex posteriors, without requiring adversarial training or constrained architectures like normalizing flows (R2).
- The noising process is flexible (not limited to Gaussian diffusion) and can incorporate prior structural knowledge (R2).
- The adaptation of the wake-sleep algorithm to avoid sampling from the generative model is interesting and helps avoid adversarial training (R1, R2).
- Paper is clearly written on background and alternative perspectives (R1). The analysis and experiments in dimensionality reduction are interesting and meaningful (R4).

### Weaknesses
- **Limited novelty relative to existing hierarchical VAEs**. The core idea is closely related to auxiliary variable models / hierarchical variational models (HVMs) and ladder VAEs (Sønderby et al., 2016; Vahdat et al., 2020, 2021). The main novelty is the regularization term in Eq. (3), but the rest is a standard hierarchical VAE with a Markov chain of Gaussian conditionals (R1, R3). One reviewer (score 3) found the motivation for introducing auxiliary variables via a diffusion process unconvincing (R4).
- **Training objective justification and analysis**. The replacement of the standard ELBO with \(\hat{\mathcal{L}}\) (Eq. 7) using a sleep-loss regularization fundamentally alters the optimization landscape; the gradient of \(q_\phi(z|x)\) is biased w.r.t. the original ELBO. The trade-off between computational savings and bound tightness is not analyzed (R1, R3). No rigorous convergence or error analysis is provided for the proposed bound (R4).
- **Weak baselines and limited experimental scope**. Comparisons are against classical VAE methods (e.g., IAF-VAE from 2016) without including more advanced hierarchical VAEs (e.g., Sønderby et al., 2016; Vahdat et al., 2020) or two-stage VAEs (Dai & Wipf, 2019) (R1, R3, R4). The experiments are restricted to two settings (semi-supervised learning and clustering) on relatively simple datasets (MNIST, CIFAR-10), which may not fully demonstrate the benefits of increased posterior expressivity (R2, R3). The clustering/visualization experiments on the 1000 Genomes dataset do not clearly show improved visual quality or diversity over AAE (Fig. 3) (R4). The paper would be strengthened by including an HVM baseline without the sleep-loss term to isolate its effect (R1).
- **Computational cost unaddressed**. The diffusion step requires 20–100 forward passes of the encoder network; no wall-clock training/inference time comparison or computational overhead analysis is provided against baselines (R2, R3, R4). The method introduces a critical hyperparameter (number of diffusion steps) with no sensitivity analysis or discussion of selection guidelines (R2).
- **Clarity and presentation issues**. Section 3 is dense and could be better structured; Sections 3.2 and 3.3 add limited value (R3). Notation is inconsistent (conditioning variables dropped, \(\bar{H}\) vs \(H\) confusion) and Figure 1 contains an error: \(q_\phi(y|z,x)\) should be \(q_\phi(z|y,x)\) (R3). The relationship between Sections 3.1 and 3.2 is unclear (R4). Typographical errors: missing parentheses in \(D_{\mathrm{KL}}(p_\theta(z|x)||q_\phi(z|x))\), missing subscripts in Eq. (4) (\(p(x,z)\) should be \(p_\theta(x,z)\)), and square instead of Swissroll prior in Fig. 2 (R1). No code is provided for reproducibility (R2). The motivation in Section 3.1.1 that the standard ELBO provides too weak a learning signal for hierarchical VAEs is not clearly explained or empirically demonstrated (R1).
- **Missing comparisons with related diffusion-based methods**. The paper does not sufficiently differentiate DiffVAE from LSGM (Vahdat et al., 2021) or variational diffusion models, nor clarify whether LSGM can also be used for semi-supervised learning (R1, R4).
- **Evaluation focus on latent NLL rather than generative NLL**. Because the sleep loss pushes the encoder toward the prior, it is not surprising that latent-space metrics improve; generative NLL should also be compared (R1).
