# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes Conditional advaNtage estimatiON (CANON), which amplifies the impact of target metrics without assuming their directionality. Specifically, CANON reclassifies the sampled responses into two groups based on the value of a specific metric, and then calculates the inter-group advantage to identify the metric trend that contributes to better performance, and calculates the intra-group advantage to select better responses within the same group. Extensive experiments show that the CANON based on entropy consistently outperforms prior methods across three LLMs on both math reasoning and high-complexity logic tasks. Moreover, when applied to response length, CANON further improves token efficiency, yielding a more favorable Pareto frontier in the performance–cost trade-off.

## Strengths
1. This paper is well-written and easy to follow.
2. The proposed CANON is well-motivated and reasonable.
3. Extensive experiments on various benchmarks demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed CANON.

## Weaknesses
1. The authors claim that the proposed CANON can amplify the impact of target metrics without assuming their directionality. However, in the experiments, the authors only conduct experiments with entropy and length. Can the authors provide more experiments with more metrics to verify the effectiveness of CANON?
2. The authors claim that the proposed CANON can be applied to any metric. However, it seems that the metric needs to satisfy certain conditions (e.g., numerical comparisons). Can the authors provide more discussion on the conditions that the metric needs to meet?
3. The authors claim that CANON can improve the token efficiency. Can the authors provide more discussion on the reasons for the improvement of token efficiency?
