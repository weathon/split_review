# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes a novel RL method for text-to-image generation, which only leverages the internal signals as rewards. It finds that maximizing self-uncertainty can improve image generation quality and contrary to the previous method in NLP. It conducts extensive experiments to demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method.

## Strengths
1. This paper is well-written and easy to follow.
2. The proposed method is simple and effective, which only leverages the intrinsic reward in the training of T2I model.
3. The experiments are extensive and the results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method.

## Weaknesses
1. As the proposed method leverages the intrinsic reward, which is a scalar value, it is not as comprehensive as the external reward model, which can evaluate the generated image from multiple aspects. Will this method be limited in some specific tasks?
2. As shown in Table 1, the proposed method performs better in some tasks but is worse in others. And the improvement is not significant. It would be better to analyze the potential reasons for these results.
