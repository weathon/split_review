# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes Purrception, a variational flow matching approach for vector-quantized image generation. Purrception learns categorical posteriors over codebook indices while computing velocity fields in the continuous embedding space. The authors evaluate Purrception on ImageNet-1k 256 × 256 generation and show that it converges faster than both continuous flow matching and discrete flow matching baselines while achieving competitive FID scores with state-of-the-art models.

## Strengths
1. The paper is well-written and easy to follow. The authors provide a clear motivation for their approach and a thorough explanation of the methodology.
2. The proposed Purrception approach is novel and addresses an important problem in generative modeling, namely the trade-off between continuous transport and discrete supervision.
3. The authors provide a comprehensive evaluation of Purrception on ImageNet-1k 256 × 256 generation, comparing it with both continuous flow matching and discrete flow matching baselines. The results demonstrate that Purrception achieves competitive FID scores with state-of-the-art models while converging faster.

## Weaknesses
1. The authors only evaluate Purrception on ImageNet-1k 256 × 256 generation. It would be beneficial to see how the approach performs on other datasets and resolutions.
2. While the authors mention that Purrception provides explicit categorical supervision, it would be helpful to see a more in-depth analysis of how this supervision affects the generated images.
3. The authors mention that Purrception can express uncertainty over plausible codes, but again, an in-depth analysis of this aspect would be beneficial. For example, can the authors show some samples where the model expresses high uncertainty and low uncertainty, and how this affects the quality of the generated images?

## Questions
1. Can the authors provide more details on how they chose the hyperparameters for Purrception?
2. How does Purrception perform on other datasets and resolutions?
3. Can the authors provide a more in-depth analysis of how the explicit categorical supervision affects the generated images?
4. How does Purrception compare to other state-of-the-art generative models in terms of sample diversity and quality?
5. Can the authors provide an in-depth analysis of how the model expresses uncertainty and how this affects the quality of the generated images?
