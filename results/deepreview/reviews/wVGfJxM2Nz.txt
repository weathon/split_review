# Review

## Summary
The paper proposes to use geometry-informed inductive biases to learn dynamics of dissipative and conservative systems. For the dissipative case, a linear state-space formulation is used with the additional constraint that the state operator is symmetric positive definite. This problem is solved via Riemannian optimization. For the conservative case, a symplectic Hamiltonian neural network is used. The paper demonstrates how structurally-naive models suffer from energy drift when referenced against the true energy surface leading to fragile roll-out generalization, unlike the proposed method which conserves phase-space volume along the correct energy level.
