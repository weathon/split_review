# Review

## Summary
This paper introduces a new method for quantum data loading, namely AQER. The method is based on the observation that a quantum state with lower entanglement can be better approximated by a product state, which is easier to prepare. The authors first reformulate most approximate quantum loader methods into a unified framework and establish information-theoretic bounds on their approximation error. Then, they develop AQER, a scalable AQL method that constructs the loading circuit by systematically reducing entanglement in target states. The numerical experiments show that AQER outperforms existing methods.

## Strengths
1. The paper is well-written and easy to follow. The authors provide a clear explanation of the existing approximate quantum loader methods and reformulate them into a unified framework. 

2. The authors provide a theoretical guarantee for their proposed method.

## Weaknesses
1. The proposed method is not suitable for quantum data, as preparing an entangled state is much easier than preparing a disentangled state. For example, the ground state of a transverse-field Ising model is an entangled state and is easy to prepare using a quantum circuit. However, the proposed method requires many steps to reduce the entanglement of the ground state, which is difficult to achieve.

2. The proposed method is not efficient. The number of steps T increases exponentially with the number of qubits, which makes the proposed method impractical for large-scale systems.

3. The numerical experiments are not enough. The authors only provide the infidelity of different methods, which is insufficient to demonstrate the superiority of the proposed method. The authors should provide more numerical results, such as the reconstruction of classical data using the quantum state, the classification accuracy for quantum kernel methods, and the expectation value of observables.
