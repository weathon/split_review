## Strengths
The authors propose a novel method for estimating mutual information between discrete random variables. The method is scalable, efficient, and does not require ad-hoc procedures to work with discrete data. The authors demonstrate the effectiveness of their method through various experiments.

## Weaknesses
The paper has some weaknesses that should be addressed:

1. The paper is not well written. In particular, the introduction does not motivate the problem of estimating mutual information between discrete random variables. The method section is difficult to follow. There is a lot of notation that is not clearly defined. For example, the notation $p_T(X_t = x)$ in Section 2.1 is not clearly defined. The authors should provide a clear definition of the notation and explain how it relates to the rest of the section.

2. The paper lacks a clear description of the proposed method. The authors should provide a clear description of the method, including the input and output, and explain how it relates to the rest of the paper.

3. The paper lacks a detailed discussion of the experimental results. The authors should provide a detailed discussion of the experimental results, including a comparison with other methods and a discussion of the limitations of the proposed method.

## Questions
1. Could the authors provide a clear definition of the notation used in the paper?

2. Could the authors provide a clear description of the proposed method, including the input and output?

3. Could the authors provide a detailed discussion of the experimental results, including a comparison with other methods and a discussion of the limitations of the proposed method?
