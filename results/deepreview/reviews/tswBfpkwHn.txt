# Review

## Summary
This paper presents a theoretical analysis of the training dynamics and in-context learning (ICL) generalization of a one-layer Mamba model, focusing on its robustness to outliers. The authors compare the results with those of linear Transformers under the same setting.

## Strengths
This paper is the first to provide a theoretical analysis of the training dynamics of Mamba models and their ICL performance, including their robustness to outliers.

## Weaknesses
The paper is based on a one-layer Mamba structure and binary classification tasks, which may limit its applicability to more complex models and tasks. The paper may lack experimental validation on real-world datasets, which is crucial to demonstrate the practical effectiveness of the theoretical findings.

## Questions
1. The paper focuses on a one-layer Mamba structure, which is simpler than the practical Mamba models used in most cases. How do the findings generalize to multi-layer Mamba models?

2. The paper focuses on binary classification tasks, which may not capture the complexity of real-world language tasks. How do the findings generalize to more complex tasks, such as multi-class classification or generation tasks?

3. The paper does not provide experimental results on real-world datasets. How do the theoretical findings apply to practical scenarios?
