# Review

## Summary
This paper introduces VisualPRM400K, a dataset containing 400K multimodal process supervision data, and develops VisualPRM, a multimodal Process Reward Model (PRM) that evaluates the value score of each step in reasoning. VisualPRM improves the reasoning performance of various MLLMs in Best-of-N evaluation. Additionally, the authors present VisualProcessBench, a benchmark designed to assess the ability of PRMs and MLLMs to detect incorrect steps in multimodal reasoning tasks.

## Strengths
1. The paper introduces VisualPRM400K, a dataset with 400K multimodal process supervision data, and develops VisualPRM, an advanced multimodal Process Reward Model (PRM) that estimates the value score of each step in the reasoning process.
2. The VisualPRM improves the reasoning performance of various MLLMs in Best-of-N evaluation.
3. The paper also presents VisualProcessBench, a benchmark designed to assess the ability of PRMs and MLLMs to detect incorrect steps in multimodal reasoning tasks.

## Weaknesses
1. The paper lacks a comparison with other MLLMs in the field. The author should compare the proposed model with other MLLMs in terms of performance and efficiency, such as LLaVA, QwenVL, and InternVL.
2. The author should provide more details about the data annotation process. The paper lacks a detailed description of the data annotation process, including the qualifications of the annotators, the criteria they used, and the inter-annotator agreement.
3. The author should provide more details about the evaluation process. The paper lacks a detailed description of the evaluation process, including the metrics used, the number of samples evaluated, and the criteria for selecting samples.
