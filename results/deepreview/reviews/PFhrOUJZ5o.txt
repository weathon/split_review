# Review

## Summary
The paper introduces LAION-Comp, a large-scale dataset with detailed structural annotations for compositional generation, addressing the core problem of unstructured training data. Models trained on LAION-Comp demonstrate improved fidelity and compositional accuracy on our CompSGen Bench and existing benchmarks, outperforming present methods. The paper also introduces a new benchmark CompSGen Bench for proper evaluation. The paper validates that large-scale, high-quality structural annotations are crucial for advancing controllable image synthesis and provides a foundational resource to the community for future research.

## Strengths
1. The paper introduces LAION-Comp, a new, large-scale dataset for compositional generation. It features high-quality structural annotations with multiple objects, attributes, and intricate relations, enhancing a model’s ability to generate complex and high-fidelity images.
2. The paper fine-tunes a new suite of foundation models based on diffusion and flow-matching backbones, demonstrating superior performance in complex scene generation.
3. The paper proposes a training-free, SG-based image editing framework, highlighting the powerful editing potential of structural annotations.
4. The paper establishes CompSGen Bench, a dedicated benchmark to evaluate complex scene generation. Through extensive experiments on this benchmark, the paper validates the significant advantages of the dataset and the effectiveness of the proposed models.

## Weaknesses
1. The paper does not provide a detailed analysis of the limitations of the proposed method or potential areas for improvement.
2. The paper does not provide a detailed analysis of the computational resources required to implement the proposed method.

## Questions
1. How can the proposed dataset and method be extended to other areas of image generation, such as style transfer or image editing?
2. What are the potential challenges in scaling the proposed method to larger datasets or more complex scenes?
3. How can the proposed method be integrated with other techniques, such as reinforcement learning or unsupervised learning, to further improve performance?
