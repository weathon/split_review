# Review

## Summary
This paper introduces AtlasKV, a method for integrating large knowledge graphs into LLMs with minimal GPU memory usage. AtlasKV employs KG2KV to convert KG triples into Q-K-V format and HiKVP to prune key-value pairs hierarchically, reducing computational and memory costs. Experiments show that AtlasKV achieves superior knowledge grounding accuracy and generalization compared to existing methods like KBLaM and RAG, while maintaining efficiency across various KG sizes.

## Strengths
1. The paper is well-organized, with clear explanations of the AtlasKV method, including KG2KV and HiKVP. Figures and tables effectively illustrate the results.
2. The method addresses a significant challenge in integrating large KGs into LLMs by reducing GPU memory usage to less than 20GB, making it scalable for real-world applications.
3. AtlasKV demonstrates strong generalization performance on OOD tasks, outperforming baseline methods like KBLaM and RAG, particularly on complex datasets such as ATLAS-CC-QKV and ATLAS-Pes2o-QKV.

## Weaknesses
1. The method's effectiveness heavily relies on the quality of the KG triples. Noisy or inaccurate data could degrade performance and knowledge grounding accuracy.
2. While the paper claims minimal memory usage, the computational overhead introduced by HiKVP and the KG2KV conversion process is not thoroughly analyzed. A detailed comparison of computational costs with other methods would strengthen the paper.
3. The paper focuses on knowledge grounding tasks. Extending the evaluation to other NLP tasks that utilize KGs, such as knowledge graph question answering (KGQA), could provide a more comprehensive assessment of AtlasKV’s applicability.

## Questions
1. How does AtlasKV handle noisy or incomplete KG triples? Could the authors provide examples of how the method deals with such cases?
2. Can the authors provide more details on the computational overhead introduced by HiKVP and KG2KV? How does this compare to traditional RAG methods?
3. How does AtlasKV perform on other NLP tasks beyond knowledge grounding, such as knowledge graph question answering (KGQA)?
4. How sensitive is AtlasKV to the choice of hyperparameters in HiKVP, such as the number of clusters or pruning thresholds?
