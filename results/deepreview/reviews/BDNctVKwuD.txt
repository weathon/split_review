# Review

## Summary
This paper introduces ContrastiveCAMs, an improvement over HiResCAM that provides more robust and accurate attention maps by removing arbitrary shifts. Additionally, it proposes Core-Focused Cross-Entropy, an extension of the cross-entropy loss that encourages the model to focus on core image regions relevant to the class label, thereby improving feature alignment and reducing reliance on non-core, potentially misleading features.

## Weaknesses
1. The proposed method is complex, requiring multiple forward passes, which increases computational cost. In Table 2, the authors provide a comparison of training time, but it is limited to a single dataset, and the comparison is made against the vanilla CE method only. To better evaluate the computational cost, I suggest including a comparison of training times across all datasets and methods, particularly focusing on the vanilla CE and CFCE+KL methods.
2. The performance of the proposed method is not convincing. In Table 2, the authors report a significant drop in accuracy under Core-Region Ablation for CFCE and CFCE+KL methods compared to the vanilla CE method. Additionally, the CFCE method performs worse than the CORM method. To improve the persuasiveness of the results, I suggest that the authors include additional comparisons with other methods in Table 2, such as the CORM+DFR method, and consider adding a column for accuracy under full-image ablation to better evaluate the method's effectiveness.
3. The experiments are insufficient. The experiments in the paper are limited to ResNet-50, making it difficult to assess the generalizability of the method. I recommend including experiments on more model architectures, such as ViT, to better demonstrate the method's generalizability.
4. The visualization results are not convincing. The visualization results in Figure 3 do not clearly demonstrate the superiority of the proposed method. I suggest that the authors provide a more detailed explanation of the advantages of the results and include visualizations of other methods for a more comprehensive comparison.
