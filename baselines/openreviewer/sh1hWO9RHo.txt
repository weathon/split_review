# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes a framework for evaluating LLM-based agents. The authors define five metrics and use LLM as a judge to score the agents' performance. They also conduct experiments on two datasets to verify the effectiveness of the proposed framework.

## Soundness
2

## Presentation

## Contribution

## Strengths
1. The authors propose a framework for evaluating LLM-based agents, which is an important topic.
2. The authors conduct experiments on two datasets and provide some analysis.

## Weaknesses
1. The proposed framework is not comprehensive enough. For example, the authors do not consider the reliability of the agent, which is an important metric in the real-world application of LLM-based agents.
2. The authors do not provide the detailed process of using LLM as a judge, which is an important part of the proposed framework.
3. The authors do not compare the proposed framework with other evaluation frameworks, which is not convincing enough to show the effectiveness of the proposed framework.
4. The authors do not provide the detailed process of the experiments, which may affect the reproducibility of the proposed framework.

## Questions
1. How to use LLM as a judge?
2. What are the advantages of the proposed framework compared with other evaluation frameworks?
