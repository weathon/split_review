# Review

## Summary
The paper introduces Continuous Online Action Detection (COAD), a novel task formulation that enables models to not only detect actions in real time, but also train and adapt directly on continuous video streams. To support this task, the authors curated Ego-OAD, a large-scale benchmark derived from Ego4D. The proposed method introduces training strategies tailored for OAD from continuous video streams, and experiments on Ego-OAD validate the effectiveness of COAD.

## Strengths
1. The paper is well-written and easy to follow.
2. The paper introduces a new task formulation that enables models to adapt online from continuous egocentric video streams using single-pass training.
3. The paper curates a new large-scale benchmark for egocentric OAD based on the Ego4D dataset, providing a diverse and realistic evaluation platform for future research in this direction.
4. The proposed method for COAD improves adaptation to the user’s environment and boosts generalization to new scenarios.
