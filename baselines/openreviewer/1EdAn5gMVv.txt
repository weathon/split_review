# Review

## Summary
This paper presents a novel training framework called SpatialBoost, which enhances the spatial understanding capabilities of pre-trained vision encoders by leveraging language-guided reasoning. The core idea is to convert dense 3D spatial information from 2D images into linguistic expressions, which are then used to inject spatial knowledge into vision encoders through a Large Language Model (LLM). The method consists of three training stages: feature alignment, visual instruction tuning, and vision encoder fine-tuning with dual-channel attention. The authors evaluate SpatialBoost on a wide range of benchmarks requiring both 3D perception and general vision abilities, and show consistent performance improvements across various tasks.

## Strengths
1. The paper introduces a novel framework, SpatialBoost, which addresses the limitation of pre-trained image representation models in learning 3D spatial relationships. The idea of converting 3D spatial information into linguistic expressions and injecting it into vision encoders through LLM is innovative.
2. The paper provides a thorough evaluation of SpatialBoost on a diverse set of vision tasks, demonstrating its effectiveness in improving performance on tasks requiring 3D spatial knowledge. The results show consistent improvements across various benchmarks.
3. The paper is well-written and easy to follow. The authors provide a clear explanation of the proposed framework and the experimental setup. The figures and tables are well-presented and help to illustrate the results.

## Weaknesses
1. The paper lacks a thorough comparison with existing methods that address the problem of learning 3D spatial relationships. While the authors mention some related work in the introduction, they do not provide a detailed comparison with these methods in the experimental section. It would be helpful to see how SpatialBoost compares to other approaches in terms of performance and computational efficiency.
2. The paper does not provide a detailed analysis of the computational requirements of SpatialBoost. It would be helpful to see a comparison of the computational cost of SpatialBoost to other methods, including training time, inference time, and memory requirements. This information would be useful for practical applications.
3. The paper does not provide a detailed analysis of the limitations of SpatialBoost. It would be helpful to see a discussion of the potential limitations or failure cases of the proposed method. This would provide a more balanced view of the approach and highlight areas for future research.

## Questions
1. How does SpatialBoost compare to other methods that address the problem of learning 3D spatial relationships? A detailed comparison with existing approaches would help to better position SpatialBoost in the context of existing research.
2. What are the computational requirements of SpatialBoost? A comparison of the computational cost of SpatialBoost to other methods would be helpful for practical applications.
3. What are the potential limitations or failure cases of SpatialBoost? A discussion of the limitations of the proposed method would provide a more balanced view and highlight areas for future research.
