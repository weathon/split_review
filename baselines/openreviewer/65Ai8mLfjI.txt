# Review

## Summary
The paper proposes a new method for global text conditioning in diffusion transformers. The authors first show that the pooled text embedding, which is often used for global text conditioning, contributes little to the overall performance of the model. They then propose a new method that uses the pooled embedding as guidance, allowing for controllable shifts towards more desirable properties. The proposed approach is training-free, simple to implement, incurs negligible runtime overhead, and can be applied to various diffusion models. The authors demonstrate the effectiveness of their approach on diverse tasks such as text-to-image/video generation and image editing.

## Strengths
- The paper presents a novel method for global text conditioning in diffusion transformers. The approach of using the pooled text embedding as guidance is innovative and has the potential to improve the performance of diffusion models across various tasks.
- The proposed method is training-free, which means it can be easily integrated into existing diffusion models without the need for extensive training. This makes it more practical and widely applicable.
- The paper provides a thorough analysis of the role of the pooled text embedding in diffusion models, showing that it contributes little to the overall performance. This analysis supports the need for the proposed method.
- The authors demonstrate the effectiveness of their approach on a diverse set of tasks, including text-to-image/video generation and image editing. This shows the broad applicability of the proposed method.
- The paper is well-written and easy to follow. The authors provide clear explanations of the proposed method and the experimental setup, making it easy for readers to understand the contributions.

## Weaknesses
- The paper does not provide a detailed analysis of the limitations of the proposed method. It would be beneficial to discuss potential drawbacks or scenarios where the approach may not be as effective.
- While the paper demonstrates the effectiveness of the proposed method on a diverse set of tasks, it would be valuable to explore its performance on more complex tasks or datasets. This would provide a more comprehensive understanding of its capabilities and limitations.
- The paper could benefit from a more detailed comparison with existing methods for global text conditioning. This would help to highlight the novelty and effectiveness of the proposed approach.

## Questions
- How does the proposed method compare to other state-of-the-art methods for global text conditioning in diffusion transformers? A more detailed comparison would help to establish the novelty and effectiveness of the proposed approach.
- Can the proposed method be applied to other types of generative models, such as GANs or flow-based models? It would be interesting to explore the generalizability of the approach.
- How does the proposed method perform on more complex tasks or datasets? A more challenging evaluation would help to further demonstrate the capabilities and limitations of the approach.
- What are the limitations of the proposed method? It would be beneficial to discuss potential drawbacks or scenarios where the approach may not be as effective.
