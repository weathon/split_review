# Review

## Summary
The paper introduces Spherical Watermark, a novel and comprehensive framework for watermarking images generated by diffusion models. This approach embeds watermarks directly within the latent space of these models, ensuring both security and the preservation of visual quality. The method leverages a unique combination of binary embedding and spherical mapping to convert binary watermark data into Gaussian noise, which is then added to the latent space of the diffusion model. This integration is seamless, requiring no modifications to the pre-trained model, and is designed to be computationally efficient, eliminating the need for complex cryptographic operations. The paper demonstrates the effectiveness of Spherical Watermark through extensive experiments, showcasing its ability to maintain high visual fidelity while providing robust and reliable watermarking.
