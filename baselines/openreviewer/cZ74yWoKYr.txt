# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes a new method to improve the performance of KV cache eviction methods in LLMs. Unlike existing methods that evict KV caches based on attention weights, this paper proposes to use output perturbation as the metric to decide which KV caches to evict. The proposed method is a plug-and-play method that can be applied to existing KV cache eviction methods. Experiments show that the proposed method can significantly improve the performance of existing KV cache eviction methods.

## Strengths
1. The paper is well-written and easy to follow.
2. The proposed method is a plug-and-play method that can be applied to existing KV cache eviction methods.
3. Experiments show that the proposed method can significantly improve the performance of existing KV cache eviction methods.

## Weaknesses
1. The proposed method introduces a hyper-parameter $\alpha$, which may require additional effort to tune in practice.
2. The efficiency evaluation is only conducted on a 128k context length, which is relatively short compared to the context length supported by modern LLMs. Therefore, it is unclear how the proposed method would impact the efficiency of LLMs in practice.

## Questions
1. How does the proposed method perform when applied to KV cache eviction methods other than SnapKV, AdaKV, and HeadKV?
2. How does the proposed method impact the efficiency of LLMs on long contexts?
